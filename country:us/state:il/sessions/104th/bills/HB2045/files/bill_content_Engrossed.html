7    Sec. 6z-126. Law Enforcement Training Fund.  
8    (a) The Law Enforcement Training Fund is hereby created as 
9a special fund in the State treasury. Moneys in the Fund shall 
10consist of: (i) the share of the revenue from increasing the 
11insurance producer license fees allocated for transfer to the 
12Fund as provided under subsection (a-5) of Section 500-135 of 
13the Illinois Insurance Code; and (ii) the share of the moneys 
14collected from auto insurance policy fees under Section 8.6 of 
15the Illinois Vehicle Hijacking and Motor Vehicle Theft 
16Prevention and Insurance Verification Act allocated for 
17deposit into the Fund; and (iii) any moneys received for the 
18purposes of item (iv) of subsection (b) of this Section, 
19including, but not limited to, fee receipts, gifts, grants, 
20and awards from any public or private entity. Any interest 
21earned on moneys in the Fund must be deposited into the Fund. 
22    (b) This Fund shall be used by the Illinois Law 
23Enforcement Training Standards Board for the following
