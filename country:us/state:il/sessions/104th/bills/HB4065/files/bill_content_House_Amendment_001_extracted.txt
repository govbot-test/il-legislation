Preemption. 9    (a) Nothing in this Act shall be construed or applied to be 10less restrictive than federal law for a person regulated by 11this Act.12    (b) Nothing in this Act shall be construed or applied in a 13manner that would conflict with: 14        (1) applicable federal law; or 15        (2) other laws of this State if the State law is 16    compatible with applicable federal law.17    (c) Limited distribution of a drug required under 21 18U.S.C. 355-1 shall not be construed as a violation of this Act. 19    Section 97.
