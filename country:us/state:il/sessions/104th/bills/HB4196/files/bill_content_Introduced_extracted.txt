Anthony DeLuca and Steven Reick SYNOPSIS AS INTRODUCED: 60 ILCS 1/80-85 new65 ILCS 5/8-1-19 new70 ILCS 1205/4-10 new105 ILCS 5/10-31 new105 ILCS 5/34-240 new110 ILCS 805/3-85 new    Amends the Township Code, the Park District Code, the Illinois Municipal Code, the School Code, and the Public Community College Act. Provides that if a township, park district, municipality, school district, or community college has been issued a credit card, then, each month, the governing authority of the township, park district, municipality, school district, or community college shall vote to verify the validity of an itemized statement of all purchases made using the credit card in the preceding month. Provides that the itemized statement must include all purchases made using the credit card in the preceding month and may not be redacted. Provides that the vote shall solely be for the approval of the itemized statement of all purchases made using the credit card and shall not include any other expense approval.
