Full Text of HB4233 Illinois General Assembly ILGA.GOV HB4233 - 104th General Assembly 104TH GENERAL ASSEMBLY State of Illinois 2025 and 2026HB4233 Introduced 1/14/2026, by Rep. Amy Briel SYNOPSIS AS INTRODUCED: 110 ILCS 330/8k new210 ILCS 85/11.7b new    Amends the Hospital Licensing Act. Provides that a hospital that operates an emergency department shall not use health care kiosks in the hospital's emergency department to assess or determine a patient's medical state, to determine the priority level of a patient's injuries or illness, or to otherwise triage patients. Provides that health care kiosks may be used for data entry of a patient's personal identifying information and billing information in place of paper forms. Amends the University of Illinois Hospital Act to set forth conforming requirements for a University of Illinois Hospital emergency department.
