All remedies, penalties, and authority granted 7to the Attorney General or the State's Attorney by the 8Consumer Fraud and Deceptive Business Practices Act shall be 9available to the Attorney General or the State's Attorney for 10the enforcement of Section 10. 11    Section 20. Action for actual damages.
