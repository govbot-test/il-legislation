Full Text of HB4251 Illinois General Assembly ILGA.GOV HB4251 - 104th General Assembly 104TH GENERAL ASSEMBLY State of Illinois 2025 and 2026HB4251 Introduced 1/14/2026, by Rep. David Friess SYNOPSIS AS INTRODUCED: 110 ILCS 167/18 new    Amends the Public Higher Education Act. Provides that if a scholarship or tuition waiver funded by the federal government has been awarded by a public institution of higher education to a student and the federal government shuts down due to a lapse in appropriations, then (i) the scholarship or waiver shall remain in effect for the duration of the shutdown and (ii) the student is not required to pay the amount that was covered by the scholarship or waiver during the award period if the institution of higher education has reasonable confidence that a disbursement from the federal government to cover the cost of the scholarship or waiver will be made upon the reopening of the federal government.LRB104 16381 LNS 29770 b  A BILL FOR HB4251LRB104 16381 LNS 29770 b1    AN ACT concerning education. 2    Be it enacted by the People of the State of Illinois, 3represented in the General Assembly: 4    Section 5.
