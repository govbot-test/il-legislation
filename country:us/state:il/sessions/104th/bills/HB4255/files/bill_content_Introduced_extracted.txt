Full Text of HB4255 Illinois General Assembly ILGA.GOV HB4255 - 104th General Assembly 104TH GENERAL ASSEMBLY State of Illinois 2025 and 2026HB4255 Introduced 1/14/2026, by Rep. Kelly M. Cassidy SYNOPSIS AS INTRODUCED: 720 ILCS 5/48-11    Amends the Criminal Code of 2012. Provides that the statute prohibiting elephants from performing in traveling animal acts applies to "covered animals" rather than just elephants. Defines "covered animal" as any of the following animals, and hybrids of those animals: (1) elephantidae; (2) felidae, but excluding a domestic cat; (3) non-human primate; or (4) ursidae. Provides that the provisions of the amendatory Act are in addition to, and not in lieu of, any other laws protecting animal welfare. Provides that the provisions may not be construed to limit any State law or rules protecting the welfare of animals or to prevent a unit of local government from adopting and enforcing its own animal welfare ordinances and regulations. Contains a severability provision.
