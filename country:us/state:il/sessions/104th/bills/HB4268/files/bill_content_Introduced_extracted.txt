Full Text of HB4268 Illinois General Assembly ILGA.GOV HB4268 - 104th General Assembly 104TH GENERAL ASSEMBLY State of Illinois 2025 and 2026HB4268 Introduced 1/14/2026, by Rep. Daniel Didech SYNOPSIS AS INTRODUCED: 60 ILCS 1/85-75 new    Amends the Township Code. Provides that a township board of trustees or township official may not enter into, amend, or renew an employment, equipment, or service contract during the 14 days before a consolidated election through the third Monday in May following the consolidated election. Provides that a township board or township official may enter into, amend, or renew a contract that does not extend more than 60 days after the third Monday of May following the consolidated election to address an emergency.
