Full Text of HB4342 Illinois General Assembly ILGA.GOV HB4342 - 104th General Assembly 104TH GENERAL ASSEMBLY State of Illinois 2025 and 2026HB4342 Introduced 1/14/2026, by Rep. Dave Vella SYNOPSIS AS INTRODUCED: 20 ILCS 2505/2505-817 new    Amends the Department of Revenue Law of the Civil Administrative Code of Illinois. Provides that the Department of Revenue shall administer a program to provide assistance to qualified mobile home owners who have experienced rent increases associated with lots in mobile home parks. Provides that a qualified mobile home owner is an individual who (i) is 65 years of age or older during the calendar year for which the subsidy is awarded, (ii) has an ownership interest in a mobile home that is located in a mobile home park or a manufactured home that is located in a mobile home park, (iii) uses the mobile home or manufactured home as the individual's principal dwelling place during specified periods of time, and (iv) has a household income that does not exceed certain limitations.
