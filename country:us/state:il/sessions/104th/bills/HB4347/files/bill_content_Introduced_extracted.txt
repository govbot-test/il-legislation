Full Text of HB4347 Illinois General Assembly ILGA.GOV HB4347 - 104th General Assembly 104TH GENERAL ASSEMBLY State of Illinois 2025 and 2026HB4347 Introduced 1/14/2026, by Rep. David Friess SYNOPSIS AS INTRODUCED: 625 ILCS 5/3-609  from Ch. 95 1/2, par. 3-609    Amends the Illinois Vehicle Code. Provides that any veteran who holds proof of a service-connected disability from the United States Department of Veterans Affairs, and whose degree of disability has been declared to be 50% or more, but whose disability does not qualify the veteran for a plate or decal for persons with disabilities, without the payment of any registration fee, make application to the Secretary of State for any other special plate for veterans for which the veteran qualifies for the registration of one motor vehicle of the first division, one motorcycle, or one motor vehicle of the second division weighing not more than 8,000 pounds.
