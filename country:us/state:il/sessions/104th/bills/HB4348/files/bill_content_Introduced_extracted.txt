Full Text of HB4348 Illinois General Assembly ILGA.GOV HB4348 - 104th General Assembly 104TH GENERAL ASSEMBLY State of Illinois 2025 and 2026HB4348 Introduced 1/14/2026, by Rep. Dan Ugaste SYNOPSIS AS INTRODUCED: 605 ILCS 10/19  from Ch. 121, par. 100-19    Amends the Toll Highway Act. Provides that the Illinois State Toll Highway Authority shall not charge a toll for a vehicle of the first division pulling a trailer with no more than 2 axles at a rate higher than an amount calculated by multiplying the toll charged to passenger vehicles using an I-PASS device by the total number of axles on the trailer. Provides that the toll rate applies to both a driver who uses an I-PASS device and a driver who uses cash to pay tolls.
