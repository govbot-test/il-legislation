Daniel Didech SYNOPSIS AS INTRODUCED: 415 ILCS 15/3  from Ch. 85, par. 5953415 ILCS 15/6  from Ch. 85, par. 5956    Amends the Solid Waste Planning and Recycling Act. Requires each county waste management plan to include a recycling program to designed to recycle or compost (rather than just recycle) municipal waste at a specified recycling rate by a certain timeline. Defines the recycling rate as 25% until January 1, 2032, and 50% beginning January 1, 2032 (rather than 15% by the end of the third year of the program and 25% by the end of the fifth year of the program).
