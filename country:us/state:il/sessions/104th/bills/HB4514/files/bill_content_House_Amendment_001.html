7    Sec. 9-226.5. General rate increase requests; public 
8hearings. 
9    (a) For any general rate increase requested by a gas, 
10electric, water, or sewer public utility company under the 
11provisions of this Act, and upon request from any ratepayer 
12affected by the requested general rate increase or any group 
13with members affected by the requested general rate increase, 
14the Commission shall hold at least one in-person public 
15hearing during which members of the public can provide input 
16on the requested general rate increase.

4statements regarding the subject matter of the public hearing 
5with the person or persons conducting the public hearing. The 
6Commission shall give each member of the public a reasonable 
7amount of time to testify and be heard by the Commission at the 
8public hearing. Any person requesting to speak at the public 
9hearing shall be heard prior to the end of the public hearing.
