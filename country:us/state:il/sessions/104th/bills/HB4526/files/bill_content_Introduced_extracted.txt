Michael Crawford SYNOPSIS AS INTRODUCED:     Appropriates the sum of $2,100,000 from the General Revenue Fund to the Illinois Criminal Justice Information Authority for a grant to Safer Pathways to support the Supportive Reentry Network Collaborative. Effective July 1, 2026.LRB104 17799 BAB 31232 b HB4526LRB104 17799 BAB 31232 b1    AN ACT concerning appropriations.  2    Be it enacted by the People of the State of Illinois, 3represented in the General Assembly: 4    Section 5. The sum of $2,100,000, or so much thereof as may 5be necessary, is appropriated from the General Revenue Fund to 6the Illinois Criminal Justice Information Authority for a 7grant to Safer Pathways to support the Supportive Reentry 8Network Collaborative, an integrated network that unifies 9clinical care, behavioral health, housing, employment, 10identification restoration, legal benefit access, and care 11coordination into one accountable system that connects people 12who are 90 days pre-release through 12 months and beyond of 13post-release from incarceration to health care, behavioral 14health, stabilization supports, employment and housing using 15close-loop referrals, shared metrics, and a unified data 16system. 17    Section 99. Effective date. This Act takes effect July 1, 182026.
