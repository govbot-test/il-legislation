Full Text of HB4563 Illinois General Assembly ILGA.GOV HB4563 - 104th General Assembly 104TH GENERAL ASSEMBLY State of Illinois 2025 and 2026HB4563 Introduced 1/30/2026, by Rep. Nabeela Syed SYNOPSIS AS INTRODUCED: 35 ILCS 200/12-1035 ILCS 200/12-12 new    Amends the Property Tax Code. In provisions concerning the publication of assessments, provides that, if no newspaper is published in the county, then the publication shall be printed in a public newspaper of general circulation in the county. Provides that, in counties with less than 3,000,000 inhabitants, the county assessor may, in lieu of other publication methods provided by law, publish the list of assessments on the county website.
