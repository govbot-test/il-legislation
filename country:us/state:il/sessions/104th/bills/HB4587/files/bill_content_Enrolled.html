5    Sec. 6.5. Qualifications for interim conditional child

7    (a) A licensed day care center may employ an individual

9requirements set forth in 89 Ill. Adm. Code 407.130(e)(2) as 
10an interim conditional child care director for a period of 12

12or (2) of subsection (b). An individual employed under this 
13Section shall comply with the process established by the

18the requirements of 89 Ill. Adm. Code 407.130(e)(2).
