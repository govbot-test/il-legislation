This Section is a denial and 11limitation of home rule powers under subsection (h) of Section 126 of Article VII of the Illinois Constitution.13    (i) A violation of this Section shall be a petty offense 14and may be fined as follows:15        (1) for a first violation, a fine not to exceed $50;16        (2) for a second violation within a 12-month period, a 17    fine not to exceed $100;18        (3) for a third violation within a 12-month period, 19    and any additional violation within a 12-month period, a 20    fine not to exceed $500.21    No person shall be fined more than $5,000 in a calendar 22year for violations of this Section. 23    Section 99.
