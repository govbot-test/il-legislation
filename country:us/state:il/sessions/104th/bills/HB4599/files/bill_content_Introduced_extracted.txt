Full Text of HB4599 Illinois General Assembly ILGA.GOV HB4599 - 104th General Assembly 104TH GENERAL ASSEMBLY State of Illinois 2025 and 2026HB4599 Introduced 2/3/2026, by Rep. Maurice A. West, II SYNOPSIS AS INTRODUCED: 20 ILCS 505/60 new    Provides that the Act may be referred to as the Nautica Horton Act. Amends the Children and Family Services Act. Requires the Department of Children and Family Services to reimburse a deceased minor's parent or any other relative for the cost of any funeral and burial or cremation services charged to or paid by the parent or relative if the minor's death occurred while the minor was under the care of or receiving services from the Department and the parent or relative lacks the resources to assume such costs.LRB104 17684 KTG 31115 b  A BILL FOR HB4599LRB104 17684 KTG 31115 b1    AN ACT concerning State government. 2    Be it enacted by the People of the State of Illinois, 3represented in the General Assembly: 4    Section 1. Reference to Act. This Act may be referred to as 5the Nautica Horton Act. 6    Section 5. The Children and Family Services Act is amended 7by adding Section 60 as follows: 8    (20 ILCS 505/60 new)9    Sec. 60. Funeral and burial or cremation reimbursements. 10In the case of the death of a minor while under the care of or 11receiving services from the Department, if the cost of the 12deceased minor's funeral and burial or cremation services is 13charged to or paid by the minor's parent or any other relative 14who lacks the resources to assume such costs, then the 15Department shall reimburse that parent or relative as follows: 16        (1) the reasonable cost of the burial or cremation 17    services; and 18        (2) no less than $1,000 for the funeral, visitation, 19    reception, and any other similar services.
