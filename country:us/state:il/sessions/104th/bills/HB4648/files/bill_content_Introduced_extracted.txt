Lawrence "Larry" Walsh, Jr. SYNOPSIS AS INTRODUCED:     Appropriates the sum of $20,000,000 from the Capital Projects Fund to the Department of Commerce and Economic Opportunity for expenses associated with the restoration of the property formerly operated as the Joliet Correctional Center. Effective July 1, 2026.LRB104 17540 HLH 30968 b HB4648LRB104 17540 HLH 30968 b1    AN ACT concerning appropriations.  2    Be it enacted by the People of the State of Illinois, 3represented in the General Assembly: 4    Section 5. The sum of $20,000,000, or so much thereof as 5may be necessary, is appropriated from the Capital Projects 6Fund to the Department of Commerce and Economic Opportunity 7for expenses associated with the restoration of the property 8formerly operated as the Joliet Correctional Center, including 9but not limited to, site preparation, environmental 10assessments, demolition of existing structures, environmental 11remediation, site stabilization activities, site restoration 12activities, and other work directly related to the demolition 13process, as deemed necessary to support infrastructure renewal 14and development, including prior year costs. 15    Section 99. Effective date. This Act takes effect July 1, 162026.
