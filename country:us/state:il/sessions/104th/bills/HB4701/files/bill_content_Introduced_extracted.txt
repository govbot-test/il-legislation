Full Text of HB4701 Illinois General Assembly ILGA.GOV HB4701 - 104th General Assembly 104TH GENERAL ASSEMBLY State of Illinois 2025 and 2026HB4701 Introduced , by Rep. Tracy Katz Muhl SYNOPSIS AS INTRODUCED: New Act210 ILCS 88/12815 ILCS 505/2MMMM new    Creates the Limitations on Facility Fees Act. Provides that no health care provider shall charge, bill, or collect a facility fee, except for: (i) services provided on a hospital's campus; (ii) services provided at a facility that includes a licensed hospital emergency department; or (iii) emergency services provided at a freestanding emergency center. Provides that, except as specified, no health care provider shall charge, bill, or collect a facility fee for: (i) outpatient evaluation and management services; or (ii) any other outpatient, diagnostic, or imaging services identified by the Department of Public Health. Requires the Department to annually identify services subject to the limitations on specified facility fees that may reliably be provided safely and effectively in settings other than hospitals. Sets forth provisions concerning reporting, rulemaking, and enforcement of the Act. Amends the Fair Patient Billing Act to make a conforming change. Amends the Consumer Fraud and Deceptive Business Practices Act.
