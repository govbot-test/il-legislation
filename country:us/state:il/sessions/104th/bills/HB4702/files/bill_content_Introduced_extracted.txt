Full Text of HB4702 Illinois General Assembly ILGA.GOV HB4702 - 104th General Assembly 104TH GENERAL ASSEMBLY State of Illinois 2025 and 2026HB4702 Introduced , by Rep. Tracy Katz Muhl SYNOPSIS AS INTRODUCED: New Act    Creates the Diaper Labeling Act. Provides that, no later than 12 months after the effective date of the Act, each package or box containing diapers sold in the State shall contain a plain and conspicuous printed list of all ingredients in order of predominance that shall either be printed on the package or box or affixed to the package or box. Provides for a civil penalty of 1% of a violator's total annual in-State sales of diapers not to exceed $1,000 per package or box. Defines "ingredient" and "diaper".
