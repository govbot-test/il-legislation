Robert "Bob" Rita SYNOPSIS AS INTRODUCED: 65 ILCS 5/11-74.4-7  from Ch. 24, par. 11-74.4-765 ILCS 5/11-74.4-8  from Ch. 24, par. 11-74.4-8    Amends the Tax Increment Allocation Redevelopment Act of the Illinois Municipal Code. Provides that not more than 5% of all surplus funds in the special tax allocation fund may be distributed. Provides that surplus funds in the special tax allocation fund may be distributed not more than once every 10 years (rather than annually).
