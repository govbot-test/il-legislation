Full Text of HB4717 Illinois General Assembly ILGA.GOV HB4717 - 104th General Assembly 104TH GENERAL ASSEMBLY State of Illinois 2025 and 2026HB4717 Introduced , by Rep. Amy L. Grant SYNOPSIS AS INTRODUCED: New Act815 ILCS 505/2MMMM new    Amends the Personalized Algorithmic Pricing Disclosure Act. Provides that any entity that sets the price of goods or services using personalized algorithmic pricing, and that directly or indirectly advertises, promotes, labels, or publishes a statement, display, image, offer, or announcement of personalized algorithmic pricing to a consumer, using personal data specific to the consumer, shall include a specified disclosure. Sets forth exceptions to the provision. Provides that a violation of the Act constitutes an unlawful practice under the Consumer Fraud and Deceptive Business Practices Act. Amends the Consumer Fraud and Deceptive Business Practices Act to make a conforming change.
