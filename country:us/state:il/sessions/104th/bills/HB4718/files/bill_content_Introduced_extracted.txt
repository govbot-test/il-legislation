Full Text of HB4718 Illinois General Assembly ILGA.GOV HB4718 - 104th General Assembly 104TH GENERAL ASSEMBLY State of Illinois 2025 and 2026HB4718 Introduced , by Rep. Nicolle Grasse SYNOPSIS AS INTRODUCED: 20 ILCS 2505/2505-350 new    Amends the Department of Revenue Law of the Civil Administrative Code of Illinois. Provides that, upon request from a taxpayer or other authorized person, the Department of Revenue may provide copies of tax returns at the rate of $5 per copy per month, year, or other filing period. Provides that, if certification is required, the Department of Revenue may charge an additional $5 per certified copy.
