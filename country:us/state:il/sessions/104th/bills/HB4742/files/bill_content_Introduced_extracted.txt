Full Text of HB4742 Illinois General Assembly ILGA.GOV HB4742 - 104th General Assembly 104TH GENERAL ASSEMBLY State of Illinois 2025 and 2026HB4742 Introduced , by Rep. Justin Cochran SYNOPSIS AS INTRODUCED: 625 ILCS 5/1-117.7    Amends the Illinois Vehicle Code. Changes the definition of "electric personal assistive mobility device" to mean a self-balancing 2 non-tandem wheeled device lacking pedals designed to transport only one person that is battery-powered or has an electric propulsion system that limits the maximum speed of the device to 15 miles per hour or less. Includes in the definition of "electric personal assistive mobility device" a device that is currently out-of-class. Excludes from the definition of "electric personal assistive mobility device" a low-speed electric bicycle, low-speed scooter, motorcycle, or motor vehicle.LRB104 18222 LNS 31661 b  A BILL FOR HB4742LRB104 18222 LNS 31661 b1    AN ACT concerning transportation. 2    Be it enacted by the People of the State of Illinois, 3represented in the General Assembly: 4    Section 5. Electric personal assistive mobility device. 8A self-balancing 2 non-tandem wheeled device lacking pedals 9designed to transport only one person that is battery-powered 10or has with an electric propulsion system that limits the 11maximum speed of the device to 15 miles per hour or less. 12"Electric personal assistive mobility device" includes a 13device that is currently out-of-class, such as Onewheels, 14electric unicycles, and new and emerging micromobility 15devices, regardless of its market name. "Electric personal 16assistive mobility device" does not include a low-speed 17electric bicycle, low-speed scooter, motorcycle, or motor 18vehicle.19(Source: P.A. 92-868, eff. 6-1-03.)
