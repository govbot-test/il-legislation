Full Text of HB4765 Illinois General Assembly ILGA.GOV HB4765 - 104th General Assembly 104TH GENERAL ASSEMBLY State of Illinois 2025 and 2026HB4765 Introduced , by Rep. Jawaharial Williams SYNOPSIS AS INTRODUCED: New Act    Creates the Ammunition Vending Machine Prohibition Act. Provides that no person shall install, maintain, place, or operate, or cause to be installed, maintained, placed or operated, any mechanical or electronic device that, upon insertion of money, tokens, or any other form of payment, automatically dispenses ammunition without the contemporaneous oversight of a licensed dealer or his or her authorized employee. Provides for penalties.
