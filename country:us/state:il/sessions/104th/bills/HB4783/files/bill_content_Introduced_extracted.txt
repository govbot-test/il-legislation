Full Text of HB4783 Illinois General Assembly ILGA.GOV HB4783 - 104th General Assembly 104TH GENERAL ASSEMBLY State of Illinois 2025 and 2026HB4783 Introduced , by Rep. Michelle Mussman SYNOPSIS AS INTRODUCED: 305 ILCS 5/5-5j new    Amends the Medical Assistance Article of the Illinois Public Aid Code. Provides that for dates of service beginning on and after January 1, 2027, the Department of Healthcare and Family Services shall, for healthcare common procedure coding system codes relating to complex rehabilitation technology wheelchair repair and accessories, apply a reimbursement rate under the Medical Assistance program equivalent to the maximum fee paid in Illinois under the federal Medicare program, including fees under the KU modifier, if applicable.
