The School Code is amended by by changing 5Section 2-3.189 as follows: 6    (105 ILCS 5/2-3.189)7    Sec. 2-3.189. School unused food sharing plan.8    (a) School districts shall incorporate a food sharing plan 9for unused food into their local wellness policy under Section 102-3.139. The food sharing plan shall focus on needy students, 11with the plan being developed and supported jointly by the 12district's local health department. Participants in the child 13nutrition programs, the National School Lunch Program and 14National School Breakfast Program, the Child and Adult Care 15Food Program (CACFP), and the Summer Food Service Program 16(SFSP) shall adhere to the provisions of the Richard B. 17Russell National School Lunch Act, as well as accompanying 18guidance from the U.S. Department of Agriculture on the Food 19Donation Program, to ensure that any leftover food items are 20properly donated in order to combat potential food insecurity 21in their communities. For the purpose of this Section, 22"properly" means in accordance with all federal regulations 23and State and local health and sanitation codes.  HB4859 Enrolled- 2 -LRB104 17053 LNS 30468 b1    (b) As used in this subsection (b):2    "Child nutrition program" means a federally reimbursable 3meal program administered by the United States Department of 4Agriculture, including, but not limited to, the National 5School Lunch Program, the School Breakfast Program, and the 6Summer Food Service Program. 7    "Share table" means a designated table, cart, bin, or 8similar area within a school's food service area where a 9student may place whole, unopened food or beverage items the 10student chooses not to consume for another student to take at 11no cost.12    A school district may comply with subsection (a) by 13implementing a share table. The school district may develop 14and follow a policy on standard operating procedures for share 15tables. This policy may reference the State Board of 16Education's School Nutrition Programs Administrative Handbook 17or the United States Department of Agriculture's Guidance on 18the Food Donation Program in Child Nutrition Programs for 19share table operations.20(Source: P.A. 102-359, eff. 8-13-21; 102-813, eff. 5-13-22.) 21    Section 99.
