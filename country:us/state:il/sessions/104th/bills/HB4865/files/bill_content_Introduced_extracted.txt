Full Text of HB4865 Illinois General Assembly ILGA.GOV HB4865 - 104th General Assembly 104TH GENERAL ASSEMBLY State of Illinois 2025 and 2026HB4865 Introduced , by Rep. Janet Yang Rohr SYNOPSIS AS INTRODUCED: 110 ILCS 167/21 new    Amends the Public Higher Education Act. Requires a public institution of higher education to make fentanyl testing strips available free of charge through the student health center, a student wellness office, wellness kiosks, and resident assistant programs. Provides that fentanyl testing strips shall be provided in confidentiality to any enrolled student upon request. Allows a public institution of higher education to offer educational materials addressing drug overdose prevention.
