Fees. No fees may be required of any child who 10files a petition under this Act. 11    Section 35. Guardian ad litem. If a child is represented 12by an attorney, a proceeding under this Act may proceed 13without the appointment of a guardian ad litem for the child.  14    Section 40. Confidentiality. All court proceedings and 15documents under this Act are confidential, must be sealed, and 16may not be made available to the public.  17    Section 99. Effective date. This Act takes effect upon 18becoming law.".
