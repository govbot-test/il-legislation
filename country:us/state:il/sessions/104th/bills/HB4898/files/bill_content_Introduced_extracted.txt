Full Text of HB4898 Illinois General Assembly ILGA.GOV HB4898 - 104th General Assembly 104TH GENERAL ASSEMBLY State of Illinois 2025 and 2026HB4898 Introduced , by Rep. Maurice A. West, II SYNOPSIS AS INTRODUCED: 20 ILCS 1305/10-85 new    Amends the Department of Human Services Act. Provides that all moneys payable from funds appropriated to the Department of Human Services in State Fiscal Year 2027 for assisted outpatient treatment related projects shall be distributed by the Department evenly across the 25 judicial circuit court jurisdictions. Provides that preference shall be given to projects that work to expand access or success for assisted outpatient treatment projects working with: (1) people who have a serious mental illness; or (2) the judicial circuit courts to assist those who are part of an assisted outpatient treatment program as a result of a court order.
