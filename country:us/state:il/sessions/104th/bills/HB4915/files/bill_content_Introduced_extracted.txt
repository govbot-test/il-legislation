Full Text of HB4915 Illinois General Assembly ILGA.GOV HB4915 - 104th General Assembly 104TH GENERAL ASSEMBLY State of Illinois 2025 and 2026HB4915 Introduced , by Rep. Michelle Mussman SYNOPSIS AS INTRODUCED: 320 ILCS 30/3  from Ch. 67 1/2, par. 453    Amends the Senior Citizens Real Estate Tax Deferral Act. Provides that the real estate taxes deferred under the Act and taxes paid by the Department of Revenue under the Act, together with all interest and costs that may accrue on those amounts, shall be a prior and first lien on the property until the deferred taxes, interest, and costs are paid. Provides that the tax deferral and recovery agreement with the collector shall expressly state that those amounts are a prior and first lien.
