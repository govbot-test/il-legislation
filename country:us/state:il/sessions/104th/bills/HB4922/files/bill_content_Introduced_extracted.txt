Full Text of HB4922 Illinois General Assembly ILGA.GOV HB4922 - 104th General Assembly 104TH GENERAL ASSEMBLY State of Illinois 2025 and 2026HB4922 Introduced , by Rep. Sonya M. Harper SYNOPSIS AS INTRODUCED: 20 ILCS 205/205-455 new    Creates the Emerging and Socially Disadvantaged Farmers Infrastructure and Market Access Grant Program. Provides that the Department of Agriculture shall administer grants to eligible applicants to increase agricultural production, strengthen farm viability, and expand market access. Defines "emerging farmer," "socially disadvantaged farmer," and related terms. Specifies allowable uses of grant funds, priorities for awards, and reporting requirements.
