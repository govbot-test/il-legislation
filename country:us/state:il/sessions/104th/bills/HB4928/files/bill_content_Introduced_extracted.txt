Full Text of HB4928 Illinois General Assembly ILGA.GOV HB4928 - 104th General Assembly 104TH GENERAL ASSEMBLY State of Illinois 2025 and 2026HB4928 Introduced , by Rep. Marcus C. Evans, Jr. SYNOPSIS AS INTRODUCED: 105 ILCS 5/27-22.2  from Ch. 122, par. 27-22.2    Amends the School Code. Requires students in the public schools in grades 6 through 12 to receive instruction in career and technical education, which may be satisfied by attending a field trip to a local career and technical education training center. Allows a school board to include the required instruction in the courses of study regularly taught in the public schools, provided that the instruction is given each year to all pupils in grades 6 through 12.
