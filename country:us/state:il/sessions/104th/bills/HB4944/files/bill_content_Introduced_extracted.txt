Full Text of HB4944 Illinois General Assembly ILGA.GOV HB4944 - 104th General Assembly 104TH GENERAL ASSEMBLY State of Illinois 2025 and 2026HB4944 Introduced , by Rep. Jay Hoffman SYNOPSIS AS INTRODUCED: 720 ILCS 5/24.5-10    Amends the Criminal Code of 2012. Provides that any person, firm, corporation, co-partnership, limited liability company, association, or business, including an online retailer, that intentionally manufactures, delivers, possesses, or sells, with intent to manufacture, deliver, sell, or otherwise transfer nitrous oxide for the purpose of causing a condition of intoxication, elation, euphoria, dizziness, stupefaction, or dulling of the senses or for the purpose of changing, distorting, or disturbing the audio, visual, or mental processes is guilty of a Class 3 felony.
