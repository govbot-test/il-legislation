Full Text of HB4955 Illinois General Assembly ILGA.GOV HB4955 - 104th General Assembly 104TH GENERAL ASSEMBLY State of Illinois 2025 and 2026HB4955 Introduced , by Rep. Will Guzzardi SYNOPSIS AS INTRODUCED: New Act    Creates the Employee Ownership Development Act. Provides that the State Treasurer shall segregate a portion of the Treasurer's State investment portfolio in the Employee Ownership Development Account. Provides that moneys in the Employee Ownership Development Account shall be invested by the State Treasurer to provide employee ownership capital to employee ownership development projects seeking to locate, expand, or remain in the State. Sets forth provisions concerning Employee Ownership Development Account-Recipient Funds. Provides that the Employee Ownership Development Fund is created as a non-appropriated trust fund within the State treasury, which may receive a portion of earnings from the Employee Ownership Development Account. Provides for reporting requirements. Provides that the State Treasurer may adopt rules necessary to implement the Act.
