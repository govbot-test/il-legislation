Full Text of HB4982 Illinois General Assembly ILGA.GOV HB4982 - 104th General Assembly 104TH GENERAL ASSEMBLY State of Illinois 2025 and 2026HB4982 Introduced , by Rep. Jackie Haas SYNOPSIS AS INTRODUCED: 225 ILCS 85/45 new    Amends the Pharmacy Practice Act. Provides that every licensed pharmacy directly serving patients at a physical location shall conspicuously post a sign provided by the Department of Human Services that contains the following: (i) a statement that the Department of Human Services runs a Suicide and Crisis Lifeline; (ii) the Lifeline number; and (iii) any other information the Department of Human Services deems appropriate. Provides that the sign shall be printed in color ink or displayed electronically in color, measure at least 8.5 x 11 inches in size, and be posted at either a cashier counter or a waiting area that is clearly visible to patients. Requires licensed pharmacies that do not maintain a physical location that directly serves patients to include a copy of the sign with any dispensed prescriptions.
