Full Text of HB4988 Illinois General Assembly ILGA.GOV HB4988 - 104th General Assembly 104TH GENERAL ASSEMBLY State of Illinois 2025 and 2026HB4988 Introduced , by Rep. Nabeela Syed SYNOPSIS AS INTRODUCED: 815 ILCS 505/2MMMM new    Amends the Consumer Fraud and Deceptive Business Practices Act. Provides that the owner, licensee, or operator of a generative artificial intelligence system shall conspicuously display a warning on the system's user interface notifying users of the limitations of generative artificial intelligence systems. Provides that each day the required warning is not displayed by the owner, licensee, or operator of a generative artificial intelligence system constitutes a separate violation of the Act. Provides that a violation of the provision constitutes an unlawful practice within the meaning of the Act.LRB104 18888 SPS 32333 b  A BILL FOR HB4988LRB104 18888 SPS 32333 b1    AN ACT concerning business. 2    Be it enacted by the People of the State of Illinois, 3represented in the General Assembly: 4    Section 5.
