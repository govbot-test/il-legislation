Patrick Sheehan SYNOPSIS AS INTRODUCED: 55 ILCS 5/3-6043 new65 ILCS 5/11-1-15 new    Amends the Counties Code and the Illinois Municipal Code. Provides that, beginning January 1, 2028, the sheriff of each county and the corporate authorities of each municipality shall: (1) equip every patrol vehicle with a choking rescue device and (2) provide for every deputy or police officer to receive training to administer a choking rescue device from specified entities.
