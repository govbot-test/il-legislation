Full Text of HB5044 Illinois General Assembly ILGA.GOV HB5044 - 104th General Assembly 104TH GENERAL ASSEMBLY State of Illinois 2025 and 2026HB5044 Introduced 2/10/2026, by Rep. Jennifer Gong-Gershowitz SYNOPSIS AS INTRODUCED: New Act    Creates the Chatbot Provider Liability Act. Provides that for purposes of the Act, chatbots are deemed products for the purpose of strict liability as other product liability actions. Provides that a chatbot provider has a duty to ensure that the use of its chatbot does not cause injury to a user and is liable for any injury it caused a user through the use of its chatbot regardless of whether the chatbot provider exercised all reasonable care in the design and distribution of the chatbot; or did not directly distribute the chatbot to the user or otherwise enter into a contractual relationship with the user. Provides that a user who is harmed by the actions of a chatbot provider under this Act may file a civil action in State or federal court seeking actual damages as allowed under Illinois law, injunctive relief, and reimbursement of reasonable attorney's fees and costs.LRB104 18201 JRC 31640 b  A BILL FOR HB5044LRB104 18201 JRC 31640 b1    AN ACT concerning civil liability. 2    Be it enacted by the People of the State of Illinois, 3represented in the General Assembly: 4    Section 1.
