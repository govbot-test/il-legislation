Full Text of HB5084 Illinois General Assembly ILGA.GOV HB5084 - 104th General Assembly 104TH GENERAL ASSEMBLY State of Illinois 2025 and 2026HB5084 Introduced 2/10/2026, by Rep. Natalie A. Manley SYNOPSIS AS INTRODUCED: 70 ILCS 105/1  from Ch. 21, par. 64.24a805 ILCS 320/25 new805 ILCS 320/30 new    Amends the Cemetery Maintenance District Act. Provides that, on January 1, 2027, all cemetery associations in the State shall be incorporated as cemetery maintenance districts subject to the Act. Provides that the initial territory incorporated in a district shall be the land the cemetery association operated as a cemetery on the effective date of the amendatory Act. Amends the Cemetery Association Act to make conforming changes. Repeals the Cemetery Association Act on January 1, 2028.
