Full Text of HB5098 Illinois General Assembly ILGA.GOV HB5098 - 104th General Assembly 104TH GENERAL ASSEMBLY State of Illinois 2025 and 2026HB5098 Introduced 2/10/2026, by Rep. Nabeela Syed SYNOPSIS AS INTRODUCED: New Act    Creates the Entertainment Water Access Act. Requires places of entertainment to provide attendees with access to potable water while entertainment is occurring by: (1) providing bottled water at no cost; (2) allowing attendees to bring bottled water; or (3) allowing attendees to bring an empty bottle and providing free water for refilling. Requires the Attorney General to enforce the Act, and allows the Attorney General to provide information to owners and operators of places of entertainment. Allows a State's Attorney or the Attorney General to seek injunctive relief.
