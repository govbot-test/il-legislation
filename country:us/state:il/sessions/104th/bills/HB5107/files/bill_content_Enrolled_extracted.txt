This Act may be referred to as Alyssa's Law. 5    Section 5. The School Safety Drill Act is amended by 6adding Section 42 as follows: 7    (105 ILCS 128/42 new)8    Sec. 42. Mobile panic alert system.9    (a) By no later than the beginning of the 2028-2029 school 10year, school districts and private schools in this State shall 11consider the use of a mobile panic alert system in the 12development of their school emergency and crisis response 13plans. If used, a mobile panic alert system that meets the 14specifications of this Section shall be known as "Alyssa's 15Alert" and shall be capable of connecting to diverse emergency 16service technologies to ensure real-time coordination between 17multiple first responder agencies. It shall also be capable of 18integrating with local, Public Safety Answering Point (PSAP) 19infrastructure for the transmission of 9-1-1 calls and mobile 20activations. Such PSAP infrastructure shall also be capable of 21receiving mobile panic alert notifications from a range of 22technologies. A mobile panic alert system deployed pursuant to   HB5107 Enrolled- 2 -LRB104 18528 LNS 31971 b1this Section shall be integrated with the PSAP serving the 2applicable facility's jurisdiction and shall, upon activation, 3transmit an alert that is delivered to the PSAP's existing 4infrastructure. A mobile panic alert system deployed pursuant 5to this Section shall undergo at least annual testing in 6coordination with the PSAP and first responder agencies.7    (b) By March 1, 2027, the Illinois State Police shall 8develop rules for a mobile panic alert system to qualify as an 9Alyssa's Alert and meet the specifications of this Section. 10The rules may take into consideration the following:11        (1) the scalability of the mobile panic alert system 12    in relation to the possibility of school expansion;13        (2) the reliability of the connection technology used, 14    including the capability of transmission across both 15    Internet and cellular networks, and how that technology 16    impacts the response time for first responders and other 17    school personnel;18        (3) the ease of integration of a mobile panic alert 19    system with existing safety systems, including, but not 20    limited to, the extent to which:21            (A) reliable communication is ensured with the 22        PSAP and first responders;23            (B) all applicable safety protocols operate in a 24        coordinated and interoperable manner; and25            (C) existing PSAP infrastructure can receive 26        mobile panic alert notifications from diverse   HB5107 Enrolled- 3 -LRB104 18528 LNS 31971 b1        technologies;2        (4) the efficiency of providing instant alerts and 3    notifications to first responders and other school 4    personnel;5        (5) testing, training, and drill requirements that 6    include the PSAP and first responders agencies; and7        (6) the interoperability with crisis response mapping 8    or other situational awareness tools.9    (c) The Illinois State Police shall post information on 10the rules developed under subsection (b) on its Internet 11website. Anything posted by the Illinois State Police under 12this subsection shall also be posted by the State Board of 13Education on its website.14    (d) Each school district and private school must 15coordinate with its local 9-1-1 authority for integration 16requirements prior to purchase and implementation under this 17Section. Any expense to the PSAP for connectivity must be 18covered by the school district or private school.
