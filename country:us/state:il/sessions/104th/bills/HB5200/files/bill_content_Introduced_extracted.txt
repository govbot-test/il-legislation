Full Text of HB5200 Illinois General Assembly ILGA.GOV HB5200 - 104th General Assembly 104TH GENERAL ASSEMBLY State of Illinois 2025 and 2026HB5200 Introduced 2/10/2026, by Rep. Angelica Guerrero-Cuellar SYNOPSIS AS INTRODUCED: 35 ILCS 200/15-190 new    Amends the Property Tax Code. Provides that a homestead exemption is granted for property that (i) is located in a county with 1,000,000 or more inhabitants and (ii) is owned and occupied as a principal residence during the taxable year by a qualified municipal worker. Provides that the amount of the homestead exemption shall be a reduction from the equalized assessed value of the property in an amount equal to 5% of the equalized assessed value of the property.
