Full Text of HB5224 Illinois General Assembly ILGA.GOV HB5224 - 104th General Assembly 104TH GENERAL ASSEMBLY State of Illinois 2025 and 2026HB5224 Introduced 2/10/2026, by Rep. Rick Ryan SYNOPSIS AS INTRODUCED: 735 ILCS 5/1-110 new    Amends the Code of Civil Procedure. Provides that an electronic filing system approved by or maintained by the Supreme Court of Illinois, a State agency, or a unit of local government may be used for the filing of documents. Provides that, as soon as reasonably practicable after an electronic filing system receives and files a document, the system shall electronically notify the court, State agency, or unit of local government in which the document was filed of the filing, the person who requested the document to be filed, and the persons and entities on whom the document was served by the system. Provides that the electronic notice is proof of filing and service of that document, and no additional proof of filing or service of that document is required. Provides that proof of service of documents on persons or entities who do not receive service from the electronic filing system shall be as otherwise required by law.LRB104 20280 JRC 33731 b  A BILL FOR HB5224LRB104 20280 JRC 33731 b1    AN ACT concerning civil law. 2    Be it enacted by the People of the State of Illinois, 3represented in the General Assembly: 4    Section 5.
