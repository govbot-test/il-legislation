Full Text of HB5263 Illinois General Assembly ILGA.GOV HB5263 - 104th General Assembly 104TH GENERAL ASSEMBLY State of Illinois 2025 and 2026HB5263 Introduced 2/10/2026, by Rep. Elizabeth "Lisa" Hernandez SYNOPSIS AS INTRODUCED: New Act    Creates the Economic Stabilization and Resilience Commission Act. Creates the Economic Stabilization and Resilience Commission to research and recommend strategies to disburse resources to deliver rapid liquidity to businesses affected by immigration enforcement disruptions. Sets forth provisions concerning membership and meetings. Provides that, no later than December 31, 2027, the Commission shall create a report and submit it to the Governor and the General Assembly containing specified findings and recommendations.
