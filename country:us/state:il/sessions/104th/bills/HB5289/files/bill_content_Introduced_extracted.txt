Full Text of HB5289 Illinois General Assembly ILGA.GOV HB5289 - 104th General Assembly 104TH GENERAL ASSEMBLY State of Illinois 2025 and 2026HB5289 Introduced 2/10/2026, by Rep. Justin Cochran SYNOPSIS AS INTRODUCED: 105 ILCS 5/27-517 new    Amends the Courses of Study Article of the School Code. Provides that, beginning with the 2027-2028 school year, every public high school shall include in its curriculum a unit of instruction on civic engagement. Provides that, as part of the unit of instruction, students must attend an open meeting of the governing body of a special district, the corporate authorities of a municipality, the township board of a township, the county board of a county, or any similar governing or legislating body of a unit of government. Provides that, if physical attendance at such a meeting is not feasible, then attendance may be virtual.LRB104 17991 LNS 31428 bSTATE MANDATES ACT MAY REQUIRE REIMBURSEMENTMAY APPLY A BILL FOR HB5289LRB104 17991 LNS 31428 b1    AN ACT concerning education. 2    Be it enacted by the People of the State of Illinois, 3represented in the General Assembly: 4    Section 5.
