The Veterinary Medicine and Surgery Practice 5Act of 2004 is amended by adding Section 8.2 as follows: 6    (225 ILCS 115/8.2 new)7    Sec. 8.2. Rabies vaccination authority; rabies vaccination 8certificates. 9    (a) A certified veterinary technician who has completed 10the required 2-hour rabies vaccination continuing education 11course may administer rabies vaccinations only under the 12direct supervision of a licensed veterinarian, and only after 13the supervising veterinarian has performed a physical 14examination of the animal and determined that the animal is 15healthy enough to receive the vaccination. 16    (b) The 2-hour rabies vaccination continuing education 17course shall apply toward the total number of hours of 18continuing education required by rule. This course must be 19repeated each renewal cycle that the certified veterinary 20technician administers the rabies vaccination. 21    (c) The rabies administration continuing education program 22must be taught by a licensed veterinarian in good standing and 23provided by a continuing education provider approved by the   HB5411 Engrossed- 2 -LRB104 19855 BDA 33305 b1Department. 2    (d) Nothing in this Section authorizes a certified 3veterinary technician to diagnose, prognose, prescribe, 4perform surgery, or engage in any activity prohibited under 5this Act. 6    (e) A certified veterinary technician administering rabies 7vaccinations under this Section remains subject to all 8disciplinary provisions of this Act. 9    (f) A rabies vaccination certificate may be signed only 10by: 11        (1) a licensed veterinarian who administered the 12    vaccination; or 13        (2) a certified veterinary technician who has 14    completed the required rabies vaccination continuing 15    education program during the previous renewal period and 16    administered the rabies vaccination.17    A rabies vaccination certificate that is signed by a 18certified veterinary technician shall also be signed by the 19veterinarian providing direct supervision of the certified 20veterinary technician. The professional credential of the 21individual or individuals who sign the certificate must be 22included with their signature. 23    (g) A copy of the current rabies vaccination continuing 24education certificate must be on display at the locations 25where the certified veterinary technician administers rabies 26vaccinations.   HB5411 Engrossed- 3 -LRB104 19855 BDA 33305 b1    (h) Any Illinois statute, administrative rule, or local 2ordinance that restricts rabies vaccination administration or 3certificate signing exclusively to veterinarians is amended to 4permit Certified Veterinary Technicians who have completed the 5required rabies vaccination continuing education course to 6perform these functions as authorized under this Act. 7    Any reference to "rabies inoculation by a licensed 8veterinarian only" shall be interpreted to include certified 9veterinary technicians who have completed the required rabies 10vaccination continuing education program acting under direct 11supervision of a licensed veterinarian.
