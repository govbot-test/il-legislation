Full Text of HB5419 Illinois General Assembly ILGA.GOV HB5419 - 104th General Assembly 104TH GENERAL ASSEMBLY State of Illinois 2025 and 2026HB5419 Introduced 2/13/2026, by Rep. Ann M. Williams SYNOPSIS AS INTRODUCED: 20 ILCS 3501/830-20    Amends the Illinois Finance Authority Act. Provides that the maximum amount of notes or bonds that may be issued by the Illinois Finance Authority for any one agricultural real estate borrower may be increased by a higher amount than currently allowed if a higher amount is permitted by statute or regulation. In provisions concerning agricultural assistance loans, removes provisions providing that no proceeds from any bonds issued by the Illinois Finance Authority shall be loaned for the purchase of new depreciable agricultural property to any natural person who has a net worth in excess of $500,000.
