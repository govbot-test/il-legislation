The Illinois Procurement Code is amended by 5changing Section 15-35 as follows: 6    (30 ILCS 500/15-35)7    Sec. 15-35. Vendor portal. Each chief procurement officer 8may, in consultation with the agencies under his or her 9jurisdiction and the Procurement Policy Board, establish a 10vendor portal. The vendor portal shall allow a potential 11vendor to provide certifications, disclosures, registrations, 12and other documentation needed to do business with a State 13agency in advance of any particular procurement. A potential 14vendor who registers with the vendor portal and provides this 15information may submit its registration number, with a 16confirmation that the portal information remains current, as 17part of its response to a competitive selection or a 18contracting process, rather than submit the same information 19in full. One or more chief procurement officers may jointly 20operate a vendor portal if a single portal would better serve 21the needs of the State agencies and the vendor community. This 2Section applies notwithstanding any laws to the contrary 3except for later enacted laws that specifically refer to this 4Section.5    Nothing in this Section shall preclude a State agency from 6implementing its own pre-qualification, certification, 7disclosure, and registration requirements necessary to conduct 8and manage its program operation.9    This Section does not apply to any contract for any 10project as to which federal funds are available for 11expenditure when its provisions may be in conflict with 12federal law or federal regulation.13    Notwithstanding any other provision of law, the vendor 14portal for the Department of Transportation must include any 15locally-applicable project labor agreement requirements. 16(Source: P.A. 97-895, eff. 8-3-12; 98-1076, eff. 1-1-15.)
