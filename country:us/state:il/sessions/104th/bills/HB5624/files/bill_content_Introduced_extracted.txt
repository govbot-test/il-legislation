Full Text of HB5624 Illinois General Assembly ILGA.GOV HB5624 - 104th General Assembly 104TH GENERAL ASSEMBLY State of Illinois 2025 and 2026HB5624 Introduced 2/13/2026, by Rep. Jawaharial Williams SYNOPSIS AS INTRODUCED:     Appropriates $700,000 from the General Revenue Fund to the Department of Commerce and Economic Opportunity for a grant to the Illinois Center for Employee Ownership for the purpose of expanding education, outreach, and technical assistance related to employee ownership transitions and supporting small businesses in evaluating and pursuing succession through employee ownership. Effective July 1, 2026.
