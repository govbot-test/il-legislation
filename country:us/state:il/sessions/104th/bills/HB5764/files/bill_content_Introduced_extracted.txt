William E Hauter SYNOPSIS AS INTRODUCED: 410 ILCS 22/70410 ILCS 22/90 rep.    Amends the End-of-Life Options for Terminally Ill Patients Act. Deletes provisions requiring a health care professional or health care entity unable or unwilling to carry out aid in dying to refer the individual to another health care professional or health care entity. Repeals provisions concerning the signing of a death certificate and the contents of a death certificate for a death under the Act.
