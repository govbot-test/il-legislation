SYNOPSIS AS INTRODUCED:
 
30 ILCS 105/8.57
    Amends the State Finance Act. Repeals provisions requiring transfers to the Budget Reserve for Immediate Disbursements and Governmental Emergencies Fund from the State Pheasant Fund, the State Migratory Waterfowl Stamp Fund, the Fish and Wildlife Endowment Fund, and the Illinois Habitat Fund.
