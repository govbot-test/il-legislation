12    Section 10. Definitions. In this Act: 
13    "Autism-related data" means personally identifiable 
14information maintained by a State agency that reveals an 
15individual has been identified as autistic. 
16    "Data-scraping technology" means automated extraction or 
17copying of data from a website, application, or service 
18outside an authorized application programming interface or 
19other authorized data exchange mechanism, for the purpose of 
20collecting personally identifiable information.
21    "De-identified data" means data that has been 
22de-identified in accordance with 45 CFR 164.514 or successor 
23regulations.
24    "Informed consent" means voluntary, written authorization 
25given by an individual, or the individual's legal guardian,

1after being clearly informed of the nature, scope, purpose, 
2risks, and potential consequences of the data collection or 
3disclosure.
4    "State agency" means any agency, department, office, 
5officer, division, bureau, board, commission, or other entity 
6of the executive branch of Illinois State government. 
 
7    Section 15. Limitation on collection of autism-related 
8data. 
9    (a) A State agency shall not collect, or use data-scraping

22        benefits program, including payment and reimbursement, 
23        providing benefits, or ensuring equality of 
24        opportunity, participation, or accessibility; 
25            (B) the provision of medical or specialized care

4    Section 20. Restrictions on disclosure of autism-related 
5data. 
6    (a) A State agency shall not disclose personally 
7identifiable autism-related data to any entity outside 
8Illinois State government unless: 
9        (1) the individual, or the individual's legal 
10    guardian, provides informed, written consent for a 
11    specific use;

13    court; 
14        (3) the disclosure is necessary to provide 
15    educational, medical, employment, housing, or other 
16    essential services and supports to the individual; or
17        (4) the disclosure is required to comply with 
18    established State or federal law. 
19    (b) All disclosures must be limited to the minimum amount 
20of information necessary to meet the legal requirement and, if 
21permitted and practicable, must be made using de-identified 
22data.
23    (c) Autism-related data is exempt from inspection and 
24copying under the Freedom of Information Act to the extent 
25disclosure is prohibited by this Act or other State or federal
