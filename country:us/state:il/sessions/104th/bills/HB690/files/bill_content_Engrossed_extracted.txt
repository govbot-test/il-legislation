Any such action 6taken by the county board shall be by an appropriate 7resolution passed at a regular meeting.8    (b) Notwithstanding any other provision of this Section, 9when any election is held and the results of such election are 10required by law to be returned to the county clerk, the office 11of the county clerk shall remain open for the purpose of 12receiving such results from the time of opening of the polls 13until the returns from each precinct have been received.14    (c) Any county clerk who fails to keep the clerk's office 15open for the purpose of receiving election returns as required 16by this Section commits a business offense, and shall be fined 17not less than $500 nor more than $5,000.18(Source: P.A. 101-253, eff. 8-9-19.)
