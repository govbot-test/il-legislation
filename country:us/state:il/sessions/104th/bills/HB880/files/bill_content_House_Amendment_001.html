8    (a) The Hunger Relief Fund is created as a special fund in

11to the extent practicable, providers for the purpose of 
12purchasing food and related supplies. In this Section, "food 
13bank" means a public or charitable institution that maintains 
14an established operation involving the provision of food or 
15edible commodities, or the products of food or edible 
16commodities, to food pantries, soup kitchens, hunger relief
