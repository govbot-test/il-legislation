HOUSE JOINT RESOLUTION

 
2    WHEREAS, The members of the Illinois House of 
3Representatives wish to congratulate Coach Neil Alexander of 
4Lincoln Community High School on his 1,000th career victory as 
5a boys' basketball coach; and
 
6    WHEREAS, Coach Alexander's impressive career began as a 
7student athlete at Nokomis High School in Montgomery County; 
8he played for four years at Monmouth College, where he was 
9later inducted into the Monmouth College Hall of Fame in 1998; 
10he later earned a master's degree from Western Illinois 
11University; and
 
12    WHEREAS, Coach Alexander began coaching at Joy Westmer 
13High School, later coaching at Monmouth Yorkwood High School 
14and then at Bushnell-Prairie City High School for 11 years; 
15after his time there, he became the head coach of the boys 
16basketball team at Lincoln Community High School; and
 
17    WHEREAS, Coach Alexander, in his time at Lincoln Community 
18High School, has led his teams to 26 seasons with 20 or more 
19wins, 19 regional championships, five super-sectional 
20appearances, and four Elite Eight state tournament runs in 
211999, 2005, 2007, and 2014, with the 2014 run resulting in a 
22state runner-up finish; and
