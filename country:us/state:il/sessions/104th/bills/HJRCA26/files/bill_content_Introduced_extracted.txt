La Shawn K. Ford SYNOPSIS AS INTRODUCED: ILCON Art. IX, Sec. 3ILCON Art. IX, Sec. 12 new    Proposes to amend the Revenue Article of the Illinois Constitution. Provides that an additional income tax shall be imposed on individuals in an amount equal to 3% of the portion of the individual's income that is greater than $1,000,000 for the taxable year. Provides that the revenue collected from the tax shall be used to provide property tax relief.
