HOUSE RESOLUTION

 
2    WHEREAS, The members of the Illinois House of 
3Representatives are saddened to learn of the death of Dorothy 
4A. Kane of Park Ridge, who passed away on June 13, 2026; and
 
5    WHEREAS, Dorothy Kane, the daughter of George 
6Sczypiorowski and Katherine Twarowski, was born in Stanley, 
7Wisconsin on July 14, 1924; and
 
8    WHEREAS, Dorothy Kane was the quintessential grandmother 
9figure; whether you were family or a family friend, she 
10welcomed all with a love and an exuberance that only a 
11grandmother could provide; and
 
12    WHEREAS, Dorothy Kane was a decades-long parishioner of 
13Our Lady of Ransom Church, where she found great joy in her 
14church family, especially serving the parish with her friends 
15in the ladies auxiliary; and
 
16    WHEREAS, Dorothy Kane had a long record of service to her 
17community, including supporting youth causes through a 
18lifelong involvement with the local Moose Lodge and being well 
19known for the impressive amount of blood donations she made 
20with the American Red Cross; and
