Full Text of HR0526 Illinois General Assembly ILGA.GOV HR0526 - 104th General Assembly HR0526LRB104 15997 MST 29246 r1HOUSE RESOLUTION 2    WHEREAS, For 250 years, the United States Postal Service 3(USPS) has bound this nation together; and 4    WHEREAS, The USPS delivers prescriptions to seniors and 5veterans, ballots to voters, and shipments for small 6businesses and provides reliable service to rural and remote 7areas, including many indigenous communities, where private 8companies often refuse service because it is not profitable; 9and 10    WHEREAS, Only the USPS serves every ZIP code and every 11household, regardless of geography or income; and 12    WHEREAS, The USPS faces renewed threats of privatization, 13which would mean jobs eliminated, closed post offices, and 14higher rates, and the loss of universal service at affordable 15rates to all ZIP codes would devastate families, local 16economies, and entire communities; and 17    WHEREAS, People have demonstrated how strongly they oppose 18privatization, including a grassroots effort that has sent 19more than half a million letters to Congress demanding 20protection of the USPS; therefore, be it   HR0526- 2 -LRB104 15997 MST 29246 r1    RESOLVED, BY THE HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES OF THE ONE 2HUNDRED FOURTH GENERAL ASSEMBLY OF THE STATE OF ILLINOIS, that 3we affirm that the United States Postal Service is a public 4service that belongs to the people and oppose any effort to 5dismantle, privatize, or weaken this vital institution; and be 6it further 7    RESOLVED, That a suitable copy of this resolution be 8delivered to U.S. Postmaster General David Steiner.
