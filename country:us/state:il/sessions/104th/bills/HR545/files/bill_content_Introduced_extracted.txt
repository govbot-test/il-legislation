and 17    WHEREAS, Dakota Welch found solace and strength in his 18faith and found a spiritual home at the Village Christian 19Church in Minooka; his faith was evident in the peace he sought 20and the love he gave so freely; and   HR0545- 2 -LRB104 16119 MST 29444 r1    WHEREAS, Dakota Welch enjoyed soccer, fishing, and hockey, 2passions that brought him joy and connection with others; and 3    WHEREAS, Dakota Welch's life was filled with laughter, 4friendship, and generosity; with a genuine love for all, his 5gentle spirit and kind heart made him beloved by all who knew 6him, and he offered kindness to everyone he met; and 7    WHEREAS, The Welch family has created the Dakota Welch 8Memorial Fund to honor his memory, which will make grants and 9scholarships available to the community; therefore, be it 10    RESOLVED, BY THE HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES OF THE ONE 11HUNDRED FOURTH GENERAL ASSEMBLY OF THE STATE OF ILLINOIS, that 12we mourn the passing of Dakota James Welch and extend our 13sincerest condolences to his family, friends, and all who knew 14and loved him;
