6    WHEREAS, The Thiess International River Prize, established

10economic benefits worldwide; and
 
11    WHEREAS, Friends of the Chicago River was awarded the

14Symposium in Brisbane, Australia on September 10, 2025; and
 
15    WHEREAS, Friends of the Chicago River, founded in 1979, is

18system, leading bold policy reforms, habitat restoration, and 
19public engagement that have transformed the river from an 
20overlooked industrial waterway into a thriving urban 
21ecosystem; and
