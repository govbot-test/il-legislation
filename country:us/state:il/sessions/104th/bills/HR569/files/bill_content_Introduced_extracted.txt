he guided Park Forest during a pivotal era when the 17village became one of the nation's first successfully 18integrated communities, and his tenure was marked by bold 19initiatives and a belief that communities thrive through 20collaboration; he championed the redevelopment of Norwood and 21Blackhawk Centers, advanced early Tax Increment Financing 22strategies, and helped launch ventures that broadened the 23village's cultural and social fabric; and 6    WHEREAS, Ron Bean's influence extended far beyond Park 7Forest; he served as executive director of the Illinois 8Development Finance Authority for 15 years, overseeing the 9issuance of billions in bonds that spurred statewide economic 10development and job creation; his leadership later proved 11indispensable at the Community and Economic Development 12Association of Cook County (CEDA), where he guided the 13organization through a fiscal restructuring that strengthened 14its capacity to serve families in need;
