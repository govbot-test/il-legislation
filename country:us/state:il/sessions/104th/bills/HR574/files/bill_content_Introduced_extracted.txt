and 5    WHEREAS, EMMAUS is a Christian, non-profit organization 6committed to ending human trafficking and sexual exploitation, 7with the unique and vital focus on supporting male-identifying 8victims and survivors; and 9    WHEREAS, EMMAUS provides critical outreach, advocacy, and 10restorative services to men and boys who are often overlooked 11in the broader anti-trafficking movement, despite comprising 12over one-third of those exploited in the sex trade; and 13    WHEREAS, Fewer than 2% of anti-trafficking organizations 14in the United States offer programs specifically for male 15survivors, making EMMAUS a rare and essential voice for this 16underserved population; and 17    WHEREAS, EMMAUS operates with a mission to support male 18survivors through their journey out of the sex trade and into 19freedom, guided by values that include affirming human 20dignity, empowering survivors, building inclusive communities, 21and practicing transparency and perseverance; and   HR0574- 2 -LRB104 16648 ECR 30052 r1    WHEREAS, EMMAUS' work is rooted in compassion, faith, and 2a commitment to justice, offering trauma-informed care, street 3outreach, and long-term support to help survivors rebuild 4their lives; and 5    WHEREAS, EMMAUS has demonstrated a long-standing 6commitment to this cause, with over 35 years of service and a 7growing impact in the Chicago area and beyond; therefore, be 8it 9    RESOLVED, BY THE HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES OF THE ONE 10HUNDRED FOURTH GENERAL ASSEMBLY OF THE STATE OF ILLINOIS, that 11we commend EMMAUS for its unwavering dedication to ending 12human trafficking and for supporting male survivors of 13exploitation; and be it further 14    RESOLVED, That we commend EMMAUS for working toward its 15mission to validate the worth of every individual, reject 16stigma, and create a world where all survivors, regardless of 17gender, can find healing, hope, and a path to wholeness; and be 18it further 19    RESOLVED, That a suitable copy of this resolution be 20delivered to EMMAUS as a symbol of our esteem and respect.
