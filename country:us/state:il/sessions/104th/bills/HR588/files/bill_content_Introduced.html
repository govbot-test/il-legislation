HOUSE RESOLUTION

 
2    WHEREAS, The members of the Illinois House of 
3Representatives wish to congratulate Leo High School on the 
4occasion of its 100th anniversary of providing education to 
5young men on Chicago's South Side; and
 
6    WHEREAS, Leo High School opened its doors in the Auburn 
7Gresham neighborhood of Chicago on September 7, 1926; the 
8school started with more than 120 students and quickly grew 
9its class sizes and footprint while achieving acclaim for the 
10academic, athletic, and cultural activities of its students 
11and alumni; and
 
12    WHEREAS, Leo High School has reflected both the 
13opportunities and challenges present in its community and the 
14City of Chicago at large, most notably by being resilient in 
15the face of population loss and demographic change, evolving 
16from once having an all-white student body to having a student 
17body that is all-Black or Hispanic today; and
 
18    WHEREAS, At present, Leo High School has more than 200 
19enrolled students and features a student-to-teacher ratio of 
2013-to-1, ensuring that students and teachers are able to 
21benefit from enhanced student-teacher interactions and a more 
22active learning environment; and

1    WHEREAS, Leo High School offers a college preparatory

8experiences and opportunities in leadership, teamwork, time

11that aims for students to develop character and discipline in 
12addition to sporting skills, which has helped its teams, the 
13Lions, to compete in more than 60 championships, earning 
14titles in track and field, football, and basketball; and
 
15    WHEREAS, Leo High School further has a talented men's 
16choir, which gained national recognition while competing and 
17being named a finalist on the competition show America's Got

19through performances at celebrations honoring Pope Leo XIV, a 
20Chicago area native, the 2024 Democratic National Convention 
21in Chicago, and as part of the 2025 Juneteenth Celebration at 
22Wrigley Field for the Chicago Cubs; and
