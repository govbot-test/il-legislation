and 7    WHEREAS, Illinois medicolegal professionals are entrusted 8with the accurate and impartial investigation of deaths, the 9determination of cause and manner of death, and the 10application of scientific principles in support of the justice 11system; and 12    WHEREAS, These professionals are routinely required to 13respond to death scenes, mass fatality incidents, public 14health emergencies, natural disasters, and acts of violence 15alongside law enforcement, fire service, emergency medical 16services, and other public safety personnel; and 17    WHEREAS, Despite their essential on-scene and emergency 18response responsibilities, medicolegal professionals are not 19uniformly recognized as first responders, notwithstanding 20their exposure to hazardous environments, traumatic scenes, 21biohazards, and significant occupational stressors; and   HR0601- 2 -LRB104 17792 ECR 31224 r1    WHEREAS, Acknowledging medicolegal professionals as first 2responders affirms the critical nature of their work, supports 3parity in professional recognition and resources, and enhances 4coordinated emergency response, responder wellness, and public 5safety outcomes; and 6    WHEREAS, The National Association of Medical Examiners 7(NAME) and the International Association of Coroners and 8Medical Examiners (IACME) have each issued position statements 9recognizing the importance of Medicolegal Professionals' Week, 10affirming the essential role medicolegal professionals play in 11death investigation, public health, the administration of 12justice, and emergency response; and 13    WHEREAS, Recognizing Medicolegal Professionals' Week 14fosters public awareness of the indispensable role these 15professionals play in protecting public health, ensuring 16accountability, and upholding justice across the State of 17Illinois; therefore, be it 18    RESOLVED, BY THE HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES OF THE ONE 19HUNDRED FOURTH GENERAL ASSEMBLY OF THE STATE OF ILLINOIS, that 20we declare the week of January 25 through January 31, 2026 as 21Medicolegal Professionals' Week in the State of Illinois.
