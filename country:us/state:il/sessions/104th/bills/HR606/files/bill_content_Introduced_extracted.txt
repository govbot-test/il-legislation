and 5    WHEREAS, Ronald Meinsma of Savanna dedicated 60 years of 6his life to serving three different communities in Carroll 7County over the course of his career; and 8    WHEREAS, Ronald Meinsma served as a volunteer firefighter 9and firefighter engineer in Chadwick for two years, in Mt. 10Carroll for 46 years, and in Savanna for 12 years; and 15    WHEREAS, Ronald Meinsma advanced as a volunteer 16firefighter in each community he served, eventually earning 17the title of firefighter engineer with the responsibility of 18driving the fire truck, which was his favorite of all the roles 19he held; and 20    WHEREAS, Alongside his volunteer firefighting career,   HR0606- 2 -LRB104 18536 LAW 31979 r1Ronald Meinsma worked as a fuel truck driver for FS, often 2delivering fuel to emergency situations after responding as a 3firefighter; and 4    WHEREAS, Outside of his volunteer firefighting duties, 5Ronald Meinsma has placed American flags nearly every Memorial 6Day at the gravestones of every deceased firefighter he could 7find across Carroll County and beyond; and 8    WHEREAS, Ronald Meinsma has never sought personal 9recognition or glory for his work, serving as a humble and 10committed public servant and first responder; therefore, be it 11    RESOLVED, BY THE HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES OF THE ONE 12HUNDRED FOURTH GENERAL ASSEMBLY OF THE STATE OF ILLINOIS, that 13we congratulate Ronald Jay Meinsma on his retirement as a 14volunteer firefighter, and we thank him for his six decades of 15service throughout Carroll County;
