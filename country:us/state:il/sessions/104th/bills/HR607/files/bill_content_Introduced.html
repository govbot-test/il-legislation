HOUSE RESOLUTION

 
2    WHEREAS, More than 55 years ago, January was designated as 
3National Blood Donor Month as an annual observance to honor 
4voluntary blood donors and to encourage more people to donate 
5blood at a time when blood supplies are historically low; and
 
6    WHEREAS, Blood donations are the backbone of Illinois' 
7health care; every two seconds, someone needs a blood 
8transfusion, but blood cannot be manufactured and is only made 
9possible by generous donors who roll up their sleeves to help 
10save lives; and
 
11    WHEREAS, The Illinois population is aging, and too few new 
12donors are volunteering to take the place of those unable to 
13donate any longer; only about 3% of the age-eligible 
14population donates blood, but patient needs are constant; and
 
15    WHEREAS, Type O blood is often used in trauma situations, 
16making it the most needed blood type and most likely to be in 
17short supply during a blood emergency; and
 
18    WHEREAS, Patients requiring blood transfusions include 
19cancer patients, accident, burn, and trauma victims, newborn 
20babies and their mothers, transplant recipients, surgery 
21patients, chronically transfused patients suffering from

2be it
 
3    RESOLVED, BY THE HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES OF THE ONE 
4HUNDRED FOURTH GENERAL ASSEMBLY OF THE STATE OF ILLINOIS, that 
5we declare January 2026 as Blood Donor Month in the State of 
6Illinois; and be it further
 
7    RESOLVED, That we urge all citizens to celebrate the cause 
8of blood donation and to recognize the lifesaving 
9contributions of volunteer blood donors; and be it further
 
10    RESOLVED, That we urge all eligible blood donors to donate 
11regularly and for businesses and organizations throughout the 
12State of Illinois to sponsor community blood drives with 
13ImpactLife, Rock River Valley Blood Center, Versiti, and 
14Vitalant, among other blood centers, providing residents of 
15our State the opportunity to save lives.
