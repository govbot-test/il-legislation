and 3    WHEREAS, Technology industry leaders across the nation 4have indicated that increased usage of artificial intelligence 5will result in less work; and 6    WHEREAS, Illinois has the opportunity to be a national 7leader on addressing workplace burnout and returning increased 8productivity to working families;
