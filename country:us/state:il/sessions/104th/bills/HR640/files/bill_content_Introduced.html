7    WHEREAS, Southside Blooms, a project of the nonprofit 
8Chicago Eco House, was founded by Quilen and Hannah Blackwell 
9with a vision to bring hope, healing, and economic opportunity 
10to neighborhoods on the South and West Sides of Chicago; and
 
11    WHEREAS, Southside Blooms has pioneered a "farm-to-vase" 
12model that transforms formerly abandoned and vacant city lots 
13in Englewood, Woodlawn, Washington Park, Pilsen, and West 
14Garfield Park into thriving, solar-powered, pesticide-free 
15flower farms; and
 
16    WHEREAS, Southside Blooms is an innovative social 
17enterprise that provides critical workforce development and 
18jobs for at-risk youth ages 16 to 24, offering a "pipeline for 
19youth" that includes partnerships with Cook County Juvenile 
20Probation to help formerly incarcerated young people 
21reintegrate into society through merit-based employment; and
