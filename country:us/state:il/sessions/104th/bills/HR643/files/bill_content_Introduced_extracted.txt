he was 19elected to the board of directors of the Illinois Association 20of Fire Protection Districts in 1998 and served as its 21president from 2005 to 2025; and 22    WHEREAS, Mike Dillon was a member of the OSFM Fire   HR0643- 2 -LRB104 20489 MST 33961 r1Advisory Commission, the Illinois Fire Service Institute 2Advisory, and the Statewide Interoperability Communications 3Executive Committee; and 8    WHEREAS, Mike Dillon was preceded in death by his parents 9and his nephew, Daniel Dillon; and 10    WHEREAS, Mike Dillon is survived by his beloved wife of 54 11years, Dawn, and his siblings, Kathy Dillon, Dan (Charita) 12Dillon, Patrick Dillon, and Jennifer Brown;
