2    WHEREAS, Dion's Chicago Dream (DCD), founded by Englewood 
3native and United States Navy veteran Dion Dawson in 2020, is a 
4non-profit social enterprise that combats food insecurity 
5through a logistics-first model, delivering fresh produce 
6directly to households across Chicagoland while centering 
7quality, dignity, and consistency in service; DCD's flagship 
8Dream Deliveries program provides five days worth of fresh 
9fruits and vegetables straight to residents' doorsteps, 
10immediately improving household food security; and
 
11    WHEREAS, DCD's innovative "last-mile" approach has been 
12profiled and recognized regionally and nationally for 
13reimagining the traditional, queue-based food distribution 
14model, replacing lines and limited hours with direct-to-door 
15delivery and dignified access and integrating with health and 
16Food-Is-Medicine partners to support prevention and better 
17outcomes; and
 
18    WHEREAS, DCD has grown from an early community fridge to a 
19county-wide engine for health equity, with program components 
20including Dream Deliveries, which are direct home deliveries, 
21and the Dream Vault, which is an Amazon-style locker that 
22allows recipients to retrieve a full produce box using a 
23unique code, extending the access and reach of last-mile
