HOUSE RESOLUTION

 
2    WHEREAS, The University of Illinois College of Medicine at 
3Rockford is the primary administrator of the Illinois Rural 
4Medical Education (RMED) Program; and
 
5    WHEREAS, The RMED Program is recognized as a valuable 
6resource to rural Illinois communities with 88% of RMED 
7graduates staying in Illinois practice in rural Illinois 
8communities; and
 
9    WHEREAS, The RMED program graduated 484 students from 1993 
10to 2023, 50% of whom practice medicine within 100 miles of 
11their hometown; and
 
12    WHEREAS, It has come to the attention of the General 
13Assembly that only 56% of RMED's graduates practice in 
14Illinois while the programs primary goal is to produce medical 
15practitioners who will live and work in Illinois's rural 
16communities; and
 
17    WHEREAS, Individuals who are from Illinois, would like to 
18be part of the RMED program, and would likely stay in Illinois 
19are being turned away from entering the RMED program in favor 
20of students who come from other states; and
