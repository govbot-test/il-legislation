HOUSE RESOLUTION

 
2    WHEREAS, The members of the Illinois House of 
3Representatives are saddened to learn of the death of Mabel 
4Orange, who passed away on November 12, 2025; and
 
5    WHEREAS, Mabel Orange was born to Jefferson Sr. and Eloise 
6Nethery in Chicago on April 12, 1949; she married Guy Orange 
7Jr. on July 1, 1967, and they had two children, Christel and 
8Yolanda; and
 
9    WHEREAS, Mabel Orange, being hearing-impaired herself, 
10carried a deep passion for serving individuals with special 
11needs, leading her to a 33-year career at Jesse Spalding, 
12where she became one of the first women of color to serve as a 
13child welfare attendant under SEIU; she was loved by the 
14children she served, and many remembered her heart, her 
15patience, and her commitment to their care; she retired with 
16immense pride in the work she had done; and
 
17    WHEREAS, Mabel Orange committed to her faith at an early 
18age and was raised at First Steadfast M.B.C.; after 
19retirement, she joined St. John Evangelist M.B.C., where she 
20worshiped faithfully until her health failed her in 2022; her 
21worship extended beyond church walls, and her social media 
22became her ministry, blessing many with praises, prayers, and
