and 5    WHEREAS, Lillian Drummond was born on October 13, 1921; 6she was a longtime resident of the Austin neighborhood and was 7instrumental in the creation of the Austin Senior Satellite 8Center right across the street from her house; and 9    WHEREAS, Lillian Drummond's most enduring work centered on 10energy affordability, a basic necessity she believed should 11never be denied to the poor or elderly; she helped organize the 12Affordable Budget Coalition in 1985, which successfully pushed 13for a 12% Energy Assistance Plan to keep heat and light 14available to low-income households; later, her advocacy helped 15lead to the passage of the Percentage of Income Payment Plan 16enacted in 2009; and 17    WHEREAS, Lillian Drummond was also a founding member of 18the South Austin Coalition Community Council; and 19    WHEREAS, Lillian Drummond was inducted into the Illinois 20Senior Citizen Hall of Fame and received an honorary street 21designation from the City of Chicago; she received the Edward   HR0674- 2 -LRB104 20833 MST 34413 r1Bailey Lifetime Community Service Award in 2006; and 2    WHEREAS, Lillian Drummond was affectionately known as 3"Mama Lill" and was a woman of grace, resilience, and 4unwavering love; and 5    WHEREAS, Lillian Drummond taught by example what it meant 6to endure, to forgive, and to love deeply; therefore, be it 7    RESOLVED, BY THE HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES OF THE ONE 8HUNDRED FOURTH GENERAL ASSEMBLY OF THE STATE OF ILLINOIS, that 9we mourn the passing of Lillian Drummond and extend our 10sincere condolences to her family, friends, and all who knew 11and loved her; and be it further 12    RESOLVED, That a suitable copy of this resolution be 13presented to the family of Lillian Drummond as an expression 14of our deepest sympathy.
