Full Text of HR0685 Illinois General Assembly ILGA.GOV HR0685 - 104th General Assembly HR0685LRB104 21019 LAW 34924 r1HOUSE RESOLUTION 2    WHEREAS, The Tribute to Valor Foundation was founded in 32018 as an all-volunteer organization focused on influencing, 4inspiring, and impacting the youth of America to ask 5themselves "Who Am I?" and to answer by reflecting on the six 6core character values derived from our nation's highest 7military decoration for valor, the Medal of Honor; and 8    WHEREAS, The Tribute to Valor Foundation works to help the 9youth of America to understand, engage in, and incorporate 10into their daily lives the core values of the Medal of Honor; 11and 12    WHEREAS, The six core values of the Medal of Honor are 13courage, sacrifice, patriotism, citizenship, integrity, and 14commitment; and 15    WHEREAS, Courage is the mental and moral strength to face 16fear, danger, and adversity, and includes both physical and 17moral courage, and it is the willingness to stand up for what 18is right, even when it is unpopular or difficult, and the 19strength to defend those that are disenfranchised or 20mistreated; and 21    WHEREAS, Sacrifice is a voluntary act of surrendering   HR0685- 2 -LRB104 21019 LAW 34924 r1personal interests for a greater cause and involves a selfless 2surrender of one's own interests or well-being for the 3betterment of another, and it is often considered an act of 4love or a demonstration of a deeper principle; and 5    WHEREAS, Patriotism is a devotion to and defense of the 6ideals of our country and is a sense of purpose derived from 7supporting the nation and its freedoms, and it manifests 8through disciplined service, commitment, and a belief in 9something greater than oneself; and 10    WHEREAS, Citizenship is the status of being a responsible 11member of a community, which includes both rights and 12responsibilities, and it means understanding your rights and 13duties, following rules, respecting others, and contributing 14positively to your community through actions such as teamwork 15and helping others; and 16    WHEREAS, Integrity is the desire to do what is right, 17legally and morally, which includes honesty, a firm adherence 18to moral and ethical principles, and acting consistently with 19one's values even when no one is watching, and it means being 20truthful and upright, saying what you mean, and doing what you 21say, with integrity forming the foundation of trust, a value 22that requires both personal moral courage and a commitment; 23and   HR0685- 3 -LRB104 21019 LAW 34924 r1    WHEREAS, Commitment is a pledge or an obligation to do 2something in the present and future, including a strong 3dedication to a cause, a person, or an activity, and it is the 4belief that giving your word and standing behind it is 5honorable and that you fulfill your promise despite any 6obstacles or adversity you might face; and 7    WHEREAS, By empowering the youth of America to face 8adversity and changing circumstances with the core values of 9the Medal of Honor, the Tribute to Valor Foundation hopes the 10next generation will gain the strength to make choices of 11which they can be proud and that will help them to become 12better citizens; therefore, be it 13    RESOLVED, BY THE HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES OF THE ONE 14HUNDRED FOURTH GENERAL ASSEMBLY OF THE STATE OF ILLINOIS, that 15we commend the Tribute to Valor Foundation and its mission to 16inspire the youth of America and all people to ask themselves 17the question "Who Am I?" and to answer by reflecting on the six 18core values of the Medal of Honor.
