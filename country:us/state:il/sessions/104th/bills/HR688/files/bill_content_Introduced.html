2    WHEREAS, The E-15 fuel blend utilizes a combination of 
3renewable ethanol and gasoline to enhance the longevity of our 
4nation's fuel supply, while maintaining the common and 
5everyday usage of motor vehicles; and
 
6    WHEREAS, The use of corn to produce ethanol for motor fuel 
7is a proven source of renewable, clean, and sustainable energy

9Illinoisan; and
 
10    WHEREAS, The ethanol by-products that are produced during 
11the distillation of ethanol for motor fuel are recyclable, 
12proving beneficial by turning waste products into a valuable, 
13renewable, and sustainable resource; and
 
14    WHEREAS, Both wet and dry distillers' grains are often 
15used in high-protein livestock feed; the carbon dioxide 
16created during the fermentation process is then captured and 
17used for carbonation in the food and beverage industry, for 
18fire extinguishers, and in greenhouses; and
 
19    WHEREAS, Illinois is the second largest corn-producing

21U.S. corn production; the various uses of corn not only as a
