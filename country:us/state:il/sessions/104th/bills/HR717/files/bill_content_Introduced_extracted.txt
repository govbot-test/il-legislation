and be it further 5    RESOLVED, That we encourage and support all efforts to 6bolster the history, heritage, and cultural organizations that 7preserve and protect our State's archives, museums, libraries, 8historic sites, and community collections, ensuring broad 9public access to Illinois' diverse histories and strengthening 10opportunities for education, research, and civic engagement 11for present and future generations; and be it further 12    RESOLVED, That we support efforts to expand access to 13high-quality civics education that prepares individuals for 14thoughtful, constructive participation in our democracy with 15an understanding of the nation's founding ideals and the 16diverse people and communities in Illinois who have engaged, 17and continue to engage, in the work required to realize them 18fully for our residents and all residents of the states and 19territories of the United States of America.
