HOUSE RESOLUTION

 
2    WHEREAS, The members of the Illinois House of 
3Representatives are saddened to learn of the death of Patrick 
4Cassidy "Pat" Finn, who passed away on December 22, 2025; and
 
5    WHEREAS, Pat Finn, the son of Leo and Betty, was born in 
6Wilmette on July 31, 1965; he graduated from Loyola Academy 
7and Marquette University; he married the love of his life 
8Donna Crowley, who was raised in Springfield; and
 
9    WHEREAS, Pat Finn studied and performed at Second City in 
10Chicago, and he toured with the Second City Touring Company; 
11after moving to Los Angeles, he became a founding member of the 
12renowned iO West improv troupe, Beer Shark Mice; he also 
13served as an adjunct professor and taught Improv for Strategic 
14Communication at the University of Colorado, where he coached, 
15befriended, and mentored countless students throughout the 
16years; and
 
17    WHEREAS, Pat Finn's first big television role was as 
18George Wendt's brother on The George Wendt Show; he had 
19recurring roles on Murphy Brown, 3rd Rock from the Sun, Ed, and 
20My Boys; he became known for his roles as Joe Mayo on Seinfeld 
21and Dr. Roger on Friends, and he played the character Bill 
22Norwood on the ABC sitcom The Middle from 2010 to 2018; he also
