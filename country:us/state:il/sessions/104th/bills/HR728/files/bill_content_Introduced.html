2    WHEREAS, The members of the Illinois House of

5    WHEREAS, Leona Walker Davis was born on March 1, 1926 in 
6Climax, Georgia; she enrolled at Oakton College in 1975 and, 
7through determination and hard work, earned an associate's 
8degree in nursing in 1981; and
 
9    WHEREAS, Leona Walker Davis devoted 21 years to the 
10nursing profession, providing compassionate care to patients 
11at Skokie Valley Hospital before retiring in 1989; and
 
12    WHEREAS, Leona Walker Davis was married to Lester Davis, 
13who served on the board of the American-West Indian 
14Association, and together they exemplified a shared dedication 
15to community involvement and civic engagement; and
 
16    WHEREAS, Throughout her life, Leona Walker Davis has given 
17generously of her time and talents to her community, serving 
18as a board member of the Council for Human Services for the 
19Village of Skokie and on the Board of Sister Cities, as a 
20hostess for the Skokie Valley Opera chapter, and as a 
21volunteer for the Salvation Army; and
