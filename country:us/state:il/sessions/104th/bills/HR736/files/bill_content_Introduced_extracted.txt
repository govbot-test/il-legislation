and 17    WHEREAS, In 2001, Arise Chicago produced the first 18Workers' Rights Manual with easy-to-read language in Spanish, 19Polish, and English, outlining Illinois-specific labor 20protections; it became a model replicated across the country 21and continues to be provided as a free public resource; and   HR0736- 2 -LRB104 20707 RMO 34207 r1    WHEREAS, Shortly after producing its Workers' Rights 2Manual, Arise Chicago opened a Worker Center that became a 3vital space for worker education, advocacy, and organizing;
