and 16    WHEREAS, The Illinois Alliance of Boys & Girls Clubs 17serves approximately 75,000 youth in 51 communities statewide 18through its member organizations, providing safe and 19supportive environments during critical out-of-school hours 20that help keep children safe while offering academic support, 21literacy programming, and mentorship that strengthens early 22learning and educational outcomes; and   HR0745- 2 -LRB104 21222 ECR 35810 r1    WHEREAS, The Illinois Alliance of Boys & Girls Clubs and 2its member organizations have served more than 1,691,325 meals 3and snacks to young people, and with the State's past 4investment, these clubs previously provided almost 10,000 5additional youth a place to belong in 2024; and 6    WHEREAS, The impact of the Illinois Alliance of Boys & 7Girls Clubs' member organizations on academic performance and 8personal and professional achievement is profound, with 95% of 9club youth in Illinois expecting to graduate high school and 1091% planning to go to college; and 11    WHEREAS, Investing in the Illinois Alliance of Boys & 12Girls Clubs represents a strong partnership between the State 13of Illinois, local communities, families, and the private 14sector, as expanding access to out-of-school time programming 15supports working families, strengthens communities, and helps 16ensure that every young person has access to the opportunities 17they need to learn, grow, and succeed; and 18    WHEREAS, Continued investment and support for the Illinois 19Alliance of Boys & Girls Clubs is essential to the health, 20safety, academic success, and future prosperity of our State's 21young people and the communities they call home; and   HR0745- 3 -LRB104 21222 ECR 35810 r1    WHEREAS, On March 24, 2026, the Illinois Alliance of Boys &2 Girls Clubs and its member organizations plan to visit 3Springfield to advocate for Illinois' young people and to 4demonstrate the statewide commitment to youth development that 5the clubs embody; therefore, be it 6    RESOLVED, BY THE HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES OF THE ONE 7HUNDRED FOURTH GENERAL ASSEMBLY OF THE STATE OF ILLINOIS, that 8we commend the Illinois Alliance of Boys & Girls Clubs and its 9member organizations, staff, and volunteers for their 10dedication to providing safe, accessible, and enriching 11programming that helps youth in communities across Illinois 12achieve their full potential; and be it further 13    RESOLVED, That a suitable copy of this resolution be 14presented to the Illinois Alliance of Boys & Girls Clubs as a 15symbol of our esteem and respect.
