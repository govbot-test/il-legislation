and 6    WHEREAS, Sunil Puri, born in Mumbai, India, came to 7Rockford, Illinois in 1979, where he attended Rockford 8College, now Rockford University, beginning a journey defined 9by determination, entrepreneurship, and service to his 10community; and 11    WHEREAS, Sunil Puri founded First Midwest Group in 1984, 12which became a driving force in the economic development of 13Rockford, helping shape the city through commercial, 14residential, and retail developments that created jobs and 15expanded opportunity; and 16    WHEREAS, Sunil Puri believed deeply in education and 17opportunity, supporting numerous initiatives, including the 18creation of the Puri Business School at Rockford University, 19cultivating future generations of leaders; and 20    WHEREAS, Sunil Puri's commitment to community extended 21beyond business through philanthropy and civic leadership that   HR0747- 2 -LRB104 21207 LAW 35720 r1strengthened organizations, supported residents, and enhanced 2the quality of life throughout the Rockford Region; and 3    WHEREAS, Sunil Puri was instrumental in establishing the 4Keeling-Puri Peace Plaza, a place dedicated to unity and 5understanding among people of all nations and backgrounds, 6reflecting his belief in the power of community and shared 7purpose; and 8    WHEREAS, Sunil Puri's legacy will endure not only through 9the developments and institutions he helped create but also 10through the countless lives he uplifted with his generosity, 11vision, and willingness to invest in the future of others; 12therefore, be it 13    RESOLVED, BY THE HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES OF THE ONE 14HUNDRED FOURTH GENERAL ASSEMBLY OF THE STATE OF ILLINOIS, that 15we mourn the passing of Sunil Puri and extend our sincere 16condolences to his family, friends, and all who knew and loved 17him; and be it further 18    RESOLVED, That a suitable copy of this resolution be 19presented to the family of Sunil Puri as an expression of our 20deepest sympathy.
