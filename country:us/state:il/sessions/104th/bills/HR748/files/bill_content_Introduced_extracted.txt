Full Text of HR0748 Illinois General Assembly ILGA.GOV HR0748 - 104th General Assembly HR0748LRB104 21012 ECR 34905 r1HOUSE RESOLUTION 2    WHEREAS, The advent of SoyFoam provides an easily 3renewable and non-toxic alternative to current foams used for 4firefighting; and 5    WHEREAS, SoyFoam is a natural product grown in the United 6States that does not contain per- and polyfluoroalkyl 7substances (PFAS), which are found in current foams and are 8hazardous to humans and wildlife, with exposure to PFAS being 9linked to thousands of cancer diagnoses every year; and 10    WHEREAS, SoyFoam is also free of fluorine, a toxic 11chemical that can cause pulmonary edema, a potentially deadly 12condition in humans, and liver and kidney damage in animals 13upon exposure; and 14    WHEREAS, SoyFoam is capable of extinguishing Class A and 15Class B fires caused by ordinary combustibles and flammable 16liquids, as SoyFoam has passed the UL-162 test for 17effectiveness; and 18    WHEREAS, SoyFoam is also a biodegradable substance, as 19SoyFoam has been certified as 84% biobased by the U.S. 20Department of Agriculture and has proven itself in tests by 21extinguishing jet fuel and gasoline burns; and   HR0748- 2 -LRB104 21012 ECR 34905 r1    WHEREAS, SoyFoam is also compatible with current foam 2inductors and aerating nozzles used by fire departments, 3meaning the transition would not require new equipment, and 4the cost for SoyFoam is also competitive with current fire 5extinguishing foams; and 6    WHEREAS, In 2025, Illinois was the number one producer of 7soybeans in the United States; the adoption of SoyFoam would 8create an increased demand and fixed market for soybeans, 9which would support Illinois farmers for the foreseeable 10future; therefore, be it 11    RESOLVED, BY THE HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES OF THE ONE 12HUNDRED FOURTH GENERAL ASSEMBLY OF THE STATE OF ILLINOIS, that 13we urge State Fire Marshal Michele Pankow and local fire 14departments to replace current foams with the 15non-carcinogenic, non-toxic, environmentally friendly 16SoyFoam; and be it further 17    RESOLVED, That suitable copies of this resolution be 18delivered to Illinois State Fire Marshal Michele Pankow for 19distribution to fire chiefs across the State of Illinois.
