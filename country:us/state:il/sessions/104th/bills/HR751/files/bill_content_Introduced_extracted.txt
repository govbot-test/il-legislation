and 5    WHEREAS, George Edward Ludkowski, a constituent of the 677th State House District in Illinois, is remembered as a 7devoted husband, father, and friend, whose warmth and presence 8left a lasting impression on those who knew him, and whose life 9continues to be honored across communities in Illinois; and 10    WHEREAS, He was married to his beloved wife, Lili 11Ludkowski, for 46 years, and together they built a life 12centered on family, love, and community; and 13    WHEREAS, As a working-class man, he deeply valued 14education and the importance of giving back, dedicating his 15time to coaching T-ball and volunteering with a children's 16bowling league, where he positively impacted the lives of many 17young people; and 18    WHEREAS, His tragic passing, resulting from a choking 19incident at a restaurant, underscored the urgent need for 20greater awareness, preparedness, and access to life-saving 21tools in public settings; and   HR0751- 2 -LRB104 21265 RMO 35960 r1    WHEREAS, Choking causes over 5,000 deaths annually in the 2United States, with adults over age 75 accounting for 3two-thirds of these incidents, and is the fourth leading cause 4of unintentional injury death; and 5    WHEREAS, In the wake of this loss, his wife has become a 6dedicated advocate for choking prevention awareness, working 7to educate the public and support efforts to prevent similar 8tragedies; and 9    WHEREAS, National Choking Prevention Awareness Day, 10observed annually on March 28, provides an important 11opportunity to promote education, prevention, and emergency 12preparedness; therefore, be it 13    RESOLVED, BY THE HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES OF THE ONE 14HUNDRED FOURTH GENERAL ASSEMBLY OF THE STATE OF ILLINOIS, that 15we honor the life and legacy of George Edward Ludkowski, whose 16story continues to inspire awareness and advocacy; and be it 17further 18    RESOLVED, That a suitable copy of this resolution be 19presented to the family of George Ludkowski as a symbol of our 20respect and remembrance.
