and 5    WHEREAS, On this day, the Greek community celebrates the 6bravery of its ancestors in their struggles and their 7relentless determination to ensure the survival of Greek 8heritage, culture, and religion; and 9    WHEREAS, In 1453, with the seizure of Constantinople, 10Greece unwillingly became part of the Ottoman Empire; for 400 11years thereafter, the Greek people were forced to relinquish 12their customs, language, traditions, and most importantly, 13their freedom; while many Greeks fled to Western Europe, 14countless others who were unable to flee were subjected to 15many forms of oppression; and 16    WHEREAS, On March 25, 1821, Bishop Germanos of Patras 17raised the flag of revolution at the Monastery of Agia Lavra 18and declared, "Eleftheria I Thanatos", meaning "Freedom or 19Death", which became the cry of the War of Greek Independence; 20this single act is credited with inciting the people of Greece 21to unite together to stage a revolution and defeat the Ottoman 22Empire; and   HR0755- 2 -LRB104 21260 LAW 35952 r1    WHEREAS, While the Greek armies won many battles, they 2were in danger of losing the War of Greek Independence by 1827; 3it was then that Great Britain, France, and Russia interceded, 4assisting the Greeks with overpowering the Ottoman forces; and 5    WHEREAS, On September 14, 1829, the War of Greek 6Independence ended with the signing of the Treaty of Edirne, 7which is also known as the Treaty of Adrianople; with the 8signing of the Treaty of Edirne, the Ottoman Empire was forced 9to recognize Greece as an autonomous state; and 10    WHEREAS, In observance of Greek Independence Day, special 11events are held around the world, including parades, military 12processions, speeches, and festivals; and 13    WHEREAS, The contributions of Greek culture on modern 14civilizations are innumerable, influencing the world in every 15way imaginable, from the fields of mathematics, astronomy, 16science, and medicine to literature, theater, philosophy, and 17democracy; therefore, be it 18    RESOLVED, BY THE HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES OF THE ONE 19HUNDRED FOURTH GENERAL ASSEMBLY OF THE STATE OF ILLINOIS, that 20we declare March 25, 2026 as Greek Independence Day in the 21State of Illinois.
