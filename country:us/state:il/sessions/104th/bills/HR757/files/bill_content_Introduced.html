HOUSE RESOLUTION

 
2    WHEREAS, The members of the Illinois House of 
3Representatives are deeply saddened to learn of the passing of 
4Delores Showalter, who passed away on February 26, 2026; and
 
5    WHEREAS, Delores Showalter, the daughter of Frank and 
6Dortha (Geer) Showalter, was born on July 8, 1944; she lived 
7most of her life in Fancy Prairie before moving to Sherman; and
 
8    WHEREAS, Delores Showalter worked for the State of 
9Illinois for 35 years, serving in key leadership roles in four 
10state agencies and advocating passionately for women in state 
11government; and
 
12    WHEREAS, Following a distinguished career with the State 
13of Illinois, Delores Showalter continued making a difference 
14for families at the Hope Institute for Children and Families 
15in Springfield, where she served as chief human resources 
16officer from 2003 until her retirement in 2018; and
 
17    WHEREAS, Delores Showalter was instrumental in shaping the 
18Hope Institute as it is known today by helping build Hope 
19School's ten-year strategic plan and strengthening human 
20resources infrastructure at a critical time of growth, helping 
21to more fully realize the Hope Institute's mission on behalf
