and 5    WHEREAS, Delores Showalter, the daughter of Frank and 6Dortha (Geer) Showalter, was born on July 8, 1944; she lived 7most of her life in Fancy Prairie before moving to Sherman; and 8    WHEREAS, Delores Showalter worked for the State of 9Illinois for 35 years, serving in key leadership roles in four 10state agencies and advocating passionately for women in state 11government; and 12    WHEREAS, Following a distinguished career with the State 13of Illinois, Delores Showalter continued making a difference 14for families at the Hope Institute for Children and Families 15in Springfield, where she served as chief human resources 16officer from 2003 until her retirement in 2018; and 17    WHEREAS, Delores Showalter was instrumental in shaping the 18Hope Institute as it is known today by helping build Hope 19School's ten-year strategic plan and strengthening human 20resources infrastructure at a critical time of growth, helping 21to more fully realize the Hope Institute's mission on behalf   HR0757- 2 -LRB104 21275 ECR 35989 r1of unique learners and special needs families; and 2    WHEREAS, Delores Showalter loved playing cards, sewing, 3and cross stitching in her free time, but she loved the company 4of family most of all; and 5    WHEREAS, Delores Showalter's life and work serve as 6powerful examples of how people who live in love and radiate 7compassion can touch countless lives; and 8    WHEREAS, Delores Showalter is survived by her sisters, 9Gail (David) Copi, Sharon (Steve) Mitts, and Diane (Bill) 10Hipshire; three nieces and six nephews; 11 great-nieces; and 11one great-great-nephew and two great-great-nieces; therefore, 12be it 13    RESOLVED, BY THE HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES OF THE ONE 14HUNDRED FOURTH GENERAL ASSEMBLY OF THE STATE OF ILLINOIS, that 15we mourn the passing of Delores Showalter and extend our 16sincere condolences to her family, friends, and all who knew 17and loved her; and be it further 18    RESOLVED, That a suitable copy of this resolution be 19presented to the family of Delores Showalter as an expression 20of our deepest sympathy.
