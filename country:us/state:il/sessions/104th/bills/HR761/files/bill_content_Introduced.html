HOUSE RESOLUTION

 
2    WHEREAS, The members of the Illinois House of 
3Representatives are saddened to learn of the death of Mia 
4Webster-Cross, who passed away on March 4, 2026; and
 
5    WHEREAS, Mia Webster-Cross, the daughter of Nancy Lee and 
6Elmo Webster, was born on February 26, 1965; she attended 
7Kennedy-Coolidge Elementary School and Thornridge High School, 
8where she was a cheerleader, and earned a degree in licensed 
9practical nursing from Thornton Community College, now South 
10Suburban College; she completed her degree in registered 
11nursing and earned a Bachelor of Science in Nursing and a 
12Master of Science in Nursing; she later earned certifications 
13in diabetes care and massage therapy; she married Michael 
14Cross on September 7, 1991; and
 
15    WHEREAS, Mia Webster-Cross began her career at Ingalls 
16Memorial Hospital, where she worked as a registered nurse for 
17ten years; she joined Family Christian Health Center at its 
18inception and worked there for more than 20 years in a variety 
19of roles, including clinical support nurse, clinical 
20operations manager, and, at the time of her passing, chief 
21operating officer; and
 
22    WHEREAS, Mia Webster-Cross served her community through
