5    WHEREAS, The aviation pioneer Walter T. Varney began 
6providing air transport of mail service on April 6, 1926, and 
7this is considered one of the foundational pillars of modern 
8commercial air service; and
 
9    WHEREAS, Walter Varney, the proprietor of Varney Air 
10Service, was involved in a consolidation of various companies, 
11and those companies organized to become what is now known as 
12United Airlines; and
 
13    WHEREAS, Boeing Air Transport, a subsidiary that evolved 
14into the entity that became United Airlines, was the first to 
15employ a "flying nurse"; in 1930, Ellen Church, a licensed 
16pilot and nurse, was the founder of the "sky girls", women 
17hired to calm sick and frightened passengers; in addition to 
18providing nursing while onboard, the sky girls loaded luggage, 
19helped fuel the plane, and collected tickets; the sky girls 
20are the forerunners to today's flight attendants who are 
21essential safety professionals aboard every commercial flight;
