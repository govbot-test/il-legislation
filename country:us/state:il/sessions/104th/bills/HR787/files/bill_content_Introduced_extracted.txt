and 7    WHEREAS, On that historic day, with an unyielding 8commitment to the principles of freedom, sovereignty, and 9self-determination, the Republic of Kosova formally proclaimed 10its independence in Prishtina, marking a transformative moment 11in its history and establishing itself as a free and sovereign 12nation; and 13    WHEREAS, As the youngest country in Europe, the Republic 14of Kosova has demonstrated a steadfast commitment to 15democratic governance, the protection of human rights, and the 16rule of law;; and 17    WHEREAS, The enduring relationship between the United 18States and the Republic of Kosova is grounded in shared values 19of freedom and democracy, with the United States having played 20a pivotal role in supporting Kosova's path toward peace, 21freedom, and independence; and   HR0787- 2 -LRB104 20958 ECR 34781 r1    WHEREAS, The Albanian-American community in Illinois has 2made lasting contributions to the cultural, economic, and 3civic life of the State, enriching its communities while 4preserving and sharing a proud cultural heritage; and 5    WHEREAS, In 2026, the people of Kosova are commemorating 6the 18th anniversary of their independence, honoring the 7resilience, sacrifices, and aspirations that have shaped their 8nation; and 9    WHEREAS, It is important to recognize the growing 10partnerships and people-to-people ties between the United 11States and the Republic of Kosova as well as the important role 12of the Kosovar and Albanian diaspora in fostering mutual 13understanding and cooperation; therefore, be it 14    RESOLVED, BY THE HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES OF THE ONE 15HUNDRED FOURTH GENERAL ASSEMBLY OF THE STATE OF ILLINOIS, that 16we recognize and honor February 17, 2026 as the 18th 17anniversary of the Independence of the Republic of Kosova; and 18be it further 19    RESOLVED, That we extend our best wishes to the people of 20the Republic of Kosova and express our appreciation to the 21Albanian-American community for its contributions to the 22strength, diversity, and prosperity of the State of Illinois.
