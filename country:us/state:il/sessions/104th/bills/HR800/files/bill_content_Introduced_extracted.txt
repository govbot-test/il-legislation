and 19    WHEREAS, The Chicago Service Unit includes nearly 1,300 20total track miles, four intermodal facilities, and five 21freight terminals and operates within one of the nation's   HR0800- 2 -LRB104 21405 RMO 36395 r1busiest and most complex rail environments, supporting the 2safe and efficient movement of goods essential to the 3economies of the State of Illinois and the United States; and 4    WHEREAS, The performance of front-line craft 5professionals, managers, and leaders in the Chicago Service 6Unit reflects a shared commitment to safety and to ensuring 7that employees return home safely at the end of each day; 8therefore, be it 9    RESOLVED, BY THE HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES OF THE ONE 10HUNDRED FOURTH GENERAL ASSEMBLY OF THE STATE OF ILLINOIS, that 11we congratulate Union Pacific Railroad's Chicago Service Unit, 12General Manager Cerwin Fleming, and all team members on 13earning the 2025 Safety Bell Award;
