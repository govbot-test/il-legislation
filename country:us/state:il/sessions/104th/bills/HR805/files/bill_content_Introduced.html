7    WHEREAS, The Illinois Emergency Management Agency and

13    WHEREAS, IEMA-OHS carries out its mission through three 
14primary program areas, including the Office of Emergency 
15Management, which provides technical assistance and direct 
16support to emergency managers throughout Illinois, the Office 
17of Homeland Security, which administers federal homeland 
18security grant programs to local, state, and regional 
19partners, and the Office of Nuclear Safety, which oversees the 
20safe use and regulation of radioactive materials and works in 
21coordination with federal, state, and local partners to ensure 
22robust emergency preparedness around nuclear facilities and to 
23advance modern radiation safety practices; and
