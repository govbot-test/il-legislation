and 18    WHEREAS, Aman Yohannes has demonstrated exceptional 19achievement in Model United Nations, earning repeated 20recognition as an Outstanding Delegate at conferences and 21contributing to the success and development of fellow 22participants through mentorship and research support; and 6    WHEREAS, Aman Yohannes has also contributed to service 7initiatives and continues to pursue opportunities to deepen 8his understanding of global issues, expressing aspirations 9toward a future career in law, public policy, or foreign 10service;
