HOUSE RESOLUTION

 
2    WHEREAS, Water is an essential resource for sustaining 
3life, promoting public health, and ensuring economic 
4prosperity in Illinois; and
 
5    WHEREAS, Illinois is uniquely situated as the nucleus of 
6the great Midwest and can demonstrate stewardship in facing 
7ongoing challenges related to water, including lead in water, 
8infrastructure needs, and emerging water quality issues, which 
9require immediate and sustained attention; and
 
10    WHEREAS, The International Association of Plumbing and 
11Mechanical Officials (IAPMO) is allied with plumbers and 
12manufacturers in Illinois, playing a critical role in 
13addressing water-related challenges by advancing safe and 
14sustainable water systems through the education and advocacy 
15for clean drinking water; and
 
16    WHEREAS, March4Water Month will serve as a platform for 
17communities, organizations, and governmental agencies to raise 
18awareness about water conservation, equitable access to clean 
19and safe drinking water, and the importance of investing in 
20water infrastructure and sustainability efforts; and
 
21    WHEREAS, Community engagement and education are critical
