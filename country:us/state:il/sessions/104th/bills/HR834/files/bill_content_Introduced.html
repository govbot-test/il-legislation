HOUSE RESOLUTION

 
2    WHEREAS, The members of the Illinois House of 
3Representatives are saddened to learn of the death of Dr. 
4Angela Martin, a legendary gospel radio host, minister, and 
5pillar of faith in the Chicago community; and
 
6    WHEREAS, Dr. Martin's distinguished media career spanned 
718 years, during which time she lent her voice to Crawford 
8Broadcasting, Clear Channel, and iHeart Media; she was the 
9celebrated host of Sky High Praise on 106.3 WYBA, a fixture on 
10The John Hannah Morning Show on Inspiration 1390, and a 
11trusted traffic announcer for The Tom Joyner Morning Show on 
12WVAZ 102.7; and

17further honored by Delta Sigma Theta Sorority, Inc. with the 
18Echoes in Excellence Award in media and journalism; and
