2    WHEREAS, The members of the Illinois House of 
3Representatives are saddened to learn of the death of former 
4United States Representative Terry L. Bruce of Olney, who 
5passed away on April 17, 2026; and
 
6    WHEREAS, Rep. Bruce was born in Olney to Lloyd and Janie 
7(Skelton) Bruce on March 25, 1944; he attended the University 
8of Illinois at Urbana-Champaign and then later the University 
9of Illinois College of Law; he married Charlotte A. Bruce on 
10January 31, 1965; and
 
11    WHEREAS, Rep. Bruce worked at the United States Department 
12of Labor on issues related to farmworkers and on the staffs of 
13Congressman George Shipley and State Senators Philip Benefiel 
14and Tom McGloon; he practiced law at the Shumaker and Bruce Law 
15Office in Olney; and
 
16    WHEREAS, Rep. Bruce was elected to the Illinois State 
17Senate in 1970 and was part of the Democratic study group 
18referred to as the "Crazy 8"; he served as assistant majority 
19leader from 1975 to 1984; and
 
20    WHEREAS, Rep. Bruce served as U.S. Representative for the 
2119th Congressional District of Illinois from 1984 to 1992,
