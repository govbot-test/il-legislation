HOUSE RESOLUTION

 
2    WHEREAS, The members of the Illinois House of 
3Representatives wish to congratulate the legendary George 
4"Buddy" Guy on the momentous occasion of his 90th birthday on 
5July 30, 2026; and
 
6    WHEREAS, Buddy Guy was born in 1936 to a sharecropping 
7family on the Feduccia family's Three Rivers Landing 
8plantation in Lettsworth, Louisiana, and his journey from 
9humble beginnings to global icon embodies the American spirit; 
10at the age of seven, he fashioned his first guitar from a piece 
11of wood, his mother's hairpins, and screen door wire, a 
12testament to the ingenuity born of necessity; and
 
13    WHEREAS, Buddy Guy arrived in Chicago on September 25, 
141957 with only a guitar and the hope of a better life; though 
15he initially faced hunger and hardship, he was soon embraced 
16by the titans of the Windy City blues scene, including Muddy 
17Waters and Otis Rush, eventually becoming a foundational 
18session guitarist for the legendary Chess Records; and
 
19    WHEREAS, Buddy Guy is a pioneer of the West Side Sound, 
20characterized by high-energy electric riffs, frantic 
21showmanship, and a soul-stirring vocal wail; his innovative 
22use of distortion and feedback, accidentally discovered when a
