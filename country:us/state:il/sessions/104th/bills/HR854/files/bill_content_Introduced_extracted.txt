Full Text of HR0854 Illinois General Assembly ILGA.GOV HR0854 - 104th General Assembly HR0854LRB104 21702 RMO 37296 r1HOUSE RESOLUTION 2    WHEREAS, The members of the Illinois House of 3Representatives are deeply saddened to learn of the passing of 4Pete Bill Kastanes of Oak Lawn on April 25, 2026; and 5    WHEREAS, Pete Kastanes was born in Oak Lawn on October 4, 61963 and was raised in the South Shore, Roseland, and Ashburn 7neighborhoods; he was a proud graduate of Bogan High School 8and DeVry University; and 9    WHEREAS, After a successful career with American Express 10Travel, Pete Kastanes found his true calling as the founder 11and administrator of Vanished Chicagoland, a digital gold mine 12of history celebrating the city's past businesses, 13restaurants, and landmarks; and 14    WHEREAS, Pete Kastanes amassed a following of more than a 15quarter of a million people through his website, podcasts, and 16social media presence, creating a vibrant community where 17Chicagoans could share memories of pizzerias, toy stores, and 18concert halls; and 19    WHEREAS, Pete Kastanes recorded over 500 podcast episodes, 20using his nearly photographic memory to transport listeners 21back to the Chicago of their youth; and   HR0854- 2 -LRB104 21702 RMO 37296 r1    WHEREAS, Pete Kastanes received the Chicago History 2Museum's Citizen Historian Award in February 2025 for making 3history available to everyone and fostering a respectful, 4nostalgia-driven community; and 5    WHEREAS, Pete Kastanes was a man of the people whose zest 6for life, sense of humor, and passion for storytelling will be 7missed by all who knew him; and 8    WHEREAS, Pete Kastanes was the cherished son of Zoe and 9the late Christ Kastanes; the proud brother of Bill and John; 10and a dear nephew and cousin to many; therefore, be it 11    RESOLVED, BY THE HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES OF THE ONE 12HUNDRED FOURTH GENERAL ASSEMBLY OF THE STATE OF ILLINOIS, that 13we mourn the passing of Pete Bill Kastanes and extend our 14sincere condolences to his family, friends, and the many 15followers of Vanished Chicagoland; and be it further 16    RESOLVED, That a suitable copy of this resolution be 17presented to the family of Pete Kastanes as an expression of 18our deepest sympathy.
