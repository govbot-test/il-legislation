Full Text of HR0870 Illinois General Assembly ILGA.GOV HR0870 - 104th General Assembly HR0870LRB104 21723 LAW 37343 r1HOUSE RESOLUTION 2    WHEREAS, The members of the Illinois House of 3Representatives wish to commend Our Savior Lutheran Church's 4Breakfast with Baby, a faith-based ministry, for serving 5families with children up to 36 months old in Carol Stream and 6the surrounding communities of DuPage and Kane counties; and 7    WHEREAS, Breakfast with Baby began in October 2019, with 8the program experiencing significant growth in 2020 during the 9COVID-19 pandemic, supporting an average of 110 families per 10month; and 11    WHEREAS, Breakfast with Baby is made possible by a 12dedicated team of volunteers and generous community donations 13that enable the ministry to provide essential physical and 14spiritual care to economically disadvantaged families; and 15    WHEREAS, On the third Saturday of each month, Breakfast 16with Baby provides a healthy breakfast to participating 17families along with diapers, baby wipes, clothing, and other 18necessary items; and 19    WHEREAS, Breakfast with Baby also distributes new and 20gently used baby items to help young parents meet their daily 21needs; and   HR0870- 2 -LRB104 21723 LAW 37343 r1    WHEREAS, In 2025 alone, Breakfast with Baby supported 21,303 local families and provided more than 74,000 diapers at 3no cost; and 4    WHEREAS, Through its mission to share the Lutheran faith's 5love and strengthen growing families, Breakfast with Baby 6continues to build a healthier and more connected community; 7therefore, be it 8    RESOLVED, BY THE HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES OF THE ONE 9HUNDRED FOURTH GENERAL ASSEMBLY OF THE STATE OF ILLINOIS, that 10we commend Our Savior Lutheran Church's Breakfast with Baby 11ministry for its outstanding service to families in DuPage and 12Kane counties; and be it further 13    RESOLVED, That a suitable copy of this resolution be 14presented to the leadership of Breakfast with Baby as an 15expression of our respect and appreciation.
