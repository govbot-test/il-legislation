and 18    WHEREAS, Depositories in the IRAD system are staffed with 19graduate student interns who are provided with training in 20basic archival principles and research skills; and   HR0879- 2 -LRB104 21698 RMO 37289 r1    WHEREAS, The IRAD system has served local government 2agencies by providing safe and secure storage for their 3permanently valuable records; and 4    WHEREAS, The IRAD system has provided reference services 5to thousands of individuals, including scholars, family 6historians, lawyers, historical societies, and journalists; 7therefore, be it 8    RESOLVED, BY THE HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES OF THE ONE 9HUNDRED FOURTH GENERAL ASSEMBLY OF THE STATE OF ILLINOIS, that 10we congratulate the Illinois State Archives and its Illinois 11Regional Archives Depository (IRAD) system on providing 50 12years of outstanding service;
