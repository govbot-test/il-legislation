HOUSE RESOLUTION

 
2    WHEREAS, The members of the Illinois House of 
3Representatives wish to congratulate Dr. Charlotte Warren on 
4her retirement from Lincoln Land Community College; and
 
5    WHEREAS, Dr. Warren has served as the president of Lincoln 
6Land Community College since 2006, becoming the 
7longest-serving community college president in Illinois 
8history, in addition to also breaking new ground as the 
9school's first female president; and
 
10    WHEREAS, Dr. Warren's visionary leadership of Lincoln Land 
11Community College has expanded not only the realm of academic 
12partnerships for the school through expanded nursing, 
13agricultural, workforce training, and professional skill 
14enhancement programs but also the physical space as well; and
 
15    WHEREAS, Dr. Warren oversaw the transformative 
16construction of five new facilities and the renovation of nine 
17buildings across Lincoln Land's 15 county districts; and
 
18    WHEREAS, Dr. Warren's direction helped Lincoln Land 
19Community College see sustained multi-year enrollment growth, 
20achieving a nearly 26% increase in opening headcount from the 
21fall of 2021 to the fall of 2025, while maintaining a
