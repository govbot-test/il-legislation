HOUSE RESOLUTION

 
2    WHEREAS, The members of the Illinois House of 
3Representatives wish to congratulate the Illinois Department 
4of Natural Resources on the occasion of the 60th anniversary 
5of the dedication of Kankakee River Nature Preserve; and
 
6    WHEREAS, The Illinois Natural Areas Preservation Act 
7created a system for preserving and protecting areas of the 
8State as a part of the heritage of the people of Illinois; and
 
9    WHEREAS, Illinois Nature Preserves are of value for 
10scientific research, for teaching, as reservoirs of natural 
11materials not all of the potential uses of which are now known, 
12as habitats for rare and vanishing species, as places of 
13historic and natural interest and scenic beauty and as living 
14museums of the native landscape wherein one may envision and 
15experience primeval conditions in a wilderness-like 
16environment; and
 
17    WHEREAS, Nature preserves contribute generally to the 
18public health and welfare and the environmental quality of the 
19State; and
 
20    WHEREAS, The Kankakee River Nature Preserve, located in 
21Kankakee County, was officially established in 1966 to protect

1and conserve its diverse natural communities, including upland 
2forest, bottomland forest, and sand hill areas representative 
3of the Grand Prairie Natural Division; and
 
4    WHEREAS, The Kankakee River Nature Preserve is recognized 
5for its globally significant natural features, most notably 
6Langham Island, the only place in the world where Kankakee 
7mallow grows in the wild, making it an important natural area 
8within northeastern Illinois; and
 
9    WHEREAS, The Kankakee River Nature Preserve provides 
10critical habitat for a diverse array of plant and animal 
11species, including more than 500 surveyed plant species on 
12Langham Island alone, such as the rare wild stonecrop, the 
13rare bulblet fern, and the federally endangered leafy prairie 
14clover, which was discovered on the island last summer for the 
15first time since 1873; and
 
16    WHEREAS, The Kankakee River Nature Preserve offers 
17opportunities for nature observation, birdwatching, and 
18environmental education, allowing residents and visitors to 
19experience one of northeastern Illinois' most ecologically 
20significant natural areas along the Kankakee River; and
 
21    WHEREAS, The stewardship of the Kankakee River Nature 
22Preserve has been supported by dedicated local partners,

15conservation, education, and stewardship; and be it further
