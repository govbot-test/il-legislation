HOUSE RESOLUTION

 
2    WHEREAS, The International Council of Museums (ICOM) 
3established International Museum Day in 1977 to increase 
4public awareness of the vital role museums play in the 
5development of society; and
 
6    WHEREAS, Museums are incomparable places of discovery that 
7teach us about our past and open our minds to new ideas, 
8serving as essential pillars in building a better future; and
 
9    WHEREAS, Museums worldwide will celebrate International 
10Museum Day on May 18, 2026 under the theme "Museums Uniting a 
11Divided World", highlighting the potential of these 
12institutions to act as bridges across cultural, social, and 
13geopolitical divides; and
 
14    WHEREAS, Museums serve as trusted public spaces where 
15people encounter diverse stories and objects, fostering 
16dialogue, understanding, inclusion, and peace in times of 
17social fragmentation and polarization; and
 
18    WHEREAS, Illinois is home to a vast array of world-class 
19museums that safeguard our heritage and memory, offering 
20learning and reflection while providing welcoming spaces where 
21diverse voices can be heard; and
