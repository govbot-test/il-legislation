Full Text of HR0892 Illinois General Assembly ILGA.GOV HR0892 - 104th General Assembly HR0892LRB104 21841 MST 37632 r1HOUSE RESOLUTION 2    WHEREAS, The members of the Illinois House of 3Representatives are saddened to mark the death of Murod Kurdi 4of Oak Lawn, who passed away on June 5, 2023, at the age of 28; 5and 6    WHEREAS, Murod Kurdi was a beloved son, brother, and 7member of the Oak Lawn community; and 8    WHEREAS, Murod Kurdi was a lifelong student of the Oak 9Lawn public school system where his mother worked and 10volunteered, contributing to the well-being of thousands of 11students; and 12    WHEREAS, Murod Kurdi was a Palestinian American, belonging 13to the largest diaspora of Palestinians in the United States, 14which is here in the State of Illinois; and 15    WHEREAS, Murod Kurdi lost his life tragically in an 16alcohol-involved traffic accident when he was struck by a 17vehicle while standing outside his home near 91st Street and 18Tulley Avenue in Oak Lawn; and 19    WHEREAS, According to the Illinois Department of 20Transportation's 2024 Illinois Crash Facts & Statistics   HR0892- 2 -LRB104 21841 MST 37632 r1report, there were 1,085 fatal crashes statewide in 2024, and 220.4% involved alcohol; and 3    WHEREAS, Murod Kurdi was deeply loved by his family and 4community, whose grief was met with an outpouring of support 5from neighbors, activists, and fellow residents who rallied in 6his honor to call for justice; and 7    WHEREAS, Murod Kurdi's passing has left an irreplaceable 8void in the lives of all who knew and loved him; and 9    WHEREAS, Murod Kurdi is survived by his mother, Fadia 10Muhamad, his brother, Suphi Kurdi, and other cherished family 11members; therefore, be it 12    RESOLVED, BY THE HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES OF THE ONE 13HUNDRED FOURTH GENERAL ASSEMBLY OF THE STATE OF ILLINOIS, that 14we mourn the passing of Murod Kurdi and extend our sincere 15condolences to his family, friends, and all who knew and loved 16him; and be it further 17    RESOLVED, That a suitable copy of this resolution be 18presented to the family of Murod Kurdi as an expression of our 19deepest sympathy.
