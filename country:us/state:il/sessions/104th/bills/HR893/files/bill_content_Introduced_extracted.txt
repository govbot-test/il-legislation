and 6    WHEREAS, Those suffering from POTS may also have 7additional medical problems, including digestion problems such 8as feeling or being sick, diarrhea, constipation, bloating and 9stomach pain, headaches and problems with sight such as 10blurred vision or tunnel vision, hands and feet looking 11purple, weakness and extreme fatigue, and problems with 12thinking, memory, and concentration;
