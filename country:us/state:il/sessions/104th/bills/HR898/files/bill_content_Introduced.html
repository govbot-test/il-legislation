HOUSE RESOLUTION

 
2    WHEREAS, The members of the Illinois House of 
3Representatives are saddened to learn of the death of David 
4Lynn Walter, who passed away on May 2, 2026; and
 
5    WHEREAS, David Walter, the son of Joseph August and Lillie 
6Belva (Epps) Walter, was born in Edwardsville on May 27, 1936; 
7he earned a Bachelor of Science and a Master of Science in 
8Music Education with high honors at Eastern Illinois 
9University and received administrative credentials from 
10Northwestern University; and
 
11    WHEREAS, David Walter began his 34-year music teaching 
12career as the choral director of Lincoln High School and 
13Lincoln College; he subsequently taught music at Glenbrook 
14North High School in north suburban Chicago for 33 years, 
15serving as choral director, instructional supervisor, and 
16district co-manager for school restructuring; he was elected 
17president of the Glenbrook Educators' Association and was a 
18member of the national test development committee for Advanced 
19Placement Music prior to his retirement in 1994; and
 
20    WHEREAS, David Walter sang as the principal second tenor 
21of the Chicago Symphony Chorus, where he was appointed 
22apprentice conductor and was honored as a scholarship student
