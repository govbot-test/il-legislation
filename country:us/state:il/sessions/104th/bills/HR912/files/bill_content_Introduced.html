2    WHEREAS, The Italian beef sandwich, comprised of thinly

8    WHEREAS, The Italian beef sandwich traces its origins to

10the early 20th century, where resourceful families stretched 
11affordable cuts of beef by slow-roasting them with herbs and 
12spices and slicing them paper-thin, a tradition of ingenuity 
13and community that reflects the enduring character of 
14Illinois; and

19commerce; and
