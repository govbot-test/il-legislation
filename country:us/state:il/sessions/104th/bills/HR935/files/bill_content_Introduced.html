2    WHEREAS, The members of the Illinois House of 
3Representatives are saddened to learn of the death of Allan 
4Mellis, who passed away on April 26, 2026; and
 
5    WHEREAS, For more than 50 years, Allan Mellis, known as 
6"The Mayor of Lincoln Park", served his community in Chicago's 
7Lincoln Park neighborhood as president and board member of the 
8Wrightwood Neighbors Association; and
 
9    WHEREAS, Allan Mellis advocated strongly for the 
10development, safety, and growth of Lincoln Park, one of the 
11densest neighborhoods in Chicago, bringing together various 
12community members' interests behind a common goal of improving 
13their neighborhood; and
 
14    WHEREAS, Allan Mellis played a key role in launching Taste 
15of Lincoln Avenue, which grew to become one of Chicago's most 
16iconic food festivals and neighborhood events; and
 
17    WHEREAS, When the Lincoln Park branch of the Chicago 
18Public Library faced a threat of closure, Allan Mellis 
19organized his community and helped not only save but expand 
20the library to a new location on Fullerton and Racine, which is 
21now among the city's busiest; and
