and 20    WHEREAS, The ladies of the Stonewall Stitchers have 21produced and delivered almost 20,000 robes and quilts since   HR0938- 2 -LRB104 22103 ECR 38275 r1they first began, with material, yarn, and batting being 2donated by many residents of the community, who have even 3donated sewing machines to assist in the project; and 4    WHEREAS, The members of the Stonewall Stitchers who 5attended the last meeting were DeAnn Anderson, Sandra Elliott, 6Vi Johns, Maryellen Kinney, Sue Mangus, Sue Thurman, and Pat 7Wasmund; therefore, be it 8    RESOLVED, BY THE HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES OF THE ONE 9HUNDRED FOURTH GENERAL ASSEMBLY OF THE STATE OF ILLINOIS, that 10we commend the members of the Stonewall Stitchers for their 11long-standing service, and we thank them for their dedication 12and contributions to the community; and be it further 13    RESOLVED, That a suitable copy of this resolution be 14presented to the Stonewall Stitchers as symbol of our esteem 15and respect.
