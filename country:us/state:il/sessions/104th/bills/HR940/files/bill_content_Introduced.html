HOUSE RESOLUTION

 
2    WHEREAS, The members of the Illinois House of 
3Representatives wish to congratulate James Andrew "Andy" Busch 
4on the occasion of his retirement as pastor at Alpha Baptist 
5Church after 34 years of service at the age of 70; and
 
6    WHEREAS, Andy Busch has been a pillar of the community for 
7decades, supporting and organizing events at hospitals and 
8churches, officiating numerous weddings, and serving as 
9chaplain for ten funerals; and
 
10    WHEREAS, Andy Busch was born in Duluth, Minnesota on 
11January 18, 1956; he attended Green Bay East High School in 
12Green Bay, Wisconsin, graduating in 1974; he then studied at 
13the University of Wisconsin-Green Bay, graduating in 1979, and 
14the Bethel Theological Seminary in Arden Hills, Minnesota, 
15graduating in 1983; after completing his education, he moved 
16several times before eventually settling in Galesburg, 
17Illinois in 2007; and
 
18    WHEREAS, Andy Busch became a full-time chaplain at the 
19United Medical Center on August 20, 1990, retiring from that 
20position on January 31, 2025; his service in hospitals 
21included improving mental health and addiction care through 
22music and spiritual teaching, facilitating community grief
