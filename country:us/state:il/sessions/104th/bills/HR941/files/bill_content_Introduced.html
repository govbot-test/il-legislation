HOUSE RESOLUTION

 
2    WHEREAS, The members of the Illinois House of 
3Representatives are saddened to learn of the death of Bishop 
4George Lee Dowell of Danville, who passed away on May 11, 2026 
5at the age of 78; and
 
6    WHEREAS, Bishop Dowell was born in Atoka, Tennessee to 
7D.C. and Alberta C. Dowell on August 7, 1947; he graduated from 
8Danville High School, where he was a star in football, 
9basketball, and track; he served in the U.S. Navy and was 
10honorably discharged on October 18, 1972; he married Shanae 
11Camille Street on September 14, 1973; and
 
12    WHEREAS, Bishop Dowell returned to Danville and began his 
13career working at the Danville Veterans Administration Medical 
14Center as a nursing assistant and substance abuse counselor; 
15he later worked as a deputy sheriff, a supervisor at Wyman 
16Gordon, and an employee of the Danville Correctional Center, 
17from which he retired as third shift supervisor/captain; after 
18his retirement, he worked as a bailiff for the Vermilion 
19County Circuit Court of the 5th Judicial Court for 
20approximately 13 years; during his career, he returned to 
21school as an adult and earned an associate's degree in police 
22science; and
