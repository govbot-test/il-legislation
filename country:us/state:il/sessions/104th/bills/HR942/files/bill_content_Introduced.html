2    WHEREAS, The members of the Illinois House of 
3Representatives are saddened to learn of the death of Porter

5    WHEREAS, Porter McNeil, the son of Donald and Nancy 
6McNeil, was born in Moline on December 5, 1959; he graduated 
7from Moline High School in 1978; he received a bachelor's 
8degree from Macalester College in St. Paul, Minnesota and a 
9master's degree in Public Affairs Reporting from the 
10University of Illinois at Springfield; he married Mary Carlin 
11in Davenport, Iowa on September 23, 1989; and
 
12    WHEREAS, Porter McNeil worked as a public affairs and 
13communications consultant for dozens of political campaigns, 
14including serving as a communications consultant for the 
15Democratic Party of Illinois in 2024 and as Illinois 
16communications director for the Kerry-Edwards presidential 
17campaign in 2004; and
 
18    WHEREAS, Porter McNeil appeared on WHBF-TV's Sunday 
19political talk show 4 the Record 70 times since 2016, and he 
20also served on the Rock Island County Board; and
 
21    WHEREAS, Porter McNeil loved spending time at the family
