2    WHEREAS, The members of the Illinois House of 
3Representatives wish to congratulate the citizens of Rockford 
4and Ferentino, Italy on the occasion of the 20th anniversary 
5of their sister city partnership; and
 
6    WHEREAS, Rockford has a substantial and vibrant Italian 
7American population that celebrates its ancestral heritage, 
8including many families and individuals who trace their 
9ancestral roots to Ferentino, an ancient, pre-Roman era hill 
10town southeast of Rome; these families and their descendants 
11have grown to become an integral and cherished part of the 
12Rockford community, enriching its cultural, civic, and social 
13fabric for generations; and
 
14    WHEREAS, Since the late 1800s, thousands of immigrants 
15made the journey from Ferentino to Rockford, building homes, 
16businesses, churches, and institutions that have shaped the 
17identity of the city; their descendants, now numbering in the 
18thousands, remain a vibrant and active part of the Rockford 
19community, preserving Italian traditions while contributing to 
20the greater civic and cultural life of the region; and
 
21    WHEREAS, Immigrants to Rockford from Ferentino founded the 
22Society of Saint Ambrogio Martyr Mutual Benefit Society (Saint
