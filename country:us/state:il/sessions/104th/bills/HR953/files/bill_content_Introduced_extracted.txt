and 6    WHEREAS, Rockford has a substantial and vibrant Italian 7American population that celebrates its ancestral heritage, 8including many families and individuals who trace their 9ancestral roots to Ferentino, an ancient, pre-Roman era hill 10town southeast of Rome; these families and their descendants 11have grown to become an integral and cherished part of the 12Rockford community, enriching its cultural, civic, and social 13fabric for generations; and 14    WHEREAS, Since the late 1800s, thousands of immigrants 15made the journey from Ferentino to Rockford, building homes, 16businesses, churches, and institutions that have shaped the 17identity of the city; their descendants, now numbering in the 18thousands, remain a vibrant and active part of the Rockford 19community, preserving Italian traditions while contributing to 20the greater civic and cultural life of the region; and 21    WHEREAS, Immigrants to Rockford from Ferentino founded the 22Society of Saint Ambrogio Martyr Mutual Benefit Society (Saint   HR0953- 2 -LRB104 22126 LAW 38315 r1Ambrogio Society) in 1918, which is still in active operation 2today, standing as a testament to the enduring bonds between 3the two communities and the dedication of Ferentino-descended 4families to preserving their shared heritage; and 5    WHEREAS, Efforts to establish an Italian sister city to 6Rockford began in 2005 through family ties in both Rockford 7and Ferentino, leading to robust community efforts in both 8cities to formalize the lasting connections that have linked 9the people of Rockford and Ferentino for over a century; and 10    WHEREAS, The Italian Sister City Committee was formed in 11Rockford in 2005 to facilitate the ongoing communication 12between citizens and officials from Rockford and Ferentino to 13promote and support a sister city agreement between the two 14cities; and 15    WHEREAS, A delegation of nine public officials from the 16Ferentino City Council and the Pro Loco Association visited 17Rockford for a week in May 2006, where they took part in 18several official meetings and activities that culminated in 19the signing of a sister city agreement by Mayor Larry 20Morrissey of Rockford and Mayor Piergianni Fiorletta of 21Ferentino at Rockford City Hall on May 15, 2006; and 11    WHEREAS, The 20th anniversary of this historic partnership 12is a milestone that reflects 20 years of friendship, cultural 13exchange, and mutual respect between two communities bound by 14shared history, shared values, and the enduring spirit of 15their people, both in Italy and in Illinois; and be it further 11    RESOLVED, That suitable copies of this resolution be 12presented to the cities of Rockford and Ferentino and 13representatives of the Rockford Italian Sister Cities Alliance 14(RISCA) as an expression of our esteem and respect.
