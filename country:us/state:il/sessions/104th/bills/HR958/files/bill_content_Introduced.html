2    WHEREAS, The members of the Illinois House of 
3Representatives wish to congratulate Dave Hellmich, Ph.D. on 
4the occasion of his retirement from Sauk Valley Community 
5College after a distinguished career in higher education and 
6community leadership; and
 
7    WHEREAS, Dr. Hellmich served as president of Sauk Valley 
8Community College from July 2015 until his retirement as 
9president emeritus in June 2026, providing visionary 
10leadership and unwavering dedication to students, faculty, 
11staff, and the communities served by the college; and
 
12    WHEREAS, Prior to joining Sauk Valley Community College, 
13Dr. Hellmich devoted 28 years to higher education as an 
14administrator and English professor at community colleges in 
15Florida, Minnesota, and Kentucky, helping shape the 
16educational experiences and success of countless students, and 
17he taught graduate courses in ethical leadership in Minnesota 
18and Kentucky; and
 
19    WHEREAS, Dr. Hellmich served as editor and contributing 
20author of the books Ethical Leadership in Community Colleges: 
21Bridging Theory and Daily Practice and Ethical Decision-Making 
22in Community Colleges: Not Losing Our Way, helping guide
