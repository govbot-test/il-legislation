2    WHEREAS, The members of the Illinois House of 
3Representatives wish to congratulate the Special Recreation 
4Association of Central Lake County (SRACLC) on the occasion of 
5its 50th anniversary of providing exceptional, community-based 
6therapeutic recreation services to individuals with 
7disabilities and their families; and
 
8    WHEREAS, SRACLC began its vital mission in 1976 with a 
9summer day camp designed to bridge the gap between school 
10years, with the association first being formed under the name 
11Northeastern Illinois Special Recreation Association (NISRA) 
12before adapting its current name to avoid confusion with a 
13neighboring organization; and
 
14    WHEREAS, Over the past five decades, SRACLC has grown into 
15a respected leader and an indispensable cooperative alliance, 
16supported faithfully by its member agencies, including the 
17Grayslake Community Park District, the Village of 
18Libertyville, the Village of Hawthorn Woods, the Village of 
19Lake Zurich, the Village of Lincolnshire, the Mundelein Park 
20and Recreation District, the Round Lake Area Park District, 
21and the Vernon Hills Park District; and
 
22    WHEREAS, SRACLC provides critical inclusion services at no
