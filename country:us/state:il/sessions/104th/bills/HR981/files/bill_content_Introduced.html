5    WHEREAS, Associated Research is celebrating nine decades

8    WHEREAS, Associated Research traces its origins to 
9engineer James Inman founding the Chicago Meter & Repair 
10Laboratory in 1934 to focus on the calibration, repair, and 
11modification of analog panel meters; and
 
12    WHEREAS, Chicago Meter & Repair Laboratory was renamed 
13Associated Research in 1936, setting the stage for a legacy 
14rooted in engineering precision and customer service; and
 
15    WHEREAS, James Inman patented the Vibrotest, a 
16battery-operated insulation resistance tester, in 1939, and 
17the device's reliability led to its adoption as standard 
18equipment on United States Navy vessels during World War II; 
19and
