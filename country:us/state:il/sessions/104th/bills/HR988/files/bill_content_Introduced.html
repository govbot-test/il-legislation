HOUSE RESOLUTION

 
2    WHEREAS, The members of the Illinois House of 
3Representatives are saddened to learn of the death of Demetra 
4Rayvoir Banks, who passed away on June 25, 2026; and
 
5    WHEREAS, Demetra Banks was born in Texarkana, Arkansas to 
6James Sr. and Bessie Marie (English) Flint on January 17, 
71959; she attended Theron Jones Elementary School until fourth 
8grade, when she became one of the first students to integrate 
9the Liberty-Eylau Independent School District; her family 
10relocated to Chicago Heights in 1970, and she attended 
11Washington Junior High School; and
 
12    WHEREAS, Demetra Banks graduated from Bloom High School in 
131977, where she was on the cheer squad and sang with the Ellis 
14Franklin Singers, a community high school gospel choir; she 
15attended Elmhurst University, where she sang in the gospel 
16choir; she married Dwayne A. Banks Sr. on July 27, 1985, and 
17they settled in Peoria; and
 
18    WHEREAS, Demetra Banks was employed by the United States 
19Postal Service, where she dedicated more than 25 years of 
20service before retiring in February 2015; and
 
21    WHEREAS, Demetra Banks served the Peoria community through
