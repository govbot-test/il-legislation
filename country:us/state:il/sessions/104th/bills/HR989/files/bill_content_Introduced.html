HOUSE RESOLUTION

 
2    WHEREAS, The members of the Illinois House of 
3Representative wish to congratulate the University of Illinois 
4on the occasion of the 150-year anniversary of the creation of 
5the Morrow Plots; and
 
6    WHEREAS, The Morrow Plots started in 1876 as an 
7experimental crop field to study if Midwestern soils required 
8fertilizer or if simple crop rotation was sufficient for soil 
9nutrient retention and stewardship, becoming the nation's 
10oldest research field and one of the oldest continual 
11agricultural research fields, second only to England's 
12Rothamsted Field; and
 
13    WHEREAS, The Morrow Plots' establishment can be attributed 
14to a collection of three talented agricultural researchers, 
15agricultural chemist Charles Wallace Silver, Agriculture 
16Professor Manly Miles, and Professor and Chair of Agriculture 
17George Espy Morrow; and
 
18    WHEREAS, The Morrow Plots were named after Professor 
19Morrow, the first dean of the College of Agricultural, 
20Consumer and Environmental Sciences (ACES) at the University 
21of Illinois Urbana-Champaign, after publishing the findings of 
22a long-term corn study, known as the Experiment 23 Rotation

2    WHEREAS, The Morrow Plots were initially ten half-acre 
3fields designed to test how cropping methods affected soil 
4health and productivity over time; as the University of 
5Illinois expanded, two plots were removed from the study in 
61895 and used for construction purposes, five other plots were

9now familiar to the campus; and
 
10    WHEREAS, The Morrow Plots were designated a National 
11Historic Landmark in 1968 in recognition of both the 
12scientific and symbolic impact of the plots on their 100-year 
13anniversary; and
 
14    WHEREAS, The Morrow Plots occupy a unique space on the 
15ACES campus as both a place of scientific study and cultural 
16prominence, reinforcing that agriculture is cyclical; and
 
17    WHEREAS, The Morrow Plots' purpose remains dedicated to 
18the advancement of agricultural knowledge to better support
