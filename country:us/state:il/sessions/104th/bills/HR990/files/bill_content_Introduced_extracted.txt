it holds the distinction of being the 12northeasternmost point in the State of Illinois, carrying the 13motto "Cornerstone of Illinois", a fitting description of a 14community that has long anchored the northeastern edge of our 15great State; and 16    WHEREAS, The Village of Winthrop Harbor is a proud part of 17the great 61st State House District, a district that runs 18along the Wisconsin border to the north and the shores of Lake 19Michigan to the east, encompassing some of the most storied 20and scenic communities in northern Lake County; the village's 21history, character, and continued growth are a source of great 22pride for the constituents and representative of District 61; 23and   HR0990- 2 -LRB104 22493 LAW 38938 r1    WHEREAS, The earliest recorded settlers of what is now 2Winthrop Harbor were Nelson Landon and Jeremiah Stowell, who 3arrived from Niles, Michigan en route from Chicago toward 4Green Bay, Wisconsin around 1835; finding the area suited to 5their needs, Nelson Landon built the first home in the area, a 6modest 12-by-12-foot cabin situated near what is now 13th 7Street and Adams or 15th Street and Kellogg Creek, before 8returning to Michigan to bring his wife, Phoebe Phelps Landon, 9to her new home; Phoebe Landon proceeded to become the first 10woman settler to give birth in Benton Township on July 27, 111836, and her cabin became a welcoming way station for other 12settlers seeking a place to rest, a meal, or a cup of hot 13coffee; Nelson Landon subsequently farmed more than 1,000 14acres and served as a prominent political figure in Lake 15County who was instrumental in relocating the county seat from 16Burlington, now known as Libertyville, to Little Fort, or 17Waukegan today; and 18    WHEREAS, The Village of Winthrop Harbor takes its name 19from Winthrop, Massachusetts, reflecting the ambitions of its 20early developers, with J.H. Van Vlissingen first envisioning 21development of the area in 1883; the Winthrop Harbor and Dock 22Company acquired more than 2,000 acres of land just south of 23the Wisconsin state line in 1899, platting the village and 24laying the foundation for organized community life; though 4originally envisioned for industrial development, it evolved 5throughout the twentieth century into the family-friendly 6residential lakefront community it is today, a place where 7neighbors know one another and community pride runs deep; and 8    WHEREAS, The Village of Winthrop Harbor is home to North 9Point Marina, the largest freshwater marina in the United 10States, encompassing 140 acres on the shores of Lake Michigan 11and hosting more than 1,500 boat slips, public beaches, a 12fishing pier, a yacht club, and 40 fishing charters; the 13marina draws nearly one million visitors annually and serves 14as an economic and recreational anchor for northeastern 15Illinois and the broader Chicago metropolitan region; and 16    WHEREAS, The Village of Winthrop Harbor is adjacent to 17Adeline Jay Geo-Karis Illinois Beach State Park, a 4,160-acre 18natural area that preserves the only remaining beach ridge and 19swale habitat in the State of Illinois; for well over a century, its 5proximity to this vital national defense installation has 6woven the fabric of military service and community into the 7daily life of local residents; and 8    WHEREAS, For 125 years, the Village of Winthrop Harbor's 9residents, elected officials, and employees have demonstrated 10a steadfast commitment to public service, sound governance, 11and the quality of life of all who call this lakefront area 12home, and the community continues to invest in its future 13through economic development, infrastructure, parks, and the 14cultivation of civic pride that makes it a model community in 15the State of Illinois; therefore, be it 16    RESOLVED, BY THE HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES OF THE ONE 17HUNDRED FOURTH GENERAL ASSEMBLY OF THE STATE OF ILLINOIS, that 18we congratulate the Village of Winthrop Harbor on the 125th 19anniversary of its incorporation, celebrated on July 15, 2026, 20and we commend its residents, officials, and civic leaders for 21their dedication to building a strong, vibrant, and welcoming 22community on the northeastern shore of Lake Michigan; and be 23it further   HR0990- 5 -LRB104 22493 LAW 38938 r1    RESOLVED, That we recognize the Village of Winthrop Harbor 2for its rich history, from its earliest settler families who 3built their first cabin in the wilderness of Benton Township 4to the thriving lakefront community it is today, and we extend 5our best wishes to all who live, work, and find joy along its 6storied shore; and be it further 7    RESOLVED, That suitable copies of this resolution be 8presented to Mayor Dr. Michael Bruno and the Village of 9Winthrop Harbor Board of Trustees as an expression of our 10esteem and respect.
