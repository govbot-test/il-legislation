HOUSE RESOLUTION

 
2    WHEREAS, The members of the Illinois House of 
3Representatives wish to congratulate the Rotary Club of Mount 
4Carroll on the occasion of its 40th anniversary; and
 
5    WHEREAS, The Rotary Club of Mount Carroll has been a 
6community staple since its founding in 1986; and
 
7    WHEREAS, The Rotary Club of Mount Carroll has worked 
8tirelessly to improve the city, raising and providing funds 
9for local infrastructure as well as donating to and supporting 
10local food pantries, youth development initiatives, and 
11various local organizations; and
 
12    WHEREAS, The Rotary Club of Mount Carroll is passionate 
13about the community's first responders, hosting meals and 
14other events for local police officers, city workers, and 
15members of the local fire, ambulance, and 911 dispatch 
16departments; and
 
17    WHEREAS, The Rotary Club of Mount Carroll also hosts 
18events for the community as a whole, including bingo nights, 
19trivia nights, and raffles; and
 
20    WHEREAS, The Rotary Club of Mount Carroll's mission is to
