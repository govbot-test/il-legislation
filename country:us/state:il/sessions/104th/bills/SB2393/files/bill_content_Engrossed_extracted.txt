Short title. This Act may be cited as the 5Prohibition of Nicotine Sales Near Schools Act. 6    Section 5. Definitions. As used in this Act:7    "Day care center" has the meaning given to that term in 8Section 2.09 of the Child Care Act of 1969. "Day care center" 9does not include day care homes, as defined in Section 2.18 of 10the Child Care Act of 1969, or group day care homes, as defined 11in Section 2.20 of the Child Care Act of 1969.12    "Near" means within 500 feet as measured by a straight 13line from the nearest point of the property line of the parcel 14on which the school or day care center is located to the 15nearest point of the property line of the parcel on which the 16retailer is located.17    "Nicotine-containing product" means any product containing 18any form of the chemical nicotine, including any salt or 19complex, regardless of whether the chemical is naturally or 20synthetically derived. "Nicotine-containing product" includes 21nicotine alkaloids and nicotine analogs and any substance that 22mimics or replicates the pharmacological effect of nicotine.23    "School" means any (i) elementary or secondary educational   SB2393 Engrossed- 2 -LRB104 10129 RTM 20201 b1institution; (ii) charter school; (iii) vocational school; 2(iv) special education facility; (v) elementary or secondary 3educational agency, as defined in Section 10 of the P-20 4Longitudinal Education Dara System Act; or (vi) nonpublic 5school, including any nonprofit, non-home-based, and nonpublic 6elementary or secondary school. 7    Section 10. County and municipal duties. 8    (a) Beginning January 1, 2027, a county or municipality 9may not permit the sale of nicotine-containing products near a 10school or day care center, unless (i) a county or municipality 11has adopted an ordinance or regulation that regulates the sale 12of nicotine-containing products near a school or day care 13center before January 1, 2027; (ii) the establishment selling 14nicotine-containing products is established before January 1, 152027; or (iii) the establishment selling nicotine-containing 16products was not near a school or day care center when it was 17established.18    (b) Any regulation or ordinance adopted by a county or 19municipality relating to the sale of nicotine-containing 20products within a specified distance of a school or daycare 21before January 1, 2027 may remain in effect and is not affected 22by this Act.23    (c) This Section does not apply to establishments in 24existence before January 1, 2027. An establishment is 25considered in existence if the physical building remains in   SB2393 Engrossed- 3 -LRB104 10129 RTM 20201 b1the same location and is not not affected if the ownership of 2the physical building changes. This Section shall not be 3construed to require an establishment in existence after 4January 1, 2027 to relocate if a school or day care center is 5established or relocates near the establishment. 6    Section 15. Home rule. A home rule county or home rule 7municipality may not regulate the retail sale of 8nicotine-containing products near a school or day care center 9in a manner inconsistent with the requirements of this Act. 10This Section is a denial and limitation of home rule powers and 11functions under subsection (i) of Section 6 of Article VII of 12the Illinois Constitution. 13    Section 99. Effective date. This Act takes effect upon 14becoming law.
