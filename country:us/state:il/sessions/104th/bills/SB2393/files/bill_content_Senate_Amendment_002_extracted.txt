As used in this Act:7    "Day care center" has the meaning given to that term in 8Section 2.09 of the Child Care Act of 1969. "Day care center" 9does not include day care homes, as defined in Section 2.18 of 10the Child Care Act of 1969, or group day care homes, as defined 11in Section 2.20 of the Child Care Act of 1969.12    "Near" means within 500 feet as measured by a straight 13line from the nearest point of the property line of the parcel 14on which the school or day care center is located to the 15nearest point of the property line of the parcel on which the 16retailer is located.  10400SB2393sam002- 2 -LRB104 10129 RTM 35192 a1    "Nicotine-containing product" means any product containing 2any form of the chemical nicotine, including any salt or 3complex, regardless of whether the chemical is naturally or 4synthetically derived. "Nicotine-containing product" includes 5nicotine alkaloids and nicotine analogs and any substance that 6mimics or replicates the pharmacological effect of nicotine.7    "School" means any (i) elementary or secondary educational 8institution; (ii) charter school; (iii) vocational school; 9(iv) special education facility; (v) elementary or secondary 10educational agency, as defined in Section 10 of the P-20 11Longitudinal Education Dara System Act; or (vi) nonpublic 12school, including any nonprofit, non-home-based, and nonpublic 13elementary or secondary school. 14    Section 10. County and municipal duties. 15    (a) Beginning January 1, 2027, a county or municipality 16may not permit the sale of nicotine-containing products near a 17school or day care center, unless (i) a county or municipality 18has adopted an ordinance or regulation that regulates the sale 19of nicotine-containing products near a school or day care 20center before January 1, 2027; (ii) the establishment selling 21nicotine-containing products is established before January 1, 222027; or (iii) the establishment selling nicotine-containing 23products was not near a school or day care center when it was 24established.25    (b) Any regulation or ordinance adopted by a county or   10400SB2393sam002- 3 -LRB104 10129 RTM 35192 a1municipality relating to the sale of nicotine-containing 2products within a specified distance of a school or daycare 3before January 1, 2027 may remain in effect and is not affected 4by this Act.5    (c) This Section does not apply to establishments in 6existence before January 1, 2027. An establishment is 7considered in existence if the physical building remains in 8the same location and is not not affected if the ownership of 9the physical building changes. This Section shall not be 10construed to require an establishment in existence after 11January 1, 2027 to relocate if a school or day care center is 12established or relocates near the establishment. 13    Section 15. Home rule. A home rule county or home rule 14municipality may not regulate the retail sale of 15nicotine-containing products near a school or day care center 16in a manner inconsistent with the requirements of this Act. 17This Section is a denial and limitation of home rule powers and 18functions under subsection (i) of Section 6 of Article VII of 19the Illinois Constitution. 20    Section 99. Effective date. This Act takes effect upon 21becoming law.".
