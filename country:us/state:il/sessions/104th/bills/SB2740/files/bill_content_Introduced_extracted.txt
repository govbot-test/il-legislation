Full Text of SB2740 Illinois General Assembly ILGA.GOV SB2740 - 104th General Assembly 104TH GENERAL ASSEMBLY State of Illinois 2025 and 2026SB2740 Introduced 1/13/2026, by Sen. Linda Holmes SYNOPSIS AS INTRODUCED: 65 ILCS 5/1-1-14 new    Amends the Illinois Municipal Code. Provides that, upon adoption of an enacting ordinance by a majority vote of the corporate authorities then holding office, a municipality with a population of more than 5,000 may exercise the powers and authorities provided to home rule units under Section 6 of Article VII of the Illinois Constitution.
