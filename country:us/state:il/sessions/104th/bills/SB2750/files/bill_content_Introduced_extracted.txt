Morrison SYNOPSIS AS INTRODUCED: 35 ILCS 200/15-169.1 new    Amends the Property Tax Code. Provides that property that is used as a qualified residence by the surviving spouse of a law enforcement officer who was killed in the line of duty at any time prior to the expiration of the application period in effect for the exemption for the taxable year for which the exemption is sought is exempt.
