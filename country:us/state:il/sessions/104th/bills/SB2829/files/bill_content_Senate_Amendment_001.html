3    Section 10. Creation of District. There is created in the 
4City of Springfield a special development district, the 
5Capital City Downtown Medical District (the "District"), whose 
6boundaries are 11th Street on the east, North Grand Avenue on 
7the north, Walnut Street on the west, and South Grand Avenue on 
8the south, and which excludes all local, State, and federal 
9government properties. The District is created to help build 
10and sustain a vibrant and thriving downtown zone in the 
11State's capital city by encouraging economic development, by 
12increasing the number of people who reside, who are employed, 
13who visit, and who study within the District, and by 
14increasing economic activity in the heart of downtown 
15Springfield through commercial development, including 
16mixed-use housing developments, workforce housing, student 
17housing, and middle-income housing within walking distance of 
18government facilities, healthcare facilities, commercial 
19facilities, and educational facilities in and near the 
20District. The District aims to support revitalization in key 
21sectors, including, but not limited to, healthcare, education, 
22research, technology, housing, retail merchandising, 
23restaurants, arts and culture, tourism, historic preservation 
24and public infrastructure, and to improve and provide for the 
25management of land and facilities within the District.
