Full Text of SB2856 Illinois General Assembly ILGA.GOV SB2856 - 104th General Assembly 104TH GENERAL ASSEMBLY State of Illinois 2025 and 2026SB2856 Introduced 1/16/2026, by Sen. Paul Faraci SYNOPSIS AS INTRODUCED: 35 ILCS 5/246 new5 ILCS 100/5-45.71 new    Amends the Illinois Income Tax Act. Creates an income tax credit for individuals who (i) serve as a volunteer for 100 hours during the taxable year, (ii) do not receive any compensation for their services as a volunteer for the taxable year, and (iii) do not serve on a full-time or part-time career basis for the entity for which they volunteer. Provides that the Department of Revenue may award not more than $5,000,000 in credits under these provisions in any calendar year.
