The right of 2reassignment shall apply to every natural person under the 3staggered registration system provided the application for 4reassignment is received in the Office of the Secretary of 5State by the 1st day of the month immediately preceding the 6applicant's month of expiration.7    In addition, every natural person shall have the right of 8reassignment of the license number issued to him for a 9two-year registration, for the ensuing two-year period. Where 10the two-year period is for two calendar years, the application 11for reassignment must be received by the Secretary of State on 12or before September 30th of the year preceding commencement of 13the two-year period. The new 8combinations so specified shall not be subject to the right of 9reassignment, and no right of reassignment thereto may at any 10future time be acquired.11    (c) If a person has a registration plate in his or her name 12and seeks to reassign the registration plate to his or her 13spouse, the Secretary shall waive any transfer fee or vanity 14or personalized registration plate fee upon both spouses 15signing a form authorizing the reassignment of registration.16    (c-1) If a person who has a registration plate in his or 17her name seeks to reassign the registration plate to his or her 18child, the Secretary shall waive any transfer fee or vanity or 19personalized registration plate fee.20(Source: P.A. 101-395, eff. 8-16-19; 101-611, eff. 6-1-20; 21102-558, eff. 8-20-21.)".
