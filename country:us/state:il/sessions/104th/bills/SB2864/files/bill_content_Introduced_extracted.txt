Full Text of SB2864 Illinois General Assembly ILGA.GOV SB2864 - 104th General Assembly 104TH GENERAL ASSEMBLY State of Illinois 2025 and 2026SB2864 Introduced 1/16/2026, by Sen. Ram Villivalam SYNOPSIS AS INTRODUCED:     Appropriates $300,000 from the General Revenue Fund to the Department of Public Health for grants to the Les Turner ALS Foundation for research on Amyotrophic Lateral Sclerosis (ALS) and for care of those persons affected by the disease. Effective July 1, 2026.
