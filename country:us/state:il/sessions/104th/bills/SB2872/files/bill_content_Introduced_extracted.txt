Morrison SYNOPSIS AS INTRODUCED: 215 ILCS 5/229.4a    Amends the Illinois Insurance Code. Provides that specified provisions of the Standard Nonforfeiture Law for Individual Deferred Annuities shall not apply to contingent deferred annuities.
