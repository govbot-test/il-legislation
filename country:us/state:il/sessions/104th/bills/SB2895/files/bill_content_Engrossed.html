7    Sec. 18.4. Information provided to adoptive parents. (a)

16appearance of biological parents; (iv) their education, 
17occupation, hobbies, interests and talents; (v) existence of 
18any other children born to the biological parents; (vi) 
19information about biological grandparents; reason for 
20emigrating into the United States, if applicable, and country 
21of origin; (vii) relationship between biological parents; 
22(viii) detailed medical and mental health histories of the 
23child, the biological parents, and their immediate relatives;
