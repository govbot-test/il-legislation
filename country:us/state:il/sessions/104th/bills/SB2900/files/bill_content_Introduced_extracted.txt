Full Text of SB2900 Illinois General Assembly ILGA.GOV SB2900 - 104th General Assembly 104TH GENERAL ASSEMBLY State of Illinois 2025 and 2026SB2900 Introduced 1/27/2026, by Sen. Patrick J. Joyce SYNOPSIS AS INTRODUCED: 625 ILCS 5/12-705.1    Amends the Illinois Vehicle Code. Provides that, beginning July 1, 2027, a diesel powered vehicle owned or operated by the State, any county or unit of local government, any school district, any community college or public college or university, or any mass transit agency must use a biodiesel blend that contains 19%, unless the engine is designed or retrofitted to operate on a higher percentage of biodiesel or on ultra low sulfur fuel. Allows, in December, January, February, and March, a biodiesel blend of at least 10% to be used.
