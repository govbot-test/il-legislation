13    Section 15. Standards for vehicle impact protection

15    (a) Vehicle protection devices required under this Act

17    (b) The Department of Transportation shall adopt rules

20    (c) The vehicle impact protection devices shall be 
21designed to withstand, at minimum, an impact resistance level 
22of 5,000 pounds at 30 miles per hour. The devices must have a 
23minimum height of 3 feet and must be spaced 48 inches from 
24inside edge to inside edge of the device. Vehicle impact 
25protection devices must not restrict access to the building
