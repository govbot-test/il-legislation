Suzy Glowiak Hilton SYNOPSIS AS INTRODUCED: 725 ILCS 5/110-6  from Ch. 38, par. 110-6    Amends the Code of Criminal Procedure of 1963. Provides that when a defendant released pretrial is charged with a felony or Class A misdemeanor that is alleged to have occurred during the defendant's pretrial release, the State may seek revocation of the defendant's pretrial release.
