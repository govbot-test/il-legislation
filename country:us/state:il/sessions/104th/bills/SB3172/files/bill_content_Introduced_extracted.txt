Full Text of SB3172 Illinois General Assembly ILGA.GOV SB3172 - 104th General Assembly 104TH GENERAL ASSEMBLY State of Illinois 2025 and 2026SB3172 Introduced 2/2/2026, by Sen. Jil Tracy SYNOPSIS AS INTRODUCED: 720 ILCS 5/16-3  from Ch. 38, par. 16-3    Amends the Criminal Code of 2012. Provides that when compensation for labor or services is ordinarily paid immediately upon the rendering of the labor or services, the trier of fact may infer that the labor or services were obtained by deception if the person refuses to pay or absconds without payment or without an offer to pay for the labor or services. A person convicted of theft under subsection (b) of 10this Section is guilty of a Class 4 felony.
