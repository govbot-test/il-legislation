Full Text of SB3259 Illinois General Assembly ILGA.GOV SB3259 - 104th General Assembly 104TH GENERAL ASSEMBLY State of Illinois 2025 and 2026SB3259 Introduced 2/3/2026, by Sen. Christopher Belt SYNOPSIS AS INTRODUCED: 815 ILCS 505/2EEEE    Amends the Consumer Fraud and Deceptive Business Practices Act. In provisions concerning medical debt, provides that it is an unlawful practice within the meaning of the Act for any person: (1) to use a medical debt listed on a consumer credit report as a negative or adverse factor when making a credit decision; or (2) to furnish information regarding a medical debt to a consumer reporting agency.
