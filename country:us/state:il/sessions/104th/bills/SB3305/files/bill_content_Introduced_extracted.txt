Full Text of SB3305 Illinois General Assembly ILGA.GOV SB3305 - 104th General Assembly 104TH GENERAL ASSEMBLY State of Illinois 2025 and 2026SB3305 Introduced 2/3/2026, by Sen. David Koehler SYNOPSIS AS INTRODUCED: 410 ILCS 625/3.08    Amends the Food Handling Regulation Enforcement Act. Requires a restaurant to display a notice, directly on its menu or in a digital format, for each menu item indicating to consumers the major food allergens that the restaurant knows or reasonably should know are contained as ingredients in the menu item. Provides that a restaurant shall provide notice of major food allergens on its menu or in digital format through specified methods.
