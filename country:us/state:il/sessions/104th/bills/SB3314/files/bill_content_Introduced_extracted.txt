Full Text of SB3314 Illinois General Assembly ILGA.GOV SB3314 - 104th General Assembly 104TH GENERAL ASSEMBLY State of Illinois 2025 and 2026SB3314 Introduced 2/3/2026, by Sen. Mary Edly-Allen SYNOPSIS AS INTRODUCED: 110 ILCS 205/9.47 new    Amends the Board of Higher Education Act. Provides that the Board of Higher Education shall compile and make available to the public a report that contains the following information for all institutions of higher learning: (1) the number of credit hours taught by full-time faculty, organized by institution, tenure status, degree, and certificate program; and (2) the number of credit hours taught by part-time faculty, organized by institution, degree, and certificate program.LRB104 17510 LNS 30937 b  A BILL FOR SB3314LRB104 17510 LNS 30937 b1    AN ACT concerning education. 2    Be it enacted by the People of the State of Illinois, 3represented in the General Assembly: 4    Section 5.
