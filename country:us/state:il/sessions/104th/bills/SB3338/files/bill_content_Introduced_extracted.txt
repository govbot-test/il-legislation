Full Text of SB3338 Illinois General Assembly ILGA.GOV SB3338 - 104th General Assembly 104TH GENERAL ASSEMBLY State of Illinois 2025 and 2026SB3338 Introduced 2/4/2026, by Sen. Adriane Johnson SYNOPSIS AS INTRODUCED: New Act    Creates the Facilitating Voting By All Eligible Citizens Act. Provides that all eligible citizens shall cast a ballot in every general election. Provides that an eligible citizen who casts a blank ballot in a general election shall satisfy the requirement.
