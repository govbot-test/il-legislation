The Birth Control Services to Minors Act is 5amended by changing Section 1 as follows: 6    (325 ILCS 10/1)  (from Ch. 111 1/2, par. 4651)7    Sec. 1. 8    (a) As used in this Section:9    "Contraceptive services" has the same meaning ascribed to 10that term in paragraph (2) of subsection (a) of Section 356z.4 11of the Illinois Insurance Code, but does not include 12sterilization.13    "Contraceptive supplies" means all contraceptive drugs, 14devices, and products approved by the federal Food and Drug 15Administration to prevent pregnancy, including long-acting 16reversible contraceptives, nonprescription oral hormonal 17contraceptives, and nonprescription emergency contraceptives. 18    (b) Any minor may give effective consent for contraceptive 19services or supplies and the consent of no other person is 20required. A request for prescription contraceptive services or 21supplies shall be made to a physician licensed to practice 22medicine in all of its branches, an advanced practice 23registered nurse or a physician assistant licensed to practice   SB3341 Engrossed- 2 -LRB104 17565 KTG 30993 b1in the State, or a pharmacist pursuant to the Pharmacy 2Practice Act. For the purposes of accessing contraceptive 3services or supplies under this Act, a minor is deemed to have 4the same legal capacity to act and has the same powers and 5obligations as a person of legal age.6Birth control services and information may be rendered by 7doctors licensed in Illinois to practice medicine in all of 8its branches to any minor:9    1. who is married; or10    2. who is a parent; or11    3. who is pregnant; or12    4. who has the consent of his parent or legal guardian; or13    5. as to whom the failure to provide such services would 14create a serious health hazard; or15    6. who is referred for such services by a physician, 16clergyman or a planned parenthood agency.17(Source: P.A. 76-1550.)
