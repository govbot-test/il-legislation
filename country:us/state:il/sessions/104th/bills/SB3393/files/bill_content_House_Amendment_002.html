2    "Registered apprenticeship program" means a program 
3registered with and approved by the United States Department 
4of Labor Office of Apprenticeship under 29 CFR Parts 29 and 30 
5that covers one or more trades employed in the construction, 
6installation, or maintenance of a covered broadband project.
7    (b) The merit review scoring criteria for competitive 
8applications for award of a covered broadband project shall 
9include criteria to award points to projects that commit to 
10demonstrating on an ongoing basis a meaningful level of labor 
11hours intended to be performed by apprentices from registered 
12apprenticeship programs. If a covered broadband project that 
13made this commitment is awarded a grant, compliance with a 
14commitment made under this subsection shall be a continuing 
15condition of the award throughout the construction phase of 
16the project unless a waiver for apprentice participation has 
17been granted by the Department of Commerce and Economic 
18Opportunity under the Illinois Works Jobs Program Act.
19    (c) Each applicant for grant or loan funding for a covered 
20broadband project shall submit, with the initial application, 
21(i) a signed commitment to comply with the Illinois Works 
22Apprenticeship Initiative under the Illinois Works Jobs 
23Program Act and (ii) if the applicant intends to seek points on 
24the application based on a commitment to meet the requirements 
25of subsection (b), a signed commitment of compliance with the 
26proposed meaningful participation of apprentices as described
