"Covered broadband project" 14does not include a project undertaken pursuant to the 15Department's agreement with the National Telecommunications 16and Information Administration under the Broadband Equity, 17Access, and Deployment Program, 2022 Notice of Funding   10400SB3393ham002- 2 -LRB104 17748 SPS 38314 a1Opportunity.2    "Registered apprenticeship program" means a program 3registered with and approved by the United States Department 4of Labor Office of Apprenticeship under 29 CFR Parts 29 and 30 5that covers one or more trades employed in the construction, 6installation, or maintenance of a covered broadband project.7    (b) The merit review scoring criteria for competitive 8applications for award of a covered broadband project shall 9include criteria to award points to projects that commit to 10demonstrating on an ongoing basis a meaningful level of labor 11hours intended to be performed by apprentices from registered 12apprenticeship programs.
