Gambling losses recoverable. 8    (a) Any person who by gambling shall lose to any other 9person, any sum of money or thing of value, amounting to the 10sum of $5,000 $50 or more and shall pay or deliver the same or 11any part thereof, may sue for and recover the money or other 12thing of value, so lost and paid or delivered, in a civil 13action against the winner thereof, with costs, in the circuit 14court. No person who accepts from another person for 15transmission, and transmits, either in his own name or in the 16name of such other person, any order for any transaction to be   10400SB3394sam001- 2 -LRB104 19516 RLC 36023 a1made upon, or who executes any order given to him by another 2person, or who executes any transaction for his own account 3on, any regular board of trade or commercial, commodity or 4stock exchange, shall, under any circumstances, be deemed a 5"winner" of any moneys lost by such other person in or through 6any such transactions.7    (b) (Blank). If within 6 months, such person who under the 8terms of Subsection 28-8(a) is entitled to initiate action to 9recover his losses does not in fact pursue his remedy, any 10person may initiate a civil action against the winner. The 11court or the jury, as the case may be, shall determine the 12amount of the loss. After such determination, the court shall 13enter a judgment of triple the amount so determined.14    (c) Gambling losses as a result of gambling conducted on a 15video gaming terminal licensed under the Video Gaming Act are 16not recoverable under this Section. 17    (d) Losses as a result of participation in any fantasy 18contests against a fantasy contest operator, including 19single-player fantasy contests, are not recoverable under this 20Section.
