Mike Simmons SYNOPSIS AS INTRODUCED: 20 ILCS 2705/2705-631 new    Amends the Department of Transportation Law of the Civil Administrative Code of Illinois. Provides that the Department of transportation shall develop a statewide bicycle grid network of State bicycle routes to include greenspace and protected bicycle routes connecting major cities, suburbs, and townships across the State.
