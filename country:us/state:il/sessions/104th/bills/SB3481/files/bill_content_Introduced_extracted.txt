Full Text of SB3481 Illinois General Assembly ILGA.GOV SB3481 - 104th General Assembly 104TH GENERAL ASSEMBLY State of Illinois 2025 and 2026SB3481 Introduced 2/5/2026, by Sen. Mike Simmons SYNOPSIS AS INTRODUCED: 210 ILCS 85/6.14i new    Amends the Hospital Licensing Act. Prohibits a hospital or health care professional from disclosing or threatening to disclose to any other person, entity, or agency information regarding or relating to gender-affirming care of a minor or personally identifiable information regarding a minor receiving gender-affirming care, unless disclosure is in strict accordance with all applicable local, State, and federal law.LRB104 19357 BAB 32805 b  A BILL FOR SB3481LRB104 19357 BAB 32805 b1    AN ACT concerning regulation. 2    Be it enacted by the People of the State of Illinois, 3represented in the General Assembly: 4    Section 5. The Hospital Licensing Act is amended by 5changing Section 6.14i as follows: 6    (210 ILCS 85/6.14i new)7    Sec. 6.14i. Prohibited disclosure of gender-affirming care 8information. A hospital or health care professional shall not 9disclose or threaten to disclose to any other person, entity, 10or agency information regarding or relating to 11gender-affirming care of a minor or personally identifiable 12information regarding a minor receiving gender-affirming care, 13unless disclosure is in strict accordance with all applicable 14local, State, and federal law, including, but not limited to, 15the Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act of 161996, as amended, and its implementing regulations, including, 17but not limited to, the Privacy Rule (45 CFR Parts 160, 162, 18and 164) and including, but not limited to, 45 CFR 164.512(e) 19and 45 CFR 164.512(f).
