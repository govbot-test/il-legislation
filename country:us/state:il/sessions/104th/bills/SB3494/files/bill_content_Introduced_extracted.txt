Willie Preston SYNOPSIS AS INTRODUCED: 35 ILCS 200/21-35035 ILCS 200/22-30    Amends the Property Tax Code. In provisions concerning tax sales, provides that, for tax sales that occur on or after the effective date of the amendatory Act, the redemption period shall be 5 years (instead of 2.5 years).
