Full Text of SB3553 Illinois General Assembly ILGA.GOV SB3553 - 104th General Assembly 104TH GENERAL ASSEMBLY State of Illinois 2025 and 2026SB3553 Introduced 2/5/2026, by Sen. David Koehler SYNOPSIS AS INTRODUCED:     Appropriates $500,000 from the General Revenue Fund to the Department of Human Services for a grant to Treatment Alternatives for Stronger Communities (TASC) to support pursuit of certification as a Comprehensive Community Mental Health Center beginning with the TASC offices in Rockford and Peoria. Effective July 1, 2026.LRB104 20612 KTG 34104 b SB3553LRB104 20612 KTG 34104 b1    AN ACT concerning appropriations.  2    Be it enacted by the People of the State of Illinois, 3represented in the General Assembly: 4    Section 5. The sum of $500,000, or so much of that amount 5as may be necessary, is appropriated from the General Revenue 6Fund to the Department of Human Services for a grant to 7Treatment Alternatives for Stronger Communities (TASC) to 8support pursuit of certification as a Comprehensive Community 9Mental Health Center beginning with the TASC offices in 10Rockford and Peoria. The grant shall support a one-year 11implementation period with allowable expenses, including, but 12not limited to, cloud-based information technology services, 13expert consultation, staffing including management and 14supervisory staff, and provision of direct service to clients. 15    Section 99.
