Full Text of SB3607 Illinois General Assembly ILGA.GOV SB3607 - 104th General Assembly 104TH GENERAL ASSEMBLY State of Illinois 2025 and 2026SB3607 Introduced 2/5/2026, by Sen. Steve Stadelman SYNOPSIS AS INTRODUCED: New Act    Amends the Fair Contracting for Health Care Practitioners Act. Provides that, except as otherwise provided, a covenant not to compete entered into on or after the effective date of the Act is deemed contrary to the public policy and is void and unenforceable by an employer. Provides that an employer may enforce a covenant not to compete if the length of the covenant not to compete is no more than one year, provided that the health care practitioner was not dismissed by the employer. Sets forth provisions concerning the scope of the Act.
