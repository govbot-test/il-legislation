The Department of Transportation Law of the 5Civil Administrative Code of Illinois is amended by adding 6Section 2705-631 as follows: 7    (20 ILCS 2705/2705-631 new)8    Sec. 2705-631. Quick-build infrastructure. 9    (a) As used in this Section, "quick-build infrastructure" 10means a temporary intersection treatment for pedestrian safety 11designed for accelerated deployment using low-cost, modular, 12temporary, or removable materials or methods, including 13delineator posts, modular curbing, temporary curb extensions, 14refuge islands, signal timing or phasing changes, removable 15barriers, or similar treatments intended to reduce crashes 16involving vulnerable road users.  10400SB3627sam002- 2 -LRB104 19781 LNS 36619 a1    (b) The Department is authorized to use quick-build 2infrastructure as a temporary alternative for permanent 3infrastructure improvements if the Department deems it 4appropriate.5        (1) When evaluating whether quick-build infrastructure 6    is appropriate. the Department may take into account crash 7    history, traffic volumes, freight patterns, vulnerable 8    road user presence, school zones, and the existing 9    context.10        (2) Local agencies may request, via permit, 11    quick-build infrastructure to remedy safety issues on 12    roads under the Department's jurisdiction.13    (c) Nothing in this Section shall be construed as 14requiring the Department to deviate from standards set out by 15the Manual on Uniform Traffic Control Devices or other 16nationally accepted standards.".
