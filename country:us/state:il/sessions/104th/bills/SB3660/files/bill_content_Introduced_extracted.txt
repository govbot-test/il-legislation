Full Text of SB3660 Illinois General Assembly ILGA.GOV SB3660 - 104th General Assembly 104TH GENERAL ASSEMBLY State of Illinois 2025 and 2026SB3660 Introduced 2/5/2026, by Sen. Christopher Belt SYNOPSIS AS INTRODUCED: 420 ILCS 35/6  from Ch. 111 1/2, par. 230.6    Amends the Radioactive Waste Storage Act. Provides that interest earned by the investment or deposit of moneys accumulated in the fund pursuant to the settlement agreement in the matter of the State of Illinois, et al. v. Teledyne, Inc., shall be deposited into the Radioactive Waste Site Perpetual Care Fund for the continued maintenance of the Sheffield Low-Level Radioactive Waste Site and the surrounding buffer zone. It is recognized by the General Assembly that any 8site used for the concentration and storage of radioactive 9waste material will represent a continuing and perpetual 10responsibility in the interests of the public health, safety 11and general welfare, and that the same must ultimately be 12reposed in a sovereign government without regard for the 13existence or nonexistence of any particular agency, 14instrumentality, department, division or officer thereof.
