Full Text of SB3686 Illinois General Assembly ILGA.GOV SB3686 - 104th General Assembly 104TH GENERAL ASSEMBLY State of Illinois 2025 and 2026SB3686 Introduced 2/5/2026, by Sen. Robert Peters SYNOPSIS AS INTRODUCED: 20 ILCS 1305/10-85 new    Amends the Department of Human Services Act. Provides that all moneys payable from funds appropriated to the Department of Human Services in State Fiscal Year 2027 for grants to counties with court-based eviction early resolution programs and associated legal assistance, mediation, and other services shall be distributed by the Department in proportion to the volume of eviction filings by judicial circuit and prioritizing support for counties that are providing at least 50% in matching funds.
