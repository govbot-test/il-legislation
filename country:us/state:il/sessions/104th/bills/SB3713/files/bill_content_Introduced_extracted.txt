Full Text of SB3713 Illinois General Assembly ILGA.GOV SB3713 - 104th General Assembly 104TH GENERAL ASSEMBLY State of Illinois 2025 and 2026SB3713 Introduced 2/5/2026, by Sen. Adriane Johnson SYNOPSIS AS INTRODUCED: 755 ILCS 5/11a-17  from Ch. 110 1/2, par. 11a-17    Amends the Probate Act of 1975. Provides that an individual under guardianship who understands the nature, effect, duties, and obligations of marriage does not lose the right to marry without the prior consent of the individual, the guardian, or authorization of the court with some exceptions. Allows a court to remove the right to marry if removal is included as requested relief in a petition for a limited or plenary guardian or upon application of the guardian, and the court finds by clear and convincing evidence after an evidentiary hearing that the individual lacks the capacity to understand the nature, effect, duties, and obligations of a decision to marry. Provides conditions in which an individual from whom the right to marry has been removed may nonetheless marry. Provides that a marriage entered into by one or more individuals from whom the right to marry has been removed is void with some exceptions.
