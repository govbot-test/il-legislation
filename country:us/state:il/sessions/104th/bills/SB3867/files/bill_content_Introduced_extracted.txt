Full Text of SB3867 Illinois General Assembly ILGA.GOV SB3867 - 104th General Assembly 104TH GENERAL ASSEMBLY State of Illinois 2025 and 2026SB3867 Introduced 2/6/2026, by Sen. John F. Curran SYNOPSIS AS INTRODUCED: New Act    Creates the State Employee Child Care Pilot Program Task Force Act. Establishes the State Employee Child Care Pilot Program Task Force within the Department of Early Childhood to evaluate the feasibility of establishing an on-site child care pilot program for State employees; develop recommendations regarding pilot program structure, staffing models, and operational oversight; identify potential State-owned or leased facilities suitable for pilot program locations; and other specified duties. Contains provisions concerning Task Force membership and meetings. Requires the Task Force to submit a report to the General Assembly and the Governor that includes findings, recommendations, estimated costs, and any proposed statutory or administrative changes necessary to implement a pilot program. Provides that the Task Force is dissolved and the Act is repealed on December 31, 2027.
