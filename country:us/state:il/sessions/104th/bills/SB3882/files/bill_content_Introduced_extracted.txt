Mike Simmons SYNOPSIS AS INTRODUCED: 305 ILCS 5/5-1.7 new305 ILCS 5/12-4.4a new    Amends the Medical Assistance Article of the Illinois Public Aid Code. Provides that the Department of Healthcare and Family Services shall not require recipients of medical assistance who are also former foster care youth, veterans, or people experiencing homelessness to work a minimum amount of hours in order to maintain eligibility for medical assistance.
