Full Text of SB3938 Illinois General Assembly ILGA.GOV SB3938 - 104th General Assembly 104TH GENERAL ASSEMBLY State of Illinois 2025 and 2026SB3938 Introduced 2/6/2026, by Sen. Chris Balkema SYNOPSIS AS INTRODUCED: 35 ILCS 200/Art. 31.1 heading new35 ILCS 200/31.1-1 new35 ILCS 200/31.1-5 new35 ILCS 200/31.1-10 new    Amends the Property Tax Code. Creates the Site Value Tax Law within the Property Tax Code. Provides that each taxing district may, by ordinance, levy a site value tax upon the assessed value of land within the taxing district. Provides that "assessed value" means 33 1/3% of the fair cash value of the land, without regard to buildings, structures, improvements, or other permanent fixtures on the land, except for the value of oil, gas, coal, and other minerals in the land and the right to remove such oil, gas, coal, and other minerals from the land. Contains provisions concerning enforcement and collection.
