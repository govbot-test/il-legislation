Full Text of SB3951 Illinois General Assembly ILGA.GOV SB3951 - 104th General Assembly 104TH GENERAL ASSEMBLY State of Illinois 2025 and 2026SB3951 Introduced 2/6/2026, by Sen. Erica Harriss SYNOPSIS AS INTRODUCED: 605 ILCS 5/9-133 new    Amends the Illinois Highway Code. Allows a unit of local government to place a blue reflective pavement marker on any highway, street, or road to mark the location of a fire hydrant or water supply. Prohibits a unit of local government from placing a blue reflective pavement marker on a State highway unless it first obtains an encroachment permit from the Department of Transportation.LRB104 17262 LNS 30684 b  A BILL FOR SB3951LRB104 17262 LNS 30684 b1    AN ACT concerning transportation. 2    Be it enacted by the People of the State of Illinois, 3represented in the General Assembly: 4    Section 5. The Illinois Highway Code is amended by adding 5Section 9-133 as follows: 6    (605 ILCS 5/9-133 new)7    Sec. 9-133. Blue reflective pavement markers. A unit of 8local government may place a blue reflective pavement marker 9on any highway, street, or road to mark the location of a fire 10hydrant or water supply. The blue reflective pavement marker 11shall not be used for any other purpose. A unit of local 12government shall not place a blue reflective pavement marker 13on a State highway unless it first obtains an encroachment 14permit from the Department.15    This Section does not apply to freeways or freeway ramps.
