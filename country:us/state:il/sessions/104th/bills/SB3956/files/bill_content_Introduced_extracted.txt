Full Text of SB3956 Illinois General Assembly ILGA.GOV SB3956 - 104th General Assembly 104TH GENERAL ASSEMBLY State of Illinois 2025 and 2026SB3956 Introduced 2/6/2026, by Sen. Erica Harriss SYNOPSIS AS INTRODUCED: 220 ILCS 5/4-620 new    Amends the Public Utilities Act. Requires the Illinois Commerce Commission to establish a process for the review of disposal fees, fuel surcharges, and other fees imposed by the owners and operators of sanitary landfills for the disposal of solid waste to ensure that the fees are just and reasonable. The Commission shall adopt any rules necessary to administer the provisions of this Section.LRB104 19400 AAS 32848 b  A BILL FOR SB3956LRB104 19400 AAS 32848 b1    AN ACT concerning regulation. 2    Be it enacted by the People of the State of Illinois, 3represented in the General Assembly: 4    Section 5. The Public Utilities Act is amended by adding 5Section 4-620 as follows: 6    (220 ILCS 5/4-620 new)7    Sec. 4-620. Solid waste disposal system regulation.8    (a) As used in this Section:9    "Sanitary landfill" has the meaning given to that term in 10Section 3.445 of the Environmental Protection Act.11    "Solid waste" has the meaning given to that term in the 12Environmental Protection Act. "Solid waste" does not include 13hazardous waste as defined in the Environmental Protection 14Act.15    (b) The Commission shall establish a process for the 16review of disposal fees, fuel surcharges, and other fees 17imposed by the owners and operators of sanitary landfills for 18the disposal of solid waste to ensure that the fees are just 19and reasonable. The Commission shall adopt any rules necessary 20to administer the provisions of this Section.
