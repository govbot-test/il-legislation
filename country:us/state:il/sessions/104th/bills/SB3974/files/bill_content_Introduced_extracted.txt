Full Text of SB3974 Illinois General Assembly ILGA.GOV SB3974 - 104th General Assembly 104TH GENERAL ASSEMBLY State of Illinois 2025 and 2026SB3974 Introduced 2/6/2026, by Sen. Omar Aquino SYNOPSIS AS INTRODUCED: 815 ILCS 505/2MMMM new    Amends the Consumer Fraud and Deceptive Business Practices Act. Provides that no person shall sell, attempt to sell, or offer to sell to a consumer in the State a non-externally vented natural gas appliance that is manufactured on or after January 1, 2027 unless a label on the gas appliance bears a specified warning message. Provides that the Attorney General may adopt rules setting forth additional requirements for the placement and formatting of the label.
