"Non-externally vented natural gas 20appliance" does not include (i) portable outdoor cooking 21appliances, laboratory equipment, or appliances intended 22exclusively for outdoor use or (ii) furnaces, boilers, water 23heaters, or other appliances that are required by the 24International Fuel Gas Code or the International Mechanical 25Code to be mechanically vented.".
