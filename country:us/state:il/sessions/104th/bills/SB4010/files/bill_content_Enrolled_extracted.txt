However, for military service not 16immediately following employment, the salary rate on the last 17date as a participating teacher prior to such military 18service, or on the first date as a participating teacher after 19such military service, whichever is greater, shall be assumed 20to be equal to the member's salary rate at the time of entering 21military service.
