Amend Senate Bill 635, AS AMENDED, 3with reference to page and line numbers of Senate Amendment 4No. 2, by replacing line 21 on page 11 through line 6 on page 512 with the following:6    "(e) Nothing in this Act exempts developments affecting 7locally designated landmarks or properties within locally 8designated historic districts from review under an adopted 9historic preservation ordinance if the ordinance has clear and 10objective standards and is applied in a generally uniform and 11nondiscriminatory manner.
