Full Text of SJR0055 Illinois General Assembly ILGA.GOV SJR0055 - 104th General Assembly SJ0055LRB104 20954 JDS 34772 r1SENATE JOINT RESOLUTION 2    WHEREAS, Section 3 of Article VIII of the Constitution of 3the State of Illinois provides that the General Assembly, by a 4vote of three-fifths of the members elected to each house, 5shall appoint an Auditor General; and 6    WHEREAS, The General Assembly has, by Section 2-3 of the 7Illinois State Auditing Act, charged the Legislative Audit 8Commission with the responsibility of diligently searching for 9qualified candidates for the office and making recommendations 10to the General Assembly, and, pursuant to this statutory 11mandate, the Legislative Audit Commission has conducted a 12diligent search and has recommended to the General Assembly 13the appointment of Christopher B. Meister of Oak Park, 14Illinois, as Auditor General; therefore, be it 15    RESOLVED, BY THE SENATE OF THE ONE HUNDRED FOURTH GENERAL 16ASSEMBLY OF THE STATE OF ILLINOIS, THE HOUSE OF 17REPRESENTATIVES CONCURRING HEREIN, that pursuant to Section 3 18of Article VIII of the Constitution and upon the 19recommendation of the Legislative Audit Commission, 20Christopher B. Meister of Oak Park, Illinois, is appointed 21Auditor General for the State of Illinois for a term 22commencing on May 1, 2026.
