and 10    WHEREAS, Fannie Jones was one of the first Black 11substitute teachers in District 189 and one of the youngest; 12she taught first grade at Franklin Elementary School for many 13years before joining the administrative staff of the district; 14she later became program coordinator of the district's Title I 15program until her retirement after 33 years; and 16    WHEREAS, Fannie Jones was the first African American to 17run on a statewide ticket for an elected office as the Clerk of 18the Illinois Supreme Court;
