and 16    WHEREAS, Jim Flanagan coached Little League baseball 17during his younger years, and he became a founding member of 18the Bulls Baseball Club; and 19    WHEREAS, Jim Flanagan and his wife, Sandy, celebrated 20their 50th wedding anniversary in 2019 with a family trip to   SR0480- 2 -LRB104 15933 LAW 29168 r1Wisconsin, where he enjoyed spending time with his entire 2family and listening to and singing all of his favorite songs; 3and 4    WHEREAS, Jim Flanagan was a very proud grandfather, and he 5often bestowed his many jokes and puns upon his grandchildren; 6he will be remembered for bringing a smile to everyone's face 7whenever he was in the room; and 8    WHEREAS, Jim Flanagan was the husband of Sandy; the father 9of Jeanne, Joey, Peggy, Bridget, and Jim; the grandfather of 1014; and the younger brother of Maureen, Pegi, and Nancy; 11therefore, be it 12    RESOLVED, BY THE SENATE OF THE ONE HUNDRED FOURTH GENERAL 13ASSEMBLY OF THE STATE OF ILLINOIS, that we mourn the passing of 14James Joseph "Jim" Flanagan and extend our sincere condolences 15to his family, friends, and all who knew and loved him;
