SENATE RESOLUTION

 
2    WHEREAS, The members of the Illinois Senate are saddened 
3to learn of the death of Sriram Sonty, M.D., F.A.C.S., who 
4passed away on June 8, 2025; and
 
5    WHEREAS, Dr. Sonty was born in Ramachandrapuram, East 
6Godavari District, Andhra Pradesh, India on July 10, 1946; he 
7studied medicine at Andhra Medical College in Visakhapatnam 
8from 1963 to 1969, and he interned at King George Hospital in 
9Visakhapatnam; he then completed his post-graduation studies 
10and residency in ophthalmology at the All India Institute of 
11Medical Sciences in New Delhi, India in 1974; he married 
12Susarla Sarada Purna on February 27, 1975; he moved to the 
13United States in May 1975; he obtained further training in 
14ophthalmology at Tufts Medical Center, the Boston Children 
15Medical Center of Harvard University, and the University of 
16Pittsburgh Eye and Ear Infirmary; and
 
17    WHEREAS, Dr. Sonty served on the faculty at Tufts Medical 
18Center; he later moved to Chicago, where he became a faculty 
19member at the University of Illinois Chicago (UIC) Eye Center 
20and a clinical associate professor at the UIC Glaucoma 
21Service; he served as a senior academic consultant at the King 
22Khalid Eye Specialist Hospital, Riyadh, Saudi Arabia; he was 
23assistant director of the Department of Ophthalmology at
