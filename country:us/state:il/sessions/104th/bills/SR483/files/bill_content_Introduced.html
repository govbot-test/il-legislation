SENATE RESOLUTION

 
2    WHEREAS, The members of the Illinois Senate are saddened 
3to learn of the death of Kenneth W. "Ken" Aupperle of Morton, 
4who passed away on October 14, 2025; and
 
5    WHEREAS, Ken Aupperle was born to William and Sophia 
6(Kaisner) Aupperle in Morton on July 27, 1933; he served in the 
7United States Army; he attended Bradley University; he married 
8Carol Ann Friedinger in Pekin on August 7, 1955, and she 
9preceded him in death on July 23, 2009; he later married Carol 
10Lindstrom in Morton on September 22, 2012; and
 
11    WHEREAS, Ken Aupperle worked for Aupperle Construction, a 
12family business that celebrated its 120-year anniversary this 
13year; he also owned and worked at Cook Rental in Morton; after 
14he retired from Aupperle Construction, he served as an 
15executive director of the Tazewell County Builders 
16Association; and
 
17    WHEREAS, Ken Aupperle served his community as precinct 
18committeeman of Morton for 50-plus years, as an assessor of 
19the Village of Morton for eight years, and as a member of the 
20original Morton Park Board and the Morton Chamber of Commerce; 
21he also gave his time to the Morton Pumpkin Festival Food Tent; 
22and
