SENATE RESOLUTION

 
2    WHEREAS, The members of the Illinois Senate wish to 
3congratulate Project Compassion, NFP on the occasion of its 
420-year anniversary; and
 
5    WHEREAS, Since its inception in 2005, Project Compassion 
6has provided vital support to more than 200,000 homeless, 
7disadvantaged, and low-income men, women, and children through 
8providing access to food, shelter, seasonal supplies, hygiene 
9items, life skills workshops, self-esteem and character 
10development initiatives, and vocational training programs; and
 
11    WHEREAS, Project Compassion opened the Compassionate 
12Resource Center in Belleville in 2011, an achievement that 
13expanded the organization's capacity to serve the community 
14through offering a free cafe, a laundry facility, and a 
15therapy center focused on nonviolent trauma and 
16trauma-informed care; and
 
17    WHEREAS, During the 2020 pandemic, Project Compassion 
18launched Meal Time, an emergency meal initiative that provided 
19hot meals to 15,000 families and partnered with 15 community 
20organizations and churches to feed more than 30,000 
21individuals across the region; and
