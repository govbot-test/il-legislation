and 17    WHEREAS, During the 2020 pandemic, Project Compassion 18launched Meal Time, an emergency meal initiative that provided 19hot meals to 15,000 families and partnered with 15 community 20organizations and churches to feed more than 30,000 21individuals across the region; and   SR0499- 2 -LRB104 16015 ECR 29268 r1    WHEREAS, Project Compassion was founded by Rachel Jackson, 2who also established Empower-Me, the first self-esteem and 3self-worth program in St. Clair County; through partnerships 4with local school districts, the program inspires youth, 5especially girls, to build inner confidence, character, and a 6foundation for continued education; she has further extended 7this mission as host of the Girlz On Purpose Tour, empowering 8young women to lead with purpose and passion; and 9    WHEREAS, Project Compassion has received numerous local 10and national honors in recognition of the organization's 11service and impact, including the Barack Obama Silver 12Presidential Award for Community Service and a proclamation 13from the Mayor of Belleville in 2014 officially designating 14October 29th as Compassion Day; therefore, be it 15    RESOLVED, BY THE SENATE OF THE ONE HUNDRED FOURTH GENERAL 16ASSEMBLY OF THE STATE OF ILLINOIS, that we congratulate 17Project Compassion, NFP on achieving 20 years of providing 18unrelenting dedication and love to those in the community who 19need it most, and we wish the organization many more years of 20continued success; and be it further 21    RESOLVED, That a suitable copy of this resolution be 22presented to Project Compassion as an expression of our esteem 23and respect.
