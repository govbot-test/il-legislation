and 5    WHEREAS, REO Speedwagon was founded in 1967 by Neal 6Doughty and Alan Gratzer, who met at the University of 7Illinois Urbana-Champaign the year prior; inspired by a 8transportation history class, the band's name comes from the 9REO Speedwagon, a 1915 truck designed by Ransom Eli Olds; and 10    WHEREAS, REO Speedwagon began performing at campus events 11and local bars, quickly igniting a passionate following that 12grew into national acclaim; the band's electrifying presence 13and relentless touring soon led to packed arenas and theaters 14coast to coast, marking the rise of a legendary Illinois-born 15rock phenomenon; and 16    WHEREAS, REO Speedwagon gained national attention in the 171970s with a string of successful albums and many energetic 18live performances, and the band's songs dominated FM radio 19during the 1980s; the group continued to electrify audiences 20well into the 21st century, sustaining a devoted fan base 21across generations; and   SR0502- 2 -LRB104 16108 ECR 29420 r1    WHEREAS, REO Speedwagon was inducted into the inaugural 2class of the Illinois Rock & Roll Museum on Route 66, a 3historic center dedicated to preserving and celebrating the 4rich musical legacy of the Prairie State, on June 11, 2021; as 5a trailblazer of Midwest rock, the band's induction honors its 6profound influence on American music history, its enduring 7connection to Illinois, and its role in shaping the sound and 8spirit of generations; and 9    WHEREAS, REO Speedwagon honored its extraordinary legacy 10with a final concert performance in June 2025, commemorating 11nearly six decades of global impact and musical excellence; 12and 13    WHEREAS, From their humble beginnings in Champaign to 14their rise as icons of American rock, the members of REO 15Speedwagon proudly carried the spirit of Illinois to stages 16around the world, forever linking their band to the cultural 17heartbeat and musical legacy of the State; therefore, be it 18    RESOLVED, BY THE SENATE OF THE ONE HUNDRED FOURTH GENERAL 19ASSEMBLY OF THE STATE OF ILLINOIS, that we recognize rock band 20REO Speedwagon on its nearly six decades of legendary musical 21and cultural impact in the State of Illinois and across the 22world;
