Full Text of SR0503 Illinois General Assembly ILGA.GOV SR0503 - 104th General Assembly SR0503LRB104 16143 MST 29506 r1SENATE RESOLUTION 2    WHEREAS, The Illinois State Court of Claims determines and 3pays out Certificate of Innocence awards under 705 ILCS 505; 4and 5    WHEREAS, The Illinois General Assembly has been working on 6legislation to make the Certificate of Innocence awards more 7equitable in keeping with federally recommended minimum 8standards; and 9    WHEREAS, All of the interested parties are in general 10agreement that such change is needed but have been unable to 11resolve the specificity of the statutory language needed; 12therefore, be it 13    RESOLVED, BY THE SENATE OF THE ONE HUNDRED FOURTH GENERAL 14ASSEMBLY OF THE STATE OF ILLINOIS, that we encourage the State 15Court of Claims to pause on Certificate of Innocence payouts 16if requested by the Certificate of Innocence awardee and their 17attorney(s) until the legislature works through the nuances of 18the Certificate of Innocence statutory language by June 30, 192026.
