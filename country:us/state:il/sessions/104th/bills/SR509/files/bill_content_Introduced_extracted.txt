5and 6    WHEREAS, Patrick Hemingway, affectionately known by his 7family as Mouse, was born in Kansas City, Missouri to Ernest 8Hemingway and Pauline Pfeiffer on June 28, 1928; and 9    WHEREAS, Patrick Hemingway spent his youth in both Cuba 10and Key West, Florida, adventuring with his father on his 11fishing boat, Pilar; after his graduation from Canterbury 12School, a Catholic boarding school in Connecticut, he attended 13Stanford University before transferring to and graduating from 14Harvard University with a Bachelor of Arts in Art History and 15Literature in 1950; and 16    WHEREAS, Patrick Hemingway and Henrietta Broyles married 17that same year, and they later moved to Tanganyika, now 18Tanzania, and purchased a 2,300-acre farm; and 19    WHEREAS, Patrick Hemingway began his career dedicated to 20wildlife instruction at the College of African Wildlife 21Management; he became a forestry officer of the United Nations   SR0509- 2 -LRB104 16168 ECR 29536 r1Food and Agriculture Organization, and over time, he gained 2global recognition for his safaris and expeditions; he served 3as a guide, expert, and honorary game warden in British East 4Africa; and 5    WHEREAS, Patrick Hemingway became a father to his 6daughter, Edwina "Mina" Hemingway, in the early 1960s while 7also looking after his sick wife, who sadly passed away in 81963; and 9    WHEREAS, Patrick Hemingway moved back to the United States 10with his daughter, settling in Bozeman in 1975; he married 11Carol Thompson, a professor of theater arts at the City 12College of New York, in 1982; and 13    WHEREAS, Patrick Hemingway supervised and edited many of 14the posthumously published works of his father, Ernest 15Hemingway, including Dear Papa: The Letters of Patrick and 16Ernest Hemingway and True at First Light, a fictitious 17rendition of Ernest Hemingway's own experiences in Africa 18around the mid-1950s; he and his second wife established the 19Patrick and Carol T. Hemingway Scholar-In-Residence at the 20John F. Kennedy Library in 2023; he was previously featured in 21a Kennedy Library Forum in 2006, where he engaged in a dialogue 22about his life, the legacy of his famous father, and his 23contributions to wildlife conservation in Africa; and   SR0509- 3 -LRB104 16168 ECR 29536 r1    WHEREAS, Patrick Hemingway was preceded in death by his 2father, Ernest Hemingway; his mother, Pauline Pfeiffer; his 3brother, Jack Hemingway; and his sister, Gloria Hemingway; and 4    WHEREAS, Patrick Hemingway is survived by his daughter, 5Edwina Hemingway; his four grandchildren; and his seven 6great-grandchildren; therefore, be it 7    RESOLVED, BY THE SENATE OF THE ONE HUNDRED FOURTH GENERAL 8ASSEMBLY OF THE STATE OF ILLINOIS, that we mourn the passing of 9Patrick Miller Hemingway and extend our sincere condolences to 10his family, friends, and all who knew and loved him; and be it 11further 12    RESOLVED, That a suitable copy of this resolution be 13presented to the family of Patrick Hemingway as an expression 14of our deepest sympathy.
