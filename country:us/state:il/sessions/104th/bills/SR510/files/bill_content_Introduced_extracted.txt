and 16    WHEREAS, Colleen Doyle was an ardent traveler to Asia, 17Africa, and multiple locations around Europe, where she 18specifically enjoyed Italian cooking schools to further her 19culinary skills and took many trips with a close-knit group of 20lifelong friends;
