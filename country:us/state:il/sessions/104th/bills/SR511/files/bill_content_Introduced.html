SENATE RESOLUTION

 
2    WHEREAS, The members of the Illinois Senate are saddened 
3to learn of the death of Roberta "Bobbi" Schaper Eastman of Oak 
4Park, who passed away on July 30, 2025 at the age of 50; and
 
5    WHEREAS, Bobbi Eastman was born in Oak Park to Pat and Paul 
6Schaper on December 2, 1974; and
 
7    WHEREAS, Bobbi Eastman started working for the luxury 
8Chicago developer LR Development, now Related Midwest, and 
9then had a 15-year career as a realtor for Baird & Warner, 
10where she also served as charitable arm team captain, helping 
11to organize community events and raise funds to support local 
12charities, including Beyond Hunger; and
 
13    WHEREAS, Bobbi Eastman won multiple sales awards during 
14her time at Baird & Warner, even their highest award, the 
15Founder's Club; and
 
16    WHEREAS, Bobbi Eastman, additionally, served as a member 
17of the board of directors for the Oak Park Area Association of 
18Realtors and the vice-chair of the Government Affairs Task 
19Force; and
 
20    WHEREAS, Bobbi Eastman was a state legislative contact
