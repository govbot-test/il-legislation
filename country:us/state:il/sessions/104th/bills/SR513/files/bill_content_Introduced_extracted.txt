he received his 9bachelor's degree from Quincy University and his master's 10degree from Western Illinois University, and he also did 11graduate coursework in school administration from other 12schools over the years; he married Geraldine "Gerry" 13Fitzgerald on June 11, 1966; and 14    WHEREAS, Chuck Radel was a history teacher at Quincy 15Junior High School before becoming the director of 16transportation for the Quincy Public School District; he later 17became assistant principal of Quincy Junior High School; he 18served the Quincy Public School District for 33 years; during 19his career, he offered his students memorable educational 20experiences, including canoe trips to the Current River and 21the Washington, D.C. trips for eighth graders, and he led more 22than 30 student trips to the Quetico Provincial Park for 23camping and canoeing in Ontario, Canada;
