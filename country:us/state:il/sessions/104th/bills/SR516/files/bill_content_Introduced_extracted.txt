and 5    WHEREAS, Russell Anders was born in Springfield on 6November 23, 1944; he served in the United States Navy as a 7photographer, capturing moments that became part of our 8nation's history; and 9    WHEREAS, Russell Anders worked with the Plumbing and 10Steamfitters Union and as a plumbing inspector for the City of 11Springfield; his career lasted more than 40 years, during 12which he was known for his diligence, intelligence, and 13integrity; and 14    WHEREAS, Russell Anders was a man of many talents and 15interests; he dressed up as Santa Claus for the Sid Wood 16Memorial Toy Run for many years, bringing joy to countless 17children during the holiday season; he also loved building 18model cars and semis, meticulously crafting each piece with 19love and attention to detail; and 20    WHEREAS, Russell Anders was a beloved father, brother, and 21member of the Springfield community; his life was one marked   SR0516- 2 -LRB104 16280 ECR 29665 r1by dedication, service, and the joy he brought to those around 2him; his legacy will endure through the lives he touched, the 3work he accomplished, and the love he shared; and 4    WHEREAS, Russell Anders was preceded in death by his 5parents; his siblings, Alice Dahl, Richard Lee Anders, Letha 6Houston, Richard Anders, Richard Franklin Anders, and Mattie 7Rosetta Luck; and his grandson, Russell Stewart Anders III; 8and 9    WHEREAS, Russell Anders is survived by his son, Russell 10Anders II; his sister, Joan Elaine Nichols; and several 11nieces, nephews, and cousins; therefore, be it 12    RESOLVED, BY THE SENATE OF THE ONE HUNDRED FOURTH GENERAL 13ASSEMBLY OF THE STATE OF ILLINOIS, that we mourn the passing of 14Russell Stewart Anders and extend our sincere condolences to 15his family, friends, and all who knew and loved him; and be it 16further 17    RESOLVED, That a suitable copy of this resolution be 18presented to the family of Russell Anders as an expression of 19our deepest sympathy.
