Coleman; and 4    WHEREAS, Joe Coleman is survived by his son, Zach (Katie) 5Coleman; three grandchildren; his special children, Bridgett, 6Abby, Lizey, Chad, and Levi; 12 special grandchildren; his 7sisters, Linda Paden and Debra (Lamar) Embry; several nieces, 8nephews, great-nieces, and great-nephews; and his best friend, 9Brett "Brent" Henschen; therefore, be it 10    RESOLVED, BY THE SENATE OF THE ONE HUNDRED FOURTH GENERAL 11ASSEMBLY OF THE STATE OF ILLINOIS, that we mourn the passing of 12Joe Coleman and extend our sincere condolences to his family, 13friends, and all who knew and loved him; and be it further 14    RESOLVED, That a suitable copy of this resolution be 15presented to the family of Joe Coleman as an expression of our 16deepest sympathy.
