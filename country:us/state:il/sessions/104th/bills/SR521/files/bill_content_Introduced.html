SENATE RESOLUTION

 
2    WHEREAS, The members of the Illinois Senate are saddened 
3to learn of the death of David "Dave" Bryant, who passed away 
4on November 13, 2025; and
 
5    WHEREAS, Dave Bryant was born to Edward and Mary Lou 
6Bryant in Chicago on October 6, 1958; he attended Marquette 
7Park School in Chicago until he and his family moved to 
8California Gardens in Blue Island, where he attended St. 
9Benedict's Catholic School; he graduated from Dwight D. 
10Eisenhower High School in Blue Island in 1977, where he 
11started the African Culture Awareness Club and performed as 
12drum major in the school marching band; he later received his 
13associate's degree from South Suburban College; he married 
14Fran Jefferson on June 16, 1984; and
 
15    WHEREAS, Dave Bryant's work experience in the Robbins 
16community began with the Robbins Police Department; as a 
17concerned local resident, he served as a trustee of the 
18Village of Robbins, a commissioner of the Robbins Park 
19District, president of the Block Club, and president of the 
20William Leonard Public Library; he was especially dedicated to 
21serving in his role as advisor to Village Mayor Darren Bryant; 
22and
