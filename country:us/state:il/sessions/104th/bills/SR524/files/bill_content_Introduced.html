SENATE RESOLUTION

 
2    WHEREAS, The members of the Illinois Senate are saddened 
3to learn of the death of Father Daren J. Zehnle of Quincy, who 
4passed away on November 27, 2025; and
 
5    WHEREAS, Father Zehnle, the son of George W. "Bill" and 
6Patricia (Szarka) Zehnle, was born in Quincy on March 26, 
71978; he graduated from Quincy High School in 1996, Quincy 
8University in 2000, and the University of St. Mary of the Lake, 
9Mundelein Seminary in 2005; he was ordained to the Priesthood 
10of Jesus Christ at the Cathedral of the Immaculate Conception 
11in Springfield on May 28, 2005; he later completed graduate 
12studies in canon law at the Pontifical Gregorian University in 
13Rome, Italy from 2013 to 2016; at the time of his passing, he 
14was completing postgraduate studies to receive his Master of 
15Arts in Liturgy at the Liturgical Institute, University of St. 
16Mary of the Lake; and
 
17    WHEREAS, Father Zehnle served as parochial vicar at St. 
18Anthony of Padua Parish in Effingham from July 2005 to 
19September 2009, where he also taught at St. Anthony High 
20School and served as an assistant coach of the boys' soccer, 
21wrestling, and track teams; he became pastor of Sacred Heart 
22Parish in Virden and St. Patrick Parish in Girard in September 
232009 before also becoming pastor at Holy Cross Parish in
