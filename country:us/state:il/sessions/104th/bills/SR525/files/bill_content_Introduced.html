2    WHEREAS, The members of the Illinois Senate are saddened 
3to learn of the death of Philip R. "Phil" Supple of Normal, who 
4passed away on November 22, 2025; and
 
5    WHEREAS, Phil Supple was born to John and Ruth Supple in 
6Danville on February 28, 1952; he served in the United States 
7Navy; after his military service, he earned a degree in 
8communication from Southern Illinois University; and
 
9    WHEREAS, Phil Supple began his career in radio at WJBC, 
10which soon led him into television journalism at WEEK 25, 
11where he eventually became director of the news station; his 
12writing and speaking skills later brought him to State Farm, 
13where he served as director of public relations and played a 
14key role in guiding the company's communications during 
15Hurricane Katrina; he also shared his passion for storytelling 
16as an instructor of communication courses at Bradley 
17University; he retired in 2018; and
 
18    WHEREAS, Phil Supple was a talented guitarist, and he 
19cherished making music, attending concerts, and playing in the 
20praise band at Community United Church of Christ in Morton; in 
21recent years, he worked at Roth Pumpkin Patch in Morton during 
22the autumn months, driving the hayrack ride and the children's
