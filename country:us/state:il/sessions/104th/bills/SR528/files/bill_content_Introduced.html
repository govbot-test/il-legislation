SENATE RESOLUTION

 
2    WHEREAS, The members of the Illinois Senate wish to 
3congratulate James Robinson, a native son of Rockford, on his 
4accomplishments; and
 
5    WHEREAS, James Robinson's football career began at 
6Rockford Lutheran School, where he set Illinois High School 
7Association all-time records for career rushing yards (9,045), 
8rushing touchdowns (158), and total points (948); he then 
9attended Illinois State University, where he finished second 
10all-time in career rushing yards, touchdowns, and all-purpose 
11yards while consistently elevating the reputation of Illinois 
12athletics on the national stage; and
 
13    WHEREAS, James Robinson made NFL history with the 
14Jacksonville Jaguars in 2020, becoming the first undrafted 
15rookie in the common-draft era to record 1,414 scrimmage 
16yards, and this achievement earned him PFWA All-Rookie honors, 
17being named NFL Offensive Rookie of the Month, and being 
18listed on NFL Top 100 Players; since then, he has continued his 
19professional journey with multiple NFL teams, further 
20cementing his reputation as one of the greatest undrafted 
21success stories in league history; and
 
22    WHEREAS, James Robinson's impact has extended beyond the
