and 5    WHEREAS, Ed Ovca was born to John and Mary Ovca in Nokomis 6on August 17, 1930; he graduated from Nokomis High School in 71947; he studied offset lithography at Rankin Trade School in 8St. Louis, Missouri; he married Eleanor Schneider in 1949; and 9    WHEREAS, Ed Ovca began working at Free Press-Progress at 10the age of 11, starting as a floor sweeper and working his way 11up to the press room; he purchased the newspaper in 12partnership with Irene Vandever in 1953; he was subsequently 13named and served as publisher and editor for the next 30 years 14until he sold the business and retired; and 15    WHEREAS, Ed Ovca served on the Nokomis Chamber of 16Commerce, the City of Nokomis Council, and the Nokomis 17Community Unit School District #22 Board; he was also a member 18of Rotary International for more than 40 years; and 19    WHEREAS, Ed Ovca and his wife loved traveling, visiting 20Italy, Germany, France, Austria, Egypt, Russia, and all of the 21United States; they also enjoyed listening to St. Louis   SR0533- 2 -LRB104 16573 ECR 29971 r1Cardinals games on the radio, sharing sandwiches, and playing 2card games with their children; and 3    WHEREAS, Ed Ovca was an avid fisherman, known to many as 4"Captain Ed", who was a member at Sportsman's Lake for many 5years and held a Shelbyville Lake muskie record; and 6    WHEREAS, Ed Ovca made an immeasurable impact on his 7community and those around him; he will be remembered for his 8civic involvement, his patience, his wisdom, and his 9dedication to his family; and 10    WHEREAS, Ed Ovca is survived by his wife of 75 years, 11Eleanor Ovca; his children, Edward (Patricia) Ovca Jr., 12Michael Ovca, Eric Ovca, and Kathy (Kevin) DeWerff; five 13grandchildren; and one great-grandchild; therefore, be it 14    RESOLVED, BY THE SENATE OF THE ONE HUNDRED FOURTH GENERAL 15ASSEMBLY OF THE STATE OF ILLINOIS, that we mourn the passing of 16Edward John "Ed" Ovca and extend our sincere condolences to 17his family, friends, and all who knew and loved him; and be it 18further 19    RESOLVED, That a suitable copy of this resolution be 20presented to the family of Ed Ovca as an expression of our 21deepest sympathy.
