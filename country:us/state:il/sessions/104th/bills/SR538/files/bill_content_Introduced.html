SENATE RESOLUTION

 
2    WHEREAS, The members of the Illinois Senate are saddened 
3to learn of the death of Donald E. "Don" McCleary of Liberty, 
4who passed away on December 3, 2025; and
 
5    WHEREAS, Don McCleary, the son of Harry Clifford and 
6Coletta Mary (Wiewel) McCleary, was born in Quincy on April 7, 
71946; he graduated from Liberty High School in 1964; he 
8married Diana Linda Morley at St. Brigid Catholic Church on 
9August 16, 1969; and
 
10    WHEREAS, Don McCleary was a lifelong farmer, and he always 
11appreciated the blessing of a good rain on the crops; and
 
12    WHEREAS, Don McCleary was a member of St. Peter Catholic 
13Church in Quincy and a former 70-year member of St. Brigid 
14Catholic Church in Liberty; he was a member of the Adams County 
15Farm Bureau, the Liberty Education Foundation, the Adams 
16County Soil and Water Conservation Committee, and the former 
17Adams County Pork Producers; he was a member of the Liberty 
18Lions Club for 44 years, and he served on Liberty Community 
19District #2 School Board for 34 years and the Adams County 
20Board for six years; he also served as a former president of 
21St. Brigid Church's Holy Name Society and as chairman of the 
22Building and Grounds Committee; and
