Full Text of SR0544 Illinois General Assembly ILGA.GOV SR0544 - 104th General Assembly SR0544LRB104 16756 MST 30164 r1SENATE RESOLUTION 2    WHEREAS, The members of the Illinois Senate are saddened 3to learn of the death of Beverly (Kall) Sussman; and 4    WHEREAS, Beverly Sussman was a dedicated teacher in School 5District 96 for 26 years and a Presidential Award Winner in 6Science; and 7    WHEREAS, Beverly Sussman was village president of Buffalo 8Grove for eight years; prior to that, she was a trustee for 10 9years; she noted the creation of the Lake-Cook Corridor Plan 10and Market Study as a village accomplishment of which she was 11most proud; and 12    WHEREAS, Beverly Sussman was a commissioner of the Rick 13Kahen Commission for Residents with Disabilities for 15 years; 14and 15    WHEREAS, Beverly Sussman enriched every community she 16touched, always wanting to help people, whether it was 17children in the schools or residents of the village; as a 18devoted teacher turned tireless public servant, her spirit 19brightened countless lives and will continue to inspire those 20who knew her; and   SR0544- 2 -LRB104 16756 MST 30164 r1    WHEREAS, Beverly Sussman was the beloved wife of 65 years 2of Martin W. Sussman; the loving mother of Richard (Marci), 3Howard, and Robert (Noa) Sussman; the proud grandmother of 4Samuel, Dana, Maxwell, Rebecca, Rebecca "Rivky", Chana Michla, 5Eliana, and Nechama Leeba; the dear sister of Gladys Goldsmith 6and the late Vera Winitch; and the adored daughter of the late 7Samuel and Rebecca Kall; therefore, be it 8    RESOLVED, BY THE SENATE OF THE ONE HUNDRED FOURTH GENERAL 9ASSEMBLY OF THE STATE OF ILLINOIS, that we mourn the passing of 10Beverly (Kall) Sussman and extend our sincere condolences to 11her family, friends, and all who knew and loved her; and be it 12further 13    RESOLVED, That a suitable copy of this resolution be 14presented to the family of Beverly (Kall) Sussman as a symbol 15of our respect and esteem.
