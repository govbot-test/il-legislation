SENATE RESOLUTION

 
2    WHEREAS, The members of the Illinois Senate are saddened 
3to learn of the death of Hal I. Smith of Springfield, who 
4passed away on December 3, 2025; and
 
5    WHEREAS, Hal Smith was born in Springfield to William H. 
6and Betty Rose (Davis) Smith on November 22, 1950; he 
7graduated from Springfield High School in 1968 and earned a 
8Bachelor of Arts in Business from Sangamon State University in 
91972 and a Master of Arts in Public Administration in 1976; and
 
10    WHEREAS, Hal Smith's career began with a vital role on the 
11U.S. Senate staff for Senator Charles H. Percy; afterward, he 
12committed over 30 years in the private sector to three global 
13gaming and entertainment companies; when the Lincoln National 
14Heritage Area was created and funded in 2008, he served as the 
15executive director of the Looking for Lincoln Heritage 
16Coalition; though he attempted retirement in 2017, his 
17expertise and passion for serving others drew him back to the 
18work force where he offered his time and skills to Lincoln 
19College and then to the office of Congressman Darin LaHood; he 
20became the executive director for development at the SIU 
21Foundation at the School of Medicine in 2019, before retiring 
22fully in 2024; and
