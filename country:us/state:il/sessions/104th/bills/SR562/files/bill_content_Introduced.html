SENATE RESOLUTION

 
2    WHEREAS, The members of the Illinois Senate are saddened 
3to learn of the death of JoAnne Fields Richardson of Peoria, 
4who passed away on December 28, 2025; and
 
5    WHEREAS, JoAnne Richardson, the daughter of Monroe Austin 
6Fields and Jessie Lee (Higgins) Fields, was born in 
7Humansville, Missouri on August 21, 1931; she graduated from 
8Humansville High School and St. John's School of Nursing in 
9Springfield, Missouri, where she received her registered 
10nursing degree in 1952; she married Robert J. Richardson, 
11D.D.S., M.D., in Kansas City, Missouri on February 19, 1955; 
12they moved to Peoria, Illinois in July 1958, where they 
13established her husband's plastic surgery practice and raised 
14their family; and
 
15    WHEREAS, JoAnne Richardson worked at St. Luke's Hospital 
16and the Veterans Hospital in Kansas City, Missouri, where she 
17met her husband; after moving to Illinois, she became very 
18active in Peoria, serving as a volunteer tour guide at the 
19Allied Agency Center for 10 years, a volunteer with the Peoria 
20Medical Society Alliance at Bel-Wood Nursing Home since 1971, 
21a driver for the Visiting Nurse Association Meals on Wheels 
22program for 20 years, a Girl Scout leader, and a volunteer at 
23the Salvation Army for 20 years, during which she assisted the

1summer camp program, helped with the Red Stocking Christmas 
2Basket applications, and received the 1983 Salvation Army 
3"Others" Award; and
 
4    WHEREAS, JoAnne Richardson was active in the Peoria 
5District Dental Auxiliary, serving as president from 1964 to 
61965, and the Peoria Medical Auxiliary, now known as the 
7Peoria Medical Society Alliance, serving as president from 
81971 to 1972, becoming an honorary member in 1997, and 
9receiving the Charlotte Barbour Award in 1998; she was also an 
10active member and past officer of the Daughters of the 
11American Revolution (DAR), PEO, and the Peoria Women's Civic 
12Federation, formerly known as the Peoria Women's Club; and
 
13    WHEREAS, JoAnne Richardson was a faithful attendee, 
14member, and devout volunteer at First Federated Church, and 
15also First Presbyterian Church for a time, since 1958; she was 
16a former chairman of the First Federated Church Board of 
17Deacons, co-chairman of the Building and Grounds Committee, a 
18founding member of the Stained-Glass Restoration Committee and 
19Fund, and a member of the church's executive board; further, 
20she participated in the Women's Club and assisted with the 
21children's programs; and
 
22    WHEREAS, JoAnne Richardson enjoyed gardening, both flowers 
23and vegetables, and she bestowed her love of gardening and
