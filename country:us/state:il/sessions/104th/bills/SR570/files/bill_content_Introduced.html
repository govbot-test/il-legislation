SENATE RESOLUTION

 
2    WHEREAS, The members of the Illinois Senate are saddened 
3to learn of the death of Robert "Bob" Pulford, who passed away 
4on January 5, 2026; and
 
5    WHEREAS, Bob Pulford was born in Newton Robinson, Ontario, 
6Canada to Joseph and Lillian Pulford on March 31, 1936; and
 
7    WHEREAS, Bob Pulford began his hockey journey playing 
8junior hockey for the Toronto Marlboros, helping them win 
9back-to-back Memorial Cup championships in 1955 and 1956; and
 
10    WHEREAS, Bob Pulford joined the National Hockey League in 
111956, playing 14 seasons with the Toronto Maple Leafs and then 
12finishing his playing career with the Los Angeles Kings for a 
13total of 1,079 games; he was known for his two-way play and as 
14a stalwart penalty killer and a clutch performer; he was part 
15of four Stanley Cup-winning teams (1962, 1963, 1964, 1967) 
16with the Maple Leafs; and
 
17    WHEREAS, Bob Pulford took pride in his education and 
18graduated with a Bachelor of Arts from McMaster University in 
191963 while playing full time in the NHL; and
 
20    WHEREAS, Bob Pulford transitioned seamlessly into coaching
