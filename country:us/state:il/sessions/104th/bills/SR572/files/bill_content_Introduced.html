2    WHEREAS, The members of the Illinois Senate wish to 
3recognize Adwa Day on the occasion of the 130th anniversary of 
4the Battle of Adwa on March 2, 2026; and
 
5    WHEREAS, Adwa Day is an Ethiopian national holiday 
6celebrated on March 2nd each year by more than 130 million 
7people in Ethiopia and throughout the Ethiopian Diaspora; and
 
8    WHEREAS, Adwa Day commemorates the pivotal Battle of Adwa, 
9where a militia of more than 100,000 men and women decisively 
10fought off European colonialism in the mountain town of Adwa, 
11Ethiopia on March 2, 1896; and
 
12    WHEREAS, Emperor Menelik II and his shrewd wife, Empress 
13Taitu, successfully united the ethnic provincial groups of 
14Tigray, Gonder, Wello, Oromo, Gojjam, and parts of modern-day 
15Eritrea, people spread across more than 1,000 miles of 
16differing altitudes and terrain, during a time of ethnic 
17division in order to defend Ethiopia's freedom and 
18independence; and
 
19    WHEREAS, Emperor Menelik II miraculously mobilized troops 
20from all over Ethiopia, while Empress Taitu created the 
21blueprint that led to the overwhelming defeat of the army of
