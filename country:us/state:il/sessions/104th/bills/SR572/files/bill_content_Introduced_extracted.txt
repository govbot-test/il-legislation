and 12    WHEREAS, Emperor Menelik II and his shrewd wife, Empress 13Taitu, successfully united the ethnic provincial groups of 14Tigray, Gonder, Wello, Oromo, Gojjam, and parts of modern-day 15Eritrea, people spread across more than 1,000 miles of 16differing altitudes and terrain, during a time of ethnic 17division in order to defend Ethiopia's freedom and 18independence; and 19    WHEREAS, Emperor Menelik II miraculously mobilized troops 20from all over Ethiopia, while Empress Taitu created the 21blueprint that led to the overwhelming defeat of the army of   SR0572- 2 -LRB104 17016 LAW 30431 r1the Kingdom of Italy, which was seeking to establish a 2colonial empire in East Africa at the time; and 3    WHEREAS, As Ethiopian men served in cavalries and 4infantries while armed with rifles, spears, and swords, 5Ethiopian women secured water and food, supplied medical care 6to the injured, acted as prison guards and morale boosters, 7provided intelligence as spies, and walked with the armies for 8over 600 miles to protect and defend their country as a family, 9ensuring Ethiopia's victory; and 10    WHEREAS, Ethiopia holds the distinction as the only 11African nation to remain independent and sovereign, never 12colonized, and officially recognized by global powers 13throughout the 19th and 20th centuries; and 14    WHEREAS, Emperor Menelik II and Empress Taitu continue to 15serve as powerful and ineradicable symbols of freedom for 16Black and African peoples globally and as paragons of African 17resistance to colonialism; and 18    WHEREAS, Adwa Day is further commemorated with a number of 19songs, including "Tikur Sew" by Teddy Afro and "Taitu" by 20Yegna, featuring Aster Aweke, which were written to celebrate 21the great military victory and the Adwa Victory Memorial in 22Ethiopia; and   SR0572- 3 -LRB104 17016 LAW 30431 r1    WHEREAS, Adwa Day is observed by many in Illinois due to 2the State being home to a sizable Ethiopian and Eritrean 3population, such as the 7th District of the Illinois State 4Senate; therefore, be it 5    RESOLVED, BY THE SENATE OF THE ONE HUNDRED FOURTH GENERAL 6ASSEMBLY OF THE STATE OF ILLINOIS, that we recognize Adwa Day 7on the 130th anniversary of the Battle of Adwa on March 2, 82026, as it serves as a source of inspiration for all 9freedom-loving people in the State of Illinois and for 10generations to come; and be it further 11    RESOLVED, That we recognize Ethiopian women for their 12instrumental involvement in the Battle of Adwa, whose actions 13secured their country's freedom and independence.
