Hawley Book Club and Fourth Monday Bridge Club; and 2    WHEREAS, Penny Radley was preceded in death by her 3parents; her sister, Christine Parks; and her niece, Shauna 4Parks; and 5    WHEREAS, Penny Radley is survived by her husband; her 6children, Colonel Laura J. Radley, USAF (Ret) and Justin P. 7(Jeanne) Radley; her grandchildren, Kathrine Radley and 8Johnathan Baymiller Radley; her brothers, Spandau Parks and 9Richard (Rebecca LaHood) Parks; her grandnephew, Richard 10(Rose) Parks Jr.; and her grandnieces, Alexa Hightower, Kemry 11Hightower, and Mykai Parks; therefore, be it 12    RESOLVED, BY THE SENATE OF THE ONE HUNDRED FOURTH GENERAL 13ASSEMBLY OF THE STATE OF ILLINOIS, that we mourn the passing of 14Penelope A. "Penny" Radley and extend our sincere condolences 15to her family, friends, and all who knew and loved her; and be 16it further 17    RESOLVED, That a suitable copy of this resolution be 18presented to the family of Penny Radley as an expression of our 19deepest sympathy.
