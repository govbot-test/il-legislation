and 7    WHEREAS, George Jacob was preceded in death by his father 8and his brothers, Walter and Paul Jacob; and 9    WHEREAS, George Jacob is survived by his wife, Jeannine; 10his children, George, Walter "Jake", Katherine, Thomas, and 11Bennett;
