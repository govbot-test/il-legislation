and 7    WHEREAS, Illinois medicolegal professionals are entrusted 8with the accurate and impartial investigation of deaths, the 9determination of cause and manner of death, and the 10application of scientific principles in support of the justice 11system; and 12    WHEREAS, These professionals are routinely required to 13respond to death scenes, mass fatality incidents, public 14health emergencies, natural disasters, and acts of violence 15alongside law enforcement, fire service, emergency medical 16services, and other public safety personnel; and 17    WHEREAS, Despite their essential on-scene and emergency 18response responsibilities, medicolegal professionals are not 19uniformly recognized as first responders, notwithstanding 20their exposure to hazardous environments, traumatic scenes, 21biohazards, and significant occupational stressors; and   SR0583- 2 -LRB104 19106 ECR 32551 r1    WHEREAS, Acknowledging medicolegal professionals as first 2responders affirms the critical nature of their work, supports 3parity in professional recognition and resources, and enhances 4coordinated emergency response, responder wellness, and public 5safety outcomes; and 6    WHEREAS, The National Association of Medical Examiners 7(NAME) and the International Association of Coroners and 8Medical Examiners (IACME) have each issued position statements 9recognizing and affirming the essential role medicolegal 10professionals play in death investigation, public health, the 11administration of justice, and emergency response; and 12    WHEREAS, Recognizing medicolegal professionals fosters 13public awareness of the indispensable role these professionals 14play in protecting public health, ensuring accountability, and 15upholding justice across the State of Illinois; therefore, be 16it 17    RESOLVED, BY THE SENATE OF THE ONE HUNDRED FOURTH GENERAL 18ASSEMBLY OF THE STATE OF ILLINOIS, that we recognize 19medicolegal professionals, and we thank them for their 20invaluable service.
