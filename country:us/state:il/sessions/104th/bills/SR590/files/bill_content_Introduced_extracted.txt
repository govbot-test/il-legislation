and 5    WHEREAS, Jeff Fred, the son of Charles Eugene Fred and 6Betty Lou (Kempen) Fred, was born in Kankakee on September 16, 71960; he married Brenda Suzanne Foster on May 19, 1995; and 8    WHEREAS, Jeff Fred held a lifelong passion for music, 9especially for playing the drums; he played in multiple bands 10over the years, including with local worship bands and as part 11of the worship team at Christ's Church in Effingham; in recent 12years, he also enjoyed refurbishing old drum sets; and 13    WHEREAS, Jeff Fred loved working with his hands, including 14rebuilding vehicles, such as his yellow 1975 CJ5 Rubigade, 15model building, drawing, glass etching, and woodworking; in 16more recent years, he also enjoyed grilling and smoking meats, 17completing bucket list travel trips with his wife, and hosting 18and participating in couples' life groups; his greatest joy 19came from spending time with his grandchildren, who brought 20him endless pride and happiness; and 21    WHEREAS, Jeff Fred was a hardworking, dedicated man known   SR0590- 2 -LRB104 20429 ECR 33893 r1for his humble and kind spirit; he will be remembered for his 2unwavering faith, his love of music, and his devotion to his 3family; and 4    WHEREAS, Jeff Fred was preceded in death by his parents 5and his brother, Mark Fred; and 6    WHEREAS, Jeff Fred is survived by his wife of 30 years, 7Brenda Fred; his daughters, Sara (Jessy Draves) Fred, Kaylee 8(Brandon) Loy, and Jennifer (Connor Greene) Fred; five 9grandchildren; his siblings, Kathy (John) Adams and Todd 10(Jane) Fred; and many nieces, nephews, and extended family 11members; therefore, be it 12    RESOLVED, BY THE SENATE OF THE ONE HUNDRED FOURTH GENERAL 13ASSEMBLY OF THE STATE OF ILLINOIS, that we mourn the passing of 14Jeffrey William "Jeff" Fred and extend our sincere condolences 15to his family, friends, and all who knew and loved him; and be 16it further 17    RESOLVED, That a suitable copy of this resolution be 18presented to the family of Jeff Fred as an expression of our 19deepest sympathy.
