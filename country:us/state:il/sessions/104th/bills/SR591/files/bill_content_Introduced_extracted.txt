he returned 10to the sport in June 1958, posting a 10-2 record for the 11Triple-A minor league team the Denver Bears; he was then 12called up by the New York Yankees, making his major league 13debut by pitching three and one-third hitless innings of 14relief against the Kansas City Athletics; and 15    WHEREAS, Zackie Monroe pitched an additional 20 games for 16the Yankees, making six starts and registering a complete game 17victory in September 1958 against the Boston Red Sox at Yankee 18Stadium; he played in the 1958 World Series, where the Yankees 19ultimately won the team's 18th World Series Championship; he 20pitched three games in relief for the Yankees in 1959 before 21being sent down to the Triple-A league; he remained in the 22minor leagues before retiring in 1962; and   SR0591- 2 -LRB104 20441 ECR 33906 r1    WHEREAS, During his Major League Baseball career, Zackie 2Monroe compiled a 4-2 record with one save, one complete game, 3a 3.38 earned run average, and 19 strikeouts in 24 4appearances; amidst the 61 and one-third innings pitched, he 5permitted 60 hits and 29 bases on balls; and 6    WHEREAS, Zackie Monroe will be remembered for his 7influence and legacy on the game of baseball; therefore, be it 8    RESOLVED, BY THE SENATE OF THE ONE HUNDRED FOURTH GENERAL 9ASSEMBLY OF THE STATE OF ILLINOIS, that we mourn the passing of 10Zachary Charles "Zackie" Monroe and extend our sincere 11condolences to his family, friends, and all who knew and loved 12him; and be it further 13    RESOLVED, That a suitable copy of this resolution be 14presented to the family of Zackie Monroe as an expression of 15our deepest sympathy.
