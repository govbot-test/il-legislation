SENATE RESOLUTION

 
2    WHEREAS, The members of the Illinois Senate are saddened 
3to learn of the death of Robert "Bob" Salzer of East Peoria, 
4who passed away on January 22, 2026; and
 
5    WHEREAS, Bob Salzer was born to Trevelyn and Margaret 
6Edwards Salzer in Weldon on January 7, 1936; he married 
7Marilyn L. Wageman in Peoria on June 22, 1957, and she preceded 
8him in death on January 24, 2016; and
 
9    WHEREAS, Bob Salzer was a crane operator for International 
10Union of Operating Engineers Local #649, retiring in 1999 
11after 43 years; during his career, he worked on many projects, 
12including such notable builds as Peoria's Twin Towers and the 
13Murray Baker Bridge, and he was noted for his perpetual 
14willingness to share his skills with others; and
 
15    WHEREAS, Bob Salzer's faith was the foundation of his 
16life; he served his church, supported charitable causes, led 
17Bible studies, and always found time to read God's word for 
18himself; he was a longtime member of Bay View Baptist Church in 
19Washington; and
 
20    WHEREAS, Bob Salzer was an avid Cardinals fan, loved 
21working on word puzzles, and enjoyed exercising at Eastside
