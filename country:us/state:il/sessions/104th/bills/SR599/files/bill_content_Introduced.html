SENATE RESOLUTION

 
2    WHEREAS, The members of the Illinois Senate are saddened 
3to learn of the death of Christian Jon "CJ" Law, who passed 
4away on December 14, 2025; and
 
5    WHEREAS, CJ Law, the son of Demetrius and LaTanya Law, was 
6born on January 8, 2002; from a young age, his love for animals 
7was evident as he lovingly cared for a bearded dragon, a cat, 
8and his dog, Wheatie; he also possessed a passion for 
9athletics, earning USATF National Junior Olympics Indoor 
10All-American honors in the 400-meter race in 2016 and 
11competing with distinction for the Zephyrs Track Club, the St. 
12Rita High School football team, and the Tinley Park High 
13School track and field team, where he was part of the school's 
14first-ever qualifying 4x100 and 4x200 relay teams; and
 
15    WHEREAS, CJ Law earned his Bachelor of Science in Animal 
16and Veterinary Sciences from Tuskegee University, where he was 
17a member of the football team, earned SIAC All-Academic 
18Honors, and became a legacy member of Kappa Alpha Psi 
19Fraternity, Inc., Gamma Epsilon Chapter, in 2022; during his 
20college career, he was recognized as a Pre-Vet Med Scholar and 
21an honors student, and he worked at VCA Animal Hospital, the 
22Tuskegee Biology Laboratory, and the Tuskegee University Farm; 
23and
