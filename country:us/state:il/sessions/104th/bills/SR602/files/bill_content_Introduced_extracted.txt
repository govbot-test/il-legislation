"Boz" O'Brien of Chicago; and 4    WHEREAS, Boz O'Brien was a beloved South Side Chicagoan, 5community leader, and proprietor who was known for his 6amiability, generosity, sharp wit, and unwavering commitment 7to his family, friends, and community, qualities that defined 8both his personal life and his professional endeavors; and 9    WHEREAS, Through his vision and hospitality, Boz O'Brien 10established Reilly's Daughter, which became far more than a 11neighborhood pub, growing into a place where Irish tradition 12lived and was shared across generations, where families 13gathered, and where every patron was treated like family the 14moment they walked through the door; and 15    WHEREAS, Boz O'Brien believed that heritage was strongest 16when celebrated together, and under his guidance, Reilly's 17Daughter became home to cherished traditions, including the 18beloved Irish soda bread contest, where family recipes, 19friendly rivalry, and community pride were joyfully shared, 20strengthening bonds among neighbors and preserving Irish 21culture on the South Side of Chicago; and   SR0602- 2 -LRB104 20531 ECR 34006 r1    WHEREAS, Boz O'Brien made Reilly's Daughter a gathering 2place for Fighting Irish faithful, where Notre Dame football 3Saturdays were observed with near-religious devotion, spirited 4debate, and the enduring belief that next year was always 5going to be the year; and 6    WHEREAS, Under Boz's O'Brien's watch, Reilly's Daughter 7became a place for every season of life, where families 8gathered to mourn a loss, celebrate milestones, and to mark 921st birthdays with pride and laughter, always with his steady 10presence and watchful eye to ensure that all felt welcome and 11made it home safely; and 12    WHEREAS, Boz O'Brien possessed the rare gift of bringing 13people together, and Reilly's Daughter reflected that gift by 14welcoming neighbors, public servants, tradesmen, first-time 15visitors, and lifelong regulars alike, all drawn in by his 16warmth, humor, and unmistakable South Side Irish spirit; and 17    WHEREAS, When Boz O'Brien brought Reilly's Daughter to 18Chicago Midway International Airport, he ensured that 19travelers passing through the city could experience a true 20taste of South Side Irish hospitality, sometimes before they 21even found their gate, demonstrating his belief that no one 22should leave Chicago without a proper welcome or a proper 23pint; and 6    WHEREAS; Boz O'Brien's life and work left an enduring mark 7on generations of Illinois residents, and he will be 8remembered by the many individuals whose lives were enriched 9by his kindness and magnanimity; therefore, be it 10    RESOLVED, BY THE SENATE OF THE ONE HUNDRED FOURTH GENERAL 11ASSEMBLY OF THE STATE OF ILLINOIS, that we mourn the passing of 12James L. "Boz" O'Brien, and extend our sincere condolences to 13his family, friends, and all who knew and loved him; and be it 14further 15    RESOLVED, That a suitable copy of this resolution be 16presented to the family of Boz O'Brien as an expression of our 17deepest sympathy.
