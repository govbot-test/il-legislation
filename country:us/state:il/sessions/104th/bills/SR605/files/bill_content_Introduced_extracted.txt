Jesse on April 20, 1957, and he 8preceded her in death on April 2, 2001; and 9    WHEREAS, Ethel Jesse worked as a CNA at Pleasant Hill 10Village in Girard, where she provided loving care for the 11residents for more than 28 years; and 12    WHEREAS, Ethel Jesse was an excellent cook and enjoyed 13baking and candy making, and some of her family's favorite 14recipes included her vegetable soup, sloppy taters, Texas 15sheet cake, and divinity and buckeyes candy; and 16    WHEREAS, Ethel Jesse loved her family fiercely and enjoyed 17attending family gatherings; she will be remembered for her 18tenacity and determination to live life on her terms; and 19    WHEREAS, Ethel Jesse was preceded in death by her parents 20and 12 siblings; and   SR0605- 2 -LRB104 20545 LAW 34022 r1    WHEREAS, Ethel Jesse is survived by her children, Debra 2(Don) Giger, Edward (Kim) Jesse, and Donna Maybury; her 3grandchildren, Todd (Rebekah) Giger, Samantha (Greg) Pelcak, 4Trevor (Erica) Giger, Trent (Kimi) Giger, Sarah Jesse, and 5Brayden (Dakotah) Maybury; six great-grandchildren; four bonus 6great-grandchildren; three great-great-grandchildren, with 7one more expected; and her friends and acquaintances; 8therefore, be it 9    RESOLVED, BY THE SENATE OF THE ONE HUNDRED FOURTH GENERAL 10ASSEMBLY OF THE STATE OF ILLINOIS, that we mourn the passing of 11Ethel Lucille Jesse and extend our sincere condolences to her 12family, friends, and all who knew and loved her; and be it 13further 14    RESOLVED, That a suitable copy of this resolution be 15presented to the family of Ethel Jesse as an expression of our 16deepest sympathy.
