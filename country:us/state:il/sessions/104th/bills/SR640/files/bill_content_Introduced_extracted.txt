and   SR0640- 2 -LRB104 21016 RMO 34911 r1    WHEREAS, Pakistan Day will enhance awareness of the 2history, culture, and contributions of the Pakistani American 3community and allow all residents of Illinois to join in 4acknowledging the values of liberty, justice, and equality 5that resonate in both American and Pakistani cultures; 6therefore, be it 7    RESOLVED, BY THE SENATE OF THE ONE HUNDRED FOURTH GENERAL 8ASSEMBLY OF THE STATE OF ILLINOIS, that that we declare March 923, 2026 as Pakistan Day in the State of Illinois; and be it 10further 11    RESOLVED, That we encourage the people of Illinois to 12celebrate this day with events and activities honoring the 13contributions of Pakistani Americans to our great State and to 14learn more about the rich Pakistani heritage while fostering 15an appreciation for this ancient culture among younger 16generations; and be it further 17    RESOLVED, That a suitable copy of this resolution be 18presented to the Pakistan Consulate General in Chicago as a 19symbol of our respect and esteem.
