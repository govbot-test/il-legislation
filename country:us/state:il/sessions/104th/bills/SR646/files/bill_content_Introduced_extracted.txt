(Ondrla) Dunn of Oak Park, 4who passed away on October 11, 2025 at the age of 83; and 5    WHEREAS, Alice Dunn was born on September 11, 1942; and 6    WHEREAS, Alice Dunn devoted her life to educating 7children, beginning at Hiawatha School in Berwyn until she 8became a mother in 1974; and 9    WHEREAS, Alice Dunn stayed home to raise her daughters 10before returning to education through the Park District of Oak 11Park's preschool program, and she concluded her career in 12education while teaching second grade at Ascension School in 13Oak Park after more than 20 years; and 14    WHEREAS, Alice Dunn was an avid gardener with a fondness 15for orchids, possessing the skill set to keep the most 16beautiful of them in bloom; and 17    WHEREAS, Alice Dunn enjoyed keeping her door open with a 18warm welcome and a tray of homemade cookies, which she also 19used as bait to ensure visits from her grandsons and all the 20children in the neighborhood; and   SR0646- 2 -LRB104 20980 LAW 34844 r1    WHEREAS, Alice Dunn, a diehard White Sox fan, watched 2every game the team played, even during the 2024 season; and 3    WHEREAS, Alice Dunn was also a longtime parishioner at 4Ascension Church, where she enjoyed being part of the church 5community and attending Mass every Sunday; and 6    WHEREAS, Alice Dunn is survived by her husband of 53 7years, Robert A. Dunn; her daughters, Julieann (Michael) 8Hedges and Megan Dunn; her grandsons, Brady, Jack and Dylan 9Hedges; her brother, Thomas (Christine) Ondrla; her nieces and 10nephews, Jennifer, Joseph, Mark, Alex, Caitlin, Moira, and 11Robert; her friend and childhood soulmate, Sharon Wisinski; 12and countless others; therefore, be it 13    RESOLVED, BY THE SENATE OF THE ONE HUNDRED FOURTH GENERAL 14ASSEMBLY OF THE STATE OF ILLINOIS, that we mourn the passing of 15Alice M. (Ondrla) Dunn and extend our sincere condolences to 16her family, friends, and all who knew and loved her; and be it 17further 18    RESOLVED, That a suitable copy of this resolution be 19presented to the family of Alice Dunn as an expression of our 20deepest sympathy.
