and 5    WHEREAS, The Veterans Assistance Commission of Lake County 6was founded on May 27, 1926 under the authority granted by the 7Illinois Military Veterans Assistance Act to create a 8county-based system of veteran support governed by veterans 9themselves, providing the just, necessary, and needed services 10for all veterans and their families, reflecting Illinois' 11enduring commitment to those who wore the uniform; and 17    WHEREAS, The Veterans Assistance Commission of Lake 18County, throughout its distinguished history, has assisted 19thousands of veterans from all branches of the United States 20Armed Forces, including those who served during World War II, 21the Korean War, the Vietnam War, the Gulf War, and the Global 22War on Terror; and   SR0662- 2 -LRB104 21039 MST 34977 r1    WHEREAS, The Veterans Assistance Commission of Lake County 2has worked collaboratively with local, state, and federal 3partners, including the United States Department of Veterans 4Affairs and numerous veteran service organizations, to ensure 5veterans receive the benefits and recognition they have 6earned; and 7    WHEREAS, The Veterans Assistance Commission of Lake County 8has demonstrated exemplary stewardship, fiscal responsibility, 9and compassion in addressing the evolving needs of veterans 10over the course of a century; therefore, be it 11    RESOLVED, BY THE SENATE OF THE ONE HUNDRED FOURTH GENERAL 12ASSEMBLY OF THE STATE OF ILLINOIS, that we congratulate the 13Veterans Assistance Commission of Lake County on its 100th 14anniversary; and be it further 15    RESOLVED, That suitable copies of this resolution be 16presented to the Veterans Assistance Commission of Lake County 17in Gurnee and Superintendent Andrew Tangen as a symbol of our 18respect and esteem.
