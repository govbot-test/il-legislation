SENATE RESOLUTION

 
2    WHEREAS, The members of the Illinois Senate are saddened 
3to learn of the death of Scott McFarland of Moline, who passed 
4away on August 2, 2025; and
 
5    WHEREAS, Scott McFarland, the son of Donald and Karla 
6(Pollack) McFarland, was born on August 10, 1983; he achieved 
7the rank of Eagle Scout in Boy Scouts; he married his wife, 
8Amber, at the Unitarian Universalist Congregation of the Quad 
9Cities in Davenport, Iowa on August 23, 2008; and
 
10    WHEREAS, For more than a decade, Scott McFarland led the 
11Serve Illinois Commission on Volunteerism and Community 
12Service (Serve Illinois), a 40-member, bipartisan board 
13appointed by the Governor and administered by the Illinois 
14Department of Human Services to strengthen communities through 
15volunteerism, national service, and recognition; during his 
16tenure, he was noted for caring deeply about the grant 
17programs he administered and all the people behind those 
18programs; and
 
19    WHEREAS, Scott McFarland was a passionate advocate for 
20education and volunteerism; as an elected member of the 
21Springfield School District 186 Board of Education, he pursued 
22initiatives that he felt would improve the lives of all
