SENATE RESOLUTION

 
2    WHEREAS, The members of the Illinois Senate are saddened 
3to learn of the death of Milton Dow "Milt" Kelly of Fisher, who 
4passed away on September 22, 2025; and
 
5    WHEREAS, Milt Kelly, the son of the late Floyd and Iva 
6Kelly, was born in Champaign on March 15, 1949; he graduated 
7from Fisher High School; he received his undergraduate and 
8graduate degrees in music at the University of Illinois, where 
9he played the trumpet in multiple student groups; and
 
10    WHEREAS, Milt Kelly began his career in music education in 
11the Armstrong school district before returning to Fisher to 
12lead its music program; he directed every student band in the 
13district as well as the high school vocal ensembles; under his 
14leadership, these programs thrived, regularly ranking among 
15the top five music programs of comparable size in the State; 
16during his more than 25 years in the district, he shaped the 
17lives of countless students, and he treasured those 
18relationships deeply; he was also a member of The Powell 
19Family, a local musical group that performed traditional and 
20contemporary Christian music, and he taught private lessons; 
21and
 
22    WHEREAS, Milt Kelly coached baseball for several decades;
