2    WHEREAS, The members of the Illinois Senate wish to 
3recognize Sue Bustin on her service as medical caseworker of 
4the Joliet Township Government Office for more than two 
5decades; and
 
6    WHEREAS, Sue Bustin was born and raised in Joliet, 
7graduating from Joliet East High School and obtaining her 
8associate's degree from Joliet Junior College; she proceeded 
9to attend Illinois Masonic Medical Center School of Nursing 
10and earned her Bachelor of Science from the University of 
11Wisconsin; and
 
12    WHEREAS, Sue Bustin was hired as medical caseworker of the 
13Joliet Township Government Office on June 17, 2002, where she

15    WHEREAS, In her role as medical caseworker, Sue Bustin 
16provided Joliet Township residents with information on 
17available community resources and was responsible for 
18assisting residents who were awaiting short- or long-term 
19disability through the Supplemental Security Income (SSI) and 
20Social Security Disability (SSD) programs as well as worker's 
21compensation, personal injury settlements, or were off work 
22due to health issues; through the Joliet Township General
