Full Text of SR0700 Illinois General Assembly ILGA.GOV SR0700 - 104th General Assembly SR0700LRB104 21082 LAW 35214 r1SENATE RESOLUTION 2    WHEREAS, The Assyrian people, a Semitic community who also 3identify as Chaldeans and Syriacs, are the indigenous people 4of Mesopotamia in what is now modern-day Iraq, Syria, Turkey, 5and Iran who possess a history dating back to ancient times; 6and 7    WHEREAS, The Assyrian people have faced systematic 8persecution in their ancestral homeland, including the 9Assyrian Genocide of 1915, which claimed the lives of hundreds 10of thousands, and the Simele Massacre of 1933, where thousands 11were killed by the Iraqi Army; and 12    WHEREAS, The Assyrian community in the United States 13numbers nearly half a million people, with approximately 14100,000 residing in Illinois, the largest concentration in the 15nation; and 16    WHEREAS, Assyrian Americans have shared their rich culture 17and traditions with neighbors and friends while also serving 18as model citizens and public servants; and 19    WHEREAS, The Assyrian people have made significant 20contributions to Illinois and the United States through their 21achievements in the arts, business, media, law, politics,   SR0700- 2 -LRB104 21082 LAW 35214 r1education, medicine, architecture, engineering, and other 2fields, enriching the cultural and economic fabric of this 3State and the nation; and 4    WHEREAS, The Assyrian New Year, known in ancient times as 5Akitu and traditionally celebrated on the first of April, 6marks the arrival of spring and symbolizes rebirth, renewal, 7and the enduring spirit of the Assyrian people; and 8    WHEREAS, The Chicago Assyrian community has expressed 9plans to proudly commemorate the Assyrian New Year 6776 with a 10series of vibrant three-day festivals throughout the City of 11Chicago and the Village of Skokie, where the largest condensed 12Assyrian population resides, in April 2026; therefore, be it 13    RESOLVED, BY THE SENATE OF THE ONE HUNDRED FOURTH GENERAL 14ASSEMBLY OF THE STATE OF ILLINOIS, that we declare April 2026 15as Assyrian Heritage Month in the State of Illinois; and be it 16further 17    RESOLVED, That we commend the Assyrian community in 18Illinois for their enduring contributions, resilience, and 19cultural heritage.
