SENATE RESOLUTION

 
2    WHEREAS, The members of the Illinois Senate are saddened 
3to learn of the death of Blake Eugene Roderick, who passed away 
4on March 27, 2026; and
 
5    WHEREAS, Blake Roderick was born to Donald Eugene and 
6Alice Joanne (Spring) Roderick in Gibson City on January 20, 
71957; he graduated from Lanphier High School in 1975, where he 
8served as student body president; he received a Bachelor of 
9Science in Agricultural Economics from Texas A&M University; 
10he was also a graduate of the Illinois Agricultural Leadership 
11Foundation program, and he was honored to be a Rotary 
12International Paul Harris Fellow; he married Betsy J. Cramsey 
13in 2009; and
 
14    WHEREAS, Blake Roderick began his career with the Sangamon 
15County Farm Bureau in 1981; he became the manager of the Pike 
16County Farm Bureau in December 1981 and assumed joint 
17management of the Scott County Farm Bureau in 1987, 
18successfully shepherding the merger of the two organizations 
19in 2018; he was also instrumental in creation of the Two Rivers 
20Farm Bureau Foundation in 1994; he retired as executive 
21director of the Pike-Scott Farm Bureau in 2022; during his 
22tenure, the organizations he was a part of won many Liberty 
23Bell awards as well as top membership honors and program
