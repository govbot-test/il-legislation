and 5    WHEREAS, The original Belvidere Lions Club was organized 6on March 21, 1939, and a formal charter ceremony was held at 7Immanuel Lutheran Church School on October 4, 1939; and 8    WHEREAS, The Belvidere Lions Club expanded its impact by 9sponsoring the Harvard Lions Club in 1942, and the Club 10sponsored new Lions clubs in Genoa, Kirkland, Marengo, Capron, 11Poplar Grove, and Cherry Valley; and 12    WHEREAS, After being disbanded during World War Two, the 13Belvidere Lions Club was reorganized and officially 14rechartered on April 27, 1946; and 15    WHEREAS, The Belvidere Lions Club has long been known as a 16hands-on, hardworking organization, sponsoring many large and 17memorable projects within the community, including Heritage 18Days, the Boone County Fair, the Fish and Fun competition, and 19more; and 20    WHEREAS, One current initiative of the Belvidere Lions   SR0715- 2 -LRB104 21357 ECR 36258 r1Club is the development of an inclusive, ADA-compliant 2playground that is being designed to remove physical and 3social barriers, ensuring that children of all abilities can 4play and interact together; and 5    WHEREAS, The Belvidere Lions Club has embodied the spirit 6of service, leadership, and compassion that defines Lions 7Clubs International for the past 80 years, supported by a 8strong foundation rooted in volunteerism, integrity, and a 9deep sense of civic responsibility; therefore, be it 10    RESOLVED, BY THE SENATE OF THE ONE HUNDRED FOURTH GENERAL 11ASSEMBLY OF THE STATE OF ILLINOIS, that we congratulate the 12Belvidere Lions Club on the occasion of its 80-year 13anniversary, and we wish the Club all the best in its future 14endeavors; and be it further 15    RESOLVED, That a suitable copy of this resolution be 16presented to the Belvidere Lions Club as a symbol of our esteem 17and respect.
