and 15    WHEREAS, American Airlines has operated commercial service 16at O'Hare International Airport since 1955, and today the 17airport serves as the airline's third-largest global hub and 18primary Midwest connection point; and 19    WHEREAS, American Airlines has the distinction of being 20the first airline to introduce an airline loyalty program, 21known as AAdvantage, which Illinoisans use to fly their 22families to vacation destinations and distant family events;   SR0720- 2 -LRB104 21468 LAW 36658 r1and 2    WHEREAS, American Airlines' centennial is a reminder that 3the airline has bolstered the economy of Chicagoland and the 4State of Illinois, generating more than $16 billion in total 5economic output for the State in Fiscal Year 2024 alone, while 6employing nearly 10,000 team members in the Chicago area and 7contributing to local organizations that help the region 8thrive; and 9    WHEREAS, American Airlines provides Illinois with 10increased connectivity and a broad global network, operating 11more than 525 peak daily departures from five airports in 12Illinois and offering flights to more than 160 destinations 13and 17 countries; and 14    WHEREAS, American Airlines annually carries more than 25 15million passengers through O'Hare International Airport; 16therefore, be it 17    RESOLVED, BY THE SENATE OF THE ONE HUNDRED FOURTH GENERAL 18ASSEMBLY OF THE STATE OF ILLINOIS, that we congratulate 19American Airlines on its 100th anniversary and centennial 20celebration, and we thank the airline for its continued 21commitment to the State of Illinois;
