2    WHEREAS, The members of the Illinois Senate are saddened

5    WHEREAS, Theris Gabinski was born on November 30, 1938; he 
6studied chemistry at Northern Illinois University and achieved 
7a Master of Education from Loyola University Chicago; and
 
8    WHEREAS, Theris Gabinski dedicated his life to service, 
9first as a teacher of math and chemistry before dedicating his

12Alderperson of the Chicago City Council in 1969, a position he 
13held until his retirement in 1998; he served his community as 
14both alderperson and vice mayor from 1988 through 1998 and as 
15ward committeeman from 1988 through his retirement from the 
16political sphere in 2008; and
