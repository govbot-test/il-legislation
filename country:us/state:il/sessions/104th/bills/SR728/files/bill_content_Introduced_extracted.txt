and 6    WHEREAS, Aqsa School graduates have proceeded to attend 7some of the nation's top colleges and universities and have 8contributed meaningfully to communities across the State of 9Illinois and beyond; and 10    WHEREAS, Aqsa School's continued success stands as a 11testament to the dedication of its board, administrators, 12teachers, staff, parents, and students, whose commitment has 13helped shape a legacy of excellence, faith, and service; 14therefore, be it 15    RESOLVED, BY THE SENATE OF THE ONE HUNDRED FOURTH GENERAL 16ASSEMBLY OF THE STATE OF ILLINOIS, that we congratulate Aqsa 17School on achieving its 40th anniversary, and we commend the 18institution for its decades of leadership, innovation, and 19service to the people of Illinois; and be it further 20    RESOLVED, That a suitable copy of this resolution be 21presented to Aqsa School as a symbol of our respect and esteem.
