SENATE RESOLUTION

 
2    WHEREAS, The members of the Illinois Senate are saddened 
3to learn of the death of Sandra Jean Booker, who passed away on 
4August 13, 2024; and
 
5    WHEREAS, Sandra Booker, the daughter of Judge A. Booker 
6and the late Wynona (Bea) Williams-Booker, was born in 
7Coatopa, Alabama on August 19, 1960; she committed to her 
8faith at an early age, joining First Corinthian Missionary 
9Baptist Church in 1970; she graduated from the North Chicago 
10school system in 1979, completed the basic nurse assistant 
11training program at the College of Lake County, and became a 
12licensed certified nursing assistant (CNA) in October 1983; 
13and
 
14    WHEREAS, Sandra Booker began her career as a CNA at 
15Winchester House in 1983 and then transitioned to the Chicago 
16Medical School, now Rosalind Franklin School of Medicine, as a 
17medical assistant; she later served at the Lake County Health 
18Department from 1991 to 2013 in a variety of roles, including 
19home health nursing assistant, community health associate, and 
20senior public health assistant, forming lasting and impactful 
21relationships by working with teen mothers in Stepping 
22Together and with seniors in Home Health; she then worked as a 
23home-based specialist, respite care provider, and
