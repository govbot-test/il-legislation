and 5    WHEREAS, The Rialto Square Theatre in Joliet stands as one 6of the State's most treasured cultural and architectural 7landmarks, first opening its doors in 1926 as a grand movie 8palace and vaudeville venue; and 9    WHEREAS, The Rialto Square Theatre, designed in the 10opulent style of the era, has long been celebrated for its 11remarkable interior, historic significance, and enduring 12beauty, earning recognition as a symbol of artistic excellence 13and community pride; and 14    WHEREAS, The Rialto Square Theatre has served as a vital 15center for the performing arts for 100 years, hosting 16countless performances, concerts, films, and community events 17that have enriched the cultural life of Joliet, Will County, 18and the entire State of Illinois; and 19    WHEREAS, The Rialto Square Theatre has demonstrated 20extraordinary resilience, having been lovingly restored and 21preserved through the dedicated efforts of community leaders,   SR0738- 2 -LRB104 21612 RMO 37078 r1preservationists, and supporters committed to safeguarding its 2legacy for future generations; and 3    WHEREAS, The Rialto Square Theatre continues to inspire 4audiences and foster a shared appreciation for the arts, 5history, and civic engagement, standing as a testament to the 6enduring importance of cultural institutions in strengthening 7communities; therefore, be it 8    RESOLVED, BY THE SENATE OF THE ONE HUNDRED FOURTH GENERAL 9ASSEMBLY OF THE STATE OF ILLINOIS, that we congratulate the 10Rialto Square Theatre on the occasion of its 100th 11anniversary, celebrating a century of artistic achievement, 12historic preservation, and community service; and be it 13further 14    RESOLVED, That we commend all those who have contributed 15to the success and preservation of the Rialto Square Theatre 16over the past century, and we extend our best wishes for its 17continued vitality and success for generations to come; and be 18it further 19    RESOLVED, That a suitable copy of this resolution be 20presented to the representatives of the Rialto Square Theatre 21as a symbol of our respect and esteem.
