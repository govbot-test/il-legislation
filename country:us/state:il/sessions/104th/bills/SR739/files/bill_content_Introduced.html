SENATE RESOLUTION

 
2    WHEREAS, The members of the Illinois Senate are saddened 
3to learn of the death of Gary G. Krueger, who passed away on 
4April 21, 2026; and
 
5    WHEREAS, Gary Krueger, the son of Anthony "Tony" and 
6Glennon (Myers) Krueger, was born in Alton on November 3, 
71950; he graduated from Jersey Community High School in 1968 
8and attended Western Illinois University; he married Rhonda 
9Mary Beiser at Delhi Baptist Church on February 6, 1971, and 
10she preceded him in death on October 26, 2025; and
 
11    WHEREAS, Gary Krueger was a lifelong farmer and later 
12retired as a supervisor with the Illinois Department of 
13Transportation; and
 
14    WHEREAS, Gary Krueger was a member of the Jersey County 
15Board, previously serving as vice chairman and chairman; 
16during his tenure, he also served as chair of the Economic 
17Development and Tourism, Liquor Control, and Personnel 
18Committees; he served as a Mississippi Township trustee and 
19was a member of the Jersey County Board of Review, the Jersey 
20County Rural Water Board, and the Soil and Water Board; he 
21served as chairman of the Jersey County Republican Committee; 
22he was a past member of the Elks Lodge and the Loyal Order of
