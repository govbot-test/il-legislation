SENATE RESOLUTION

 
2    WHEREAS, The members of the Illinois Senate are saddened 
3to learn of the death of Nancy Kay Olsen, who passed away on 
4March 19, 2026; and
 
5    WHEREAS, Nancy Olsen was born to Thomas C. and Flossea K. 
6"Kathryn" (McKinney) Wanless in Springfield on March 11, 1950; 
7she spent her childhood living in various places, including 
8California and Las Vegas, Nevada, before returning to her 
9birthplace during her senior year of high school, and she 
10consequently graduated from Springfield High School before 
11returning to Las Vegas; she married Wm. Richard "Rick" Olsen 
12in Minneapolis, Minnesota on March 22, 1970; and
 
13    WHEREAS, After spending three years in Minneapolis, Nancy 
14Olsen and her husband Rick moved to Williamsville, Illinois to 
15be closer to her family; while there, she continued her 
16education, taking accounting classes at Lincoln Land Community 
17College; she invested her heart and soul into her family, 
18managing various family businesses with a grace and efficiency 
19that was admired by all who knew her, and her work included a 
20nursery at Sangamon Prairie Pork Farm in Williamsville, the 
21Country Corner General Store, previously known as Bud's 
22Corner, in Springfield, and the Lithia Resort in Shelbyville 
23for more than 20 years; and
