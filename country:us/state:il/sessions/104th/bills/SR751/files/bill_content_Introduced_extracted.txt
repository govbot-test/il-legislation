Full Text of SR0751 Illinois General Assembly ILGA.GOV SR0751 - 104th General Assembly SR0751LRB104 21031 LAW 34951 r1SENATE RESOLUTION 2    WHEREAS, The members of the Illinois Senate wish to 3congratulate the Retired Teachers Association of Chicago 4(RTAC) on the occasion of its 100th anniversary; and 5    WHEREAS, Since its founding in 1926, RTAC has maintained 6its mission of protecting retired Chicago Public School (CPS) 7retirees' pensions and ancillary benefits, including access to 8high quality health insurance through the Chicago Teachers 9Pension Fund; and 10    WHEREAS, Beginning in 1972, RTAC secured representation on 11the Chicago Teachers' Pension Fund (CTPF) with a retiree 12trustee, and the pioneer of that position, Edna Fanning, also 13served as president of the association; today, there are three 14retiree trustees serving on the CTPF; and 15    WHEREAS, In 1986, RTAC used a legacy gift from Ferol 16Potter, a former association president, to establish the 17Chicago Retired Teachers Aid Fund, which provides financial 18assistance to retired CPS educators with modest pensions; and 19    WHEREAS, In the early 2000s, RTAC established an internet 20website to provide timely e-alerts to its members in order to 21foster legislative advocacy and address other significant   SR0751- 2 -LRB104 21031 LAW 34951 r1matters of concern regarding the well-being of retired CPS 2educators; and 3    WHEREAS, Beginning in 2013, RTAC began offering 4complimentary seminars for its members in the spring and fall 5regarding taxation, health, legal issues, and other areas of 6concern to retired CPS educators; and 7    WHEREAS, RTAC is also a proud charter member of the 8National Retired Teachers Association (NRTA), which 9successfully lobbied to secure the 1968 anti-age 10discrimination legislation as well as to reverse the Windfall 11Elimination Provision (WEP) and Government Pension Offset 12(GPO) Social Security limitations imposed on public agency 13retirees in 2025; and 14    WHEREAS, Throughout its existence, RTAC has continued to 15advocate on behalf of retired CPS educators; therefore, be it 16    RESOLVED, BY THE SENATE OF THE ONE HUNDRED FOURTH GENERAL 17ASSEMBLY OF THE STATE OF ILLINOIS, that we congratulate the 18Retired Teachers Association of Chicago (RTAC) on achieving 19100 years of service to retired Chicago Public School (CPS) 20educators.
