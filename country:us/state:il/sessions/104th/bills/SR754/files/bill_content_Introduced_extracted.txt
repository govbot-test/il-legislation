and 5    WHEREAS, The Illinois Fire Service Medal of Valor is 6awarded by the Office of the Illinois State Fire Marshal to 7firefighters who demonstrate exceptional courage, bravery, and 8a commitment to protecting others at great personal risk; and 9    WHEREAS, Firefighter-Paramedic Gino Casciola was 10recognized for his extraordinary actions during a commercial 11structure fire at 330 West Lake Street in Addison on May 21, 122025, where crews encountered a working fire in a commercial 13occupancy with smoke showing upon arrival; and 14    WHEREAS, As interior operations began, 15Firefighter-Paramedic Gino Casciola was assigned to the entry 16point to assist with advancing hose lines; during suppression 17efforts, conditions rapidly deteriorated after fire extended 18into a concealed void space above operating crews; heavy fire, 19extreme heat, and zero visibility forced firefighters to the 20floor as they attempted to locate an exit, leading to an 21evacuation being ordered; and   SR0754- 2 -LRB104 21690 LAW 37265 r1    WHEREAS, Upon the order to evacuate, Firefighter-Paramedic 2Gino Casciola maintained his position at the threshold of the 3structure, where he was met with zero-visibility conditions 4and extreme thermal stress; he initially facilitated the 5egress of three interior members who were disoriented and 6searching for the exit; despite repeated orders to retreat 7from the building for his own safety, he remained at the door, 8identifying that two members were still absent; and 9    WHEREAS, Recognizing that the remaining members had not 10emerged and that the window for survival was closing, 11Firefighter-Paramedic Gino Casciola reentered the structure; 12after clearing a blocked exit path, he conducted a rapid 13search in high-heat, zero-visibility conditions and 14successfully located and extracted the first remaining member; 15without hesitation, he reentered the structure a second time, 16located the final missing member, and successfully pulled them 17to safety; and 18    WHEREAS, Firefighter-Paramedic Gino Casciola only exited 19the structure after accounting for all remaining members; 20through his decisive and selfless actions, five members, 21including two lieutenants and three firefighter-paramedics, 22were safely removed from the structure and survived the 23incident without injury; and   SR0754- 3 -LRB104 21690 LAW 37265 r1    WHEREAS, Firefighter-Paramedic Gino Casciola's actions 2exemplified the highest traditions of fire service and reflect 3great credit upon himself and Addison Fire Protection District 4#1; and 5    WHEREAS, Firefighter-Paramedic Gino Casciola will be 6awarded the Illinois Fire Service Medal of Valor at the 7Illinois Fallen Firefighter Memorial and Firefighting Medal of 8Honor Awards Ceremony in Springfield on May 12, 2026; 9therefore, be it 10    RESOLVED, BY THE SENATE OF THE ONE HUNDRED FOURTH GENERAL 11ASSEMBLY OF THE STATE OF ILLINOIS, that we congratulate 12Firefighter-Paramedic Gino Casciola of Addison Fire Protection 13District #1 on being awarded the Illinois Fire Service Medal, 14and we thank him for his service and dedication to the Addison 15community and the State of Illinois; and be it further 16    RESOLVED, That a suitable copy of this resolution be 17presented to Firefighter-Paramedic Gino Casciola as an 18expression of our esteem and respect.
