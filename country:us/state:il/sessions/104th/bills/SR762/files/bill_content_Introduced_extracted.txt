Full Text of SR0762 Illinois General Assembly ILGA.GOV SR0762 - 104th General Assembly SR0762LRB104 21710 RMO 37320 r1SENATE RESOLUTION 2    WHEREAS, The members of the Illinois Senate wish to 3congratulate the Illinois State Archives on the occasion of 4the Illinois Regional Archives Depository (IRAD) system's 50th 5anniversary; and 6    WHEREAS, The IRAD system was established July 1, 1976 as a 7program of the Illinois State Archives to preserve local 8government agency records that possess permanent 9administrative, legal, or historical research value; and 10    WHEREAS, The IRAD system has made local government records 11available to the public; and 12    WHEREAS, The IRAD system consists of seven depositories 13located on the campuses of Eastern Illinois University, 14Illinois State University, Northeastern Illinois University, 15Northern Illinois University, Southern Illinois University, 16Western Illinois University, and the University of Illinois 17Springfield; and 18    WHEREAS, Depositories in the IRAD system are staffed with 19graduate student interns who are provided with training in 20basic archival principles and research skills; and   SR0762- 2 -LRB104 21710 RMO 37320 r1    WHEREAS, The IRAD system has served local government 2agencies by providing safe and secure storage for their 3permanently valuable records; and 4    WHEREAS, The IRAD system has provided reference services 5to thousands of individuals, including scholars, family 6historians, lawyers, historical societies, and journalists; 7therefore, be it 8    RESOLVED, BY THE SENATE OF THE ONE HUNDRED FOURTH GENERAL 9ASSEMBLY OF THE STATE OF ILLINOIS, that we congratulate the 10Illinois State Archives and its Illinois Regional Archives 11Depository (IRAD) system on providing 50 years of outstanding 12service; and be it further 13    RESOLVED, That a suitable copy of this resolution be 14presented to the Illinois State Archives as an expression of 15our esteem and respect.
