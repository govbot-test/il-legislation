Full Text of SR0774 Illinois General Assembly ILGA.GOV SR0774 - 104th General Assembly SR0774LRB104 21711 RMO 37322 r1SENATE RESOLUTION 2    WHEREAS, Our Community Salutes is a national nonprofit 3organization dedicated to recognizing, honoring, and 4supporting high school seniors who have chosen to enlist in 5the Armed Forces of the United States immediately following 6graduation; and 7    WHEREAS, Our Community Salutes was founded by educator and 8former United States Army officer Dr. Kenneth Hartman in 2009 9to provide enlistees and their parents with guidance, 10information, resources, and meaningful community support 11during the transition from civilian life to military service; 12and 13    WHEREAS, Each year, thousands of high school seniors 14across the United States make the honorable decision to enlist 15in the Armed Forces, including the United States Army, Navy, 16Air Force, Marine Corps, Coast Guard, and Space Force, 17demonstrating courage, discipline, and an unwavering 18commitment to duty; and 19    WHEREAS, Our Community Salutes serves enlistees by 20providing recognition and resources that assist in their 21successful transition into military service, ensuring they are 22celebrated and supported by their communities; and   SR0774- 2 -LRB104 21711 RMO 37322 r1    WHEREAS, Our Community Salutes also serves parents, 2guardians, and families by offering information, connection, 3and encouragement as they support their sons and daughters 4during this important life transition; and 5    WHEREAS, Our Community Salutes Chicagoland has 6demonstrated exceptional leadership in Illinois by hosting 7Enlistee Recognition Ceremonies that bring together more than 870 high school enlistees and their families, along with 9educators, veterans, and community partners, to ensure that 10those who choose Active, Guard, and Reserve military service 11are recognized, supported, and connected to a strong local 12network from the moment they enlist; and 13    WHEREAS, Our Community Salutes Chicagoland strengthens 14military family support systems across the region by fostering 15connections among enlistees, families, and veteran and 16community organizations, ensuring support continues beyond the 17ceremony; therefore, be it 18    RESOLVED, BY THE SENATE OF THE ONE HUNDRED FOURTH GENERAL 19ASSEMBLY OF THE STATE OF ILLINOIS, that we commend and 20recognize Our Community Salutes for its outstanding service in 21honoring high school military enlistees and supporting their 22families and communities; and be it further   SR0774- 3 -LRB104 21711 RMO 37322 r1    RESOLVED, That a suitable copy of this resolution be 2presented to Our Community Salutes as a symbol of our esteem 3and respect.
