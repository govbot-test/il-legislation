SENATE RESOLUTION

 
2    WHEREAS, The members of the Illinois Senate are saddened 
3to learn of the death of Howard Lane Beagles, who passed away 
4on April 13, 2026; and
 
5    WHEREAS, Howard Beagles was born to Eugene and Frances 
6(McGurk) Beagles in Indianapolis, Indiana on July 9, 1928; 
7during the era of the Great Depression, he went to live with 
8his aunt and uncle, Margaret "Maudie" (McGurk) and Guy 
9Briscoe, in Springfield, Illinois, where he was raised and 
10continued to live for many decades; he served in the United 
11States Navy beginning in the spring of 1946, refurbishing 
12battleships in California at the end of World War II; 
13following his service, he returned home and married Mary Anne 
14Renfro at the Cathedral of the Immaculate Conception in 
15Springfield on February 2, 1948, and she preceded him in death 
16after 62 years of marriage on November 9, 2010; and
 
17    WHEREAS, Howard Beagles, a decent and humble man, worked 
18very hard to take care of his family; he was employed at the 
19Allis Chalmers Factory for years and then served the 
20Springfield Fire Department for three decades beginning in 
211960, retiring as fire marshal 30 years later; his commitment 
22to public safety and his colleagues earned him deep respect 
23throughout the community; and

1    WHEREAS, Howard Beagles was baptized into the Catholic

10spending time with family and friends in retirement, visiting 
11his children across Illinois and Tennessee; he moved in with

13later divided his time between their home, the home of his son 
14and daughter-in-law, Gerry and Cindy, in Palos Heights, and 
15the home of his daughter, Anne, in Murfreesboro, Tennessee; 
16and
 
17    WHEREAS, Howard Beagles' life was defined by faith,

19kindness, humor, laughter, and storytelling; and

21Barry Joseph (widow, April) Beagles; and
