and 17    WHEREAS, Howard Beagles, a decent and humble man, worked 18very hard to take care of his family; he was employed at the 19Allis Chalmers Factory for years and then served the 20Springfield Fire Department for three decades beginning in 211960, retiring as fire marshal 30 years later;
