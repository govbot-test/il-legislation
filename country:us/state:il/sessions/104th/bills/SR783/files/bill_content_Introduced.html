SENATE RESOLUTION

 
2    WHEREAS, The members of the Illinois Senate wish to 
3congratulate the City of Harvard on the 85th anniversary of 
4its annual Harvard Milk Days festival; and
 
5    WHEREAS, Planning for the first Harvard Milk Days festival 
6began under Harvard Chamber of Commerce President Frank W. 
7Lanning, owner of Lanning Bag Company, in 1941 and was 
8finalized by President R.J. Goll in 1942, when the festival 
9became a reality with the help of Earl McIntyre and Vern Gay; 
10and
 
11    WHEREAS, The first Harvard Milk Day was a single-day event 
12in which men, women, and children gathered to honor local 
13dairy farms on June 18, 1942; the festival received the 
14official title of Milk Day in 1943, when it featured a showing 
15of dairy cattle; the first Milk Days Queen was crowned in 1945, 
16the first large-scale parade was held in 1946, and events such 
17as the 1949 "whitewash" of Ayer Street, which caused it to 
18resemble the Milky Way, continued to flourish throughout the 
19years; and
 
20    WHEREAS, Harvard Milk Days eventually grew into a 
21three-day celebration that was held in a variety of locations 
22before making its permanent home at Milky Way Park in 2022,

2festival grounds, including the addition of a barn hosting 
3dairy cows and calves, the creation of paths, and the 
4establishment of a water supply for multiple areas; and
 
5    WHEREAS, Harvard Milk Days celebrates its 85th anniversary 
6as the United States marks 250 years, with this summer's 
7festival taking place June 5-7, 2026 and a variety of events 
8being held in May and June, including the crowning of the Milk 
9Days Queen on May 9th and the Milk Days breakfast on June 6th; 
10and
 
11    WHEREAS, Harvard Milk Days, which draws thousands of 
12participants each year, is the oldest and longest-running 
13hometown celebration in the State of Illinois; therefore, be 
14it
 
15    RESOLVED, BY THE SENATE OF THE ONE HUNDRED FOURTH GENERAL 
16ASSEMBLY OF THE STATE OF ILLINOIS, that we congratulate the 
17City of Harvard on the 85th anniversary of its annual Harvard 
18Milk Days festival, and we extend our best wishes for many more 
19years of successful celebrations; and be it further
 
20    RESOLVED, That a suitable copy of this resolution be 
21presented to the Mayor of the City of Harvard as an expression 
22of our esteem and respect.
