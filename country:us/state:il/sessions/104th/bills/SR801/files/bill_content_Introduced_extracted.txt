and 5    WHEREAS, Mayor Walik was born to Joseph and Patricia Walik 6on February 24, 1966; and 7    WHEREAS, Mayor Walik held a number of roles over the 8course of his life, working as a newspaper boy, a grocer, a 9delivery driver, a landscaper, a snow plower, a laundromat 10owner, a police officer, a village trustee, and a village 11mayor; no matter the title, he brought the same dedication, 12grit, and personality into every position he undertook; and 13    WHEREAS, Mayor Walik served as Mayor of the Village of 14Stickney since 2017, dedicating himself to caring for the 15community, especially seniors, veterans, residents, and 16students, while always striving to make others feel welcomed, 17valued, and supported; and 18    WHEREAS, Mayor Walik lived a life marked by service, 19humor, hard work, and a deep love for the people around him; he 20will be remembered for his contagious smile, his 21larger-than-life personality, and his sense of humor that   SR0801- 2 -LRB104 21895 ECR 37778 r1could make anyone laugh; and 2    WHEREAS, Mayor Walik was preceded in death by his parents; 3and 4    WHEREAS, Mayor Walik is survived by his fiancee, Debra; 5his children, Samantha (Alistair), Ashlee, Robert, Nicole, and 6Sami; three grandchildren; his sister, Lisa; many nieces, 7nephews, and extended family members; his friends; and 8countless community members; therefore, be it 9    RESOLVED, BY THE SENATE OF THE ONE HUNDRED FOURTH GENERAL 10ASSEMBLY OF THE STATE OF ILLINOIS, that we mourn the passing of 11Mayor Jeffrey Joseph "Jeff" Walik of the Village of Stickney 12and extend our sincere condolences to his family, friends, and 13all who knew and loved him; and be it further 14    RESOLVED, That a suitable copy of this resolution be 15presented to the family of Mayor Walik as an expression of our 16deepest sympathy.
