SENATE RESOLUTION

 
2    WHEREAS, The members of the Illinois Senate are saddened 
3to learn of the death of Raymond Smith Jr., who passed away on 
4March 2, 2026; and
 
5    WHEREAS, Raymond Smith was born to John Raymond and Ruth 
6Jimola Smith in Etta, Mississippi on February 18, 1935; he 
7graduated from Liberty Hill School in 1958, and he later 
8earned several trade certifications; he married Mattie Mae 
9Brown on June 6, 1959, and they had six children, later raising 
10four additional children and fostering more than 25 children; 
11and
 
12    WHEREAS, Raymond Smith, after moving to Chicago, Illinois 
13in 1960, worked as a maintenance worker at Photo Press in 
14Broadview, where he was later promoted to a binary press 
15controller; he then worked at International Paper, retiring in 
16June 2000; and
 
17    WHEREAS, Raymond Smith was a member of the Greater 
18Bethlehem Healing Temple, where he served on the usher board, 
19was ordained as a deacon, and was appointed to the board of 
20trustees; he later became a member of House of Prayer 
21Apostolic Church, where he continued to serve as an active 
22deacon; and
