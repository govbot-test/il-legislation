SENATE RESOLUTION

 
2    WHEREAS, The members of the Illinois Senate are saddened 
3to learn of the death of Kathryn "Kathy" Hickey-Heimlich, who 
4passed away on May 5, 2026; and
 
5    WHEREAS, Kathy Hickey-Heimlich was born to Robert "Bob" 
6Hickey and Loretta Hickey on June 22, 1955; she graduated from 
7Springfield High School in 1973 and earned her bachelor's 
8degree in special education from Illinois State University in 
91977; she married Philip "Phil" Heimlich in 1979; and
 
10    WHEREAS, Kathy Hickey-Heimlich served as a special 
11education and English as a second language instructor in 
12District 186, stepping away from teaching for 14 years to 
13raise her three children; and
 
14    WHEREAS, Kathy Hickey-Heimlich, who had a passion for 
15serving individuals with disabilities and helping animals in 
16need, earned her master's degree in vocational rehabilitation 
17counseling from the University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign in 
182000; she started an animal-assisted therapy program that 
19inspired her graduate research on the benefits of 
20animal-assisted therapy for children with severe disabilities, 
21and her work was later published and received the Graduate 
22Literary Award; and
