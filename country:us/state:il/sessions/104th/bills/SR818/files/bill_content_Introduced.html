SENATE RESOLUTION

 
2    WHEREAS, The members of the Illinois Senate wish to 
3congratulate the Retired State Employees Association (RSEA) on 
4the occasion of its 50th anniversary of advocating for and 
5protecting the rights and benefits of state retirees, their 
6spouses, and survivors; and
 
7    WHEREAS, RSEA is a not-for-profit corporation under the 
8General Not for Profit Corporation Act of the State of 
9Illinois, whose Articles of Incorporation were filed with the 
10Illinois Secretary of State on September 27, 1976, to support 
11and protect the pension, other retirement benefits and the 
12general welfare of our state retirees, their spouses, and 
13survivors; and
 
14    WHEREAS, RSEA members are retired state employees who have 
15given years of dedicated service to the State of Illinois; and
 
16    WHEREAS, RSEA membership has grown to over 9,000 strong 
17with retirees from the State Employees Retirement System 
18(SERS), the Judges Retirement System (JRS), and the General 
19Assembly Retirement System (GARS); and
 
20    WHEREAS, RSEA is governed by a volunteer board, which 
21includes a president, 1st and 2nd vice presidents, treasurer,
