SENATE RESOLUTION

 
2    WHEREAS, The members of the Illinois Senate are saddened 
3to learn of the death of Robert Bruce "Bob" Lyons, who passed 
4away on May 7, 2026; and
 
5    WHEREAS, Bob Lyons was born to Patrick B. Lyons and Margie 
6Franklin Lyons in Oak Park on September 18, 1938; he graduated 
7from J. Sterling Morton High School, and he earned his 
8Bachelor of Science in History and a master's degree from 
9Northern Illinois University; he served in the United States 
10Air Force in the late 1950s and early 1960s, being called to 
11active duty during the Cuban Missile Crisis; he married Nancy 
12Kaminski, and they bought their first home in Hoffman Estates, 
13where they lived for 54 years; six years ago, they moved to 
14Friendship Village, now known as Encore Village, where he 
15shared his knowledge of politics as a regular contributor to 
16the weekly Current Events program; and
 
17    WHEREAS, Bob Lyons was a high school teacher at 
18Barrington, Conant, and Hoffman Estates high schools for 32 
19years, and he chaired the Social Science Department for 22 
20years; during his 30-year career at Hoffman Estates High 
21School, he coached girls and boys cross country; he was a 
22member of Local 1211 of the Northwestern Suburban Teachers 
23Union; in retirement, he served on the Illinois Teachers
