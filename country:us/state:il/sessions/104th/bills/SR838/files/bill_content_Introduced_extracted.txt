and 4    WHEREAS, Joane Watkins was born in Chicago to Parks and 5Louise (Thompson) Watkins on August 15, 1935; she graduated in 61953 from John Marshall High School and attended Roosevelt 7University while working as a caseworker for the Department of 8Human Services in the Austin, Garfield, and Lawndale 9communities; and 10    WHEREAS, Joane Watkins was the matriarch of the 11Thompson-Watkins Family Tree and a descendant of the freed 12slave, Wade Watkins from Halls, Tennessee; and 13    WHEREAS, Joane Watkins enjoyed volunteering, family 14events, music, dancing, and traveling; and 15    WHEREAS, Joane Watkins received a birthday resolution from 16President Obama for her 80th Birthday; she received the 2025 17President's Lifetime Achievement Award and a congratulatory 18award letter from President Biden for serving over 50,000 19volunteer hours in the Austin community as a board member of, 20Diondai's Place Breast Cancer Support; she enjoyed mentoring 21and participating in events at the Austin Branch Library and   SR0838- 2 -LRB104 13489 MST 25935 r1the Goodman Theatre, as well as Lupus walks, the Juneteenth 2Peace Walk, and many others; and 3    WHEREAS, Joane Watkins was preceded in death by her 4parents; her children, Louis Michael and Barbara; and her 5siblings, Lawrence and Lorraine Watkins, Lillian Broussard, 6Laura Harden, Lenora Godwin, Lois Davis, Barbara Harden, and 7Nancy Bains; and 8    WHEREAS, Joane Watkins is survived by her children, Sheila 9(Ronald) Smith, Sharon Cobbs, Diondai (Edwin Jr.) 10Brown-Whitfield, Joseph (Karen) Veal, Judith Stroger, and G. 11Sean Crittleton; 17 grandchildren; 30 great-grandchildren; 12 12great-great-grandchildren; and numerous nieces, nephews, 13cousins, extended family, and friends; therefore, be it 14    RESOLVED, BY THE SENATE OF THE ONE HUNDRED FOURTH GENERAL 15ASSEMBLY OF THE STATE OF ILLINOIS, that we mourn the passing of 16Joane Margaret Watkins and extend our sincere condolences to 17her family, friends, and all who knew and loved her; and be it 18further 19    RESOLVED, That a suitable copy of this resolution be 20presented to the family of Joane Watkins as an expression of 21our deepest sympathy.
