Full Text of SR0844 Illinois General Assembly ILGA.GOV SR0844 - 104th General Assembly SR0844LRB104 15661 MST 28833 r1SENATE RESOLUTION 2    WHEREAS, The members of the Illinois Senate are saddened 3to learn of the death of Billie Jean Lucas, who passed away on 4September 22, 2025; and 5    WHEREAS, Billie Jean Lucas was born in Florence, 6Mississippi to Mitchell and Mattie Hopson on March 17, 1936; 7and 8    WHEREAS, Billie Jean Lucas spent 23 years at the Sara Lee 9Bakery Corporation; later, she worked at the Illinois State 10Tollway Authority before retiring in 1991; and 11    WHEREAS, Billie Jean Lucas's faith was the foundation of 12her life; she was a member of Mt. Zion First Holy Miracle 13Baptist Church for 30 years, where she served in the gospel 14choir for 16 years and was Mother's Board chairperson, 15secretary, and treasurer for seven years; and 16    WHEREAS, Billie Jean Lucas enjoyed cooking, gardening, 17backyard grilling, and partying with her friends and family; 18she had a great sense of humor and uplifted everyone around her 19with her wit; her kindness and compassion inspired those 20around her, and her generosity and consideration for others 21were hallmarks of her character; and   SR0844- 2 -LRB104 15661 MST 28833 r1    WHEREAS, Billie Jean Lucas was preceded in death by her 2parents; her husband, Frank; her children, Kenneth 3Hopson-Lucas, Daryl Lucas, and Faye Lucas; three sisters; and 4six brothers; and 5    WHEREAS, Billie Jean Lucas is survived by her daughters, 6Carolyn Todd and Sherry Lucas-Tate; her Godson, Edgar 7(Carolyn) Seale; her granddaughters, Melloney (Henry) Owens, 8Kamilah (Jeremy) Todd, LaTashia Hayes, Rokenna (Cordney) 9Holmes, and Kenya (Antoine) Patterson; 10 great-grandchildren; 10five great-great-grandchildren; numerous nieces, nephews, 11cousins, and friends; and her special friends, Mother Callie 12Shears, Sister Jennie Howell, Anthony James, and Larry Hughes; 13therefore, be it 14    RESOLVED, BY THE SENATE OF THE ONE HUNDRED FOURTH GENERAL 15ASSEMBLY OF THE STATE OF ILLINOIS, that we mourn the passing of 16Billie Jean Lucas and extend our sincere condolences to her 17family, friends, and all who knew and loved her; and be it 18further 19    RESOLVED, That a suitable copy of this resolution be 20presented to the family of Billie Jean Lucas as an expression 21of our deepest sympathy.
