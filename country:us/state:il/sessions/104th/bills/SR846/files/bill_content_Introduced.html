SENATE RESOLUTION

 
2    WHEREAS, The members of the Illinois Senate are saddened 
3to learn of the death of Chalmus English Sr.; and
 
4    WHEREAS, Chalmus English, the son of Charles English and 
5Willie Mae Green English, was born in Birmingham, Alabama on 
6March 15, 1947; he graduated from Ullman High School in 1964; 
7he received a degree in commerce and social studies from 
8LaSalle Extension University; he married Wilma Marie Wilson on 
9January 24, 1968; and
 
10    WHEREAS, Chalmus English began working as a postal worker 
11in 1964; he later served as an area supervisor at Corn Products 
12for 15 years and as a plant manager for Heller Seasoning for 
13seven years; he fulfilled his longtime dream by establishing 
14See-English Accounting & Tax Services in 1994, becoming a 
15respected business owner and trusted advisor to countless 
16families and organizations; and
 
17    WHEREAS, Chalmus English was an active member of First 
18Baptist Church of Chicago, becoming affiliated in 1969 and 
19then a member in 1970 before being baptized in 1977; he served 
20in numerous leadership roles within the church, including as a 
21Sunday school teacher, a deacon, a youth coordinator, director 
22of the Community Youth Program, chairperson of the Board of
