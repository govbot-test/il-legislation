he fulfilled his longtime dream by establishing 14See-English Accounting & Tax Services in 1994, becoming a 15respected business owner and trusted advisor to countless 16families and organizations; and 17    WHEREAS, Chalmus English was an active member of First 18Baptist Church of Chicago, becoming affiliated in 1969 and 19then a member in 1970 before being baptized in 1977; he served 20in numerous leadership roles within the church, including as a 21Sunday school teacher, a deacon, a youth coordinator, director 22of the Community Youth Program, chairperson of the Board of   SR0846- 2 -LRB104 20405 ECR 33867 r1Christian Education, a member of the usher board and the board 2of trustees, and a FBC moderator; and 3    WHEREAS, Chalmus English remained committed to serving the 4youth, earning the Sheriff's Youth Enrichment Award in 1987; 5and 6    WHEREAS, Chalmus English, affectionately referred to as 7"Paw-Paw" by his grandchildren, was known for his integrity, 8his humor, and his deep faith; he will be remembered for his 9legacy of wisdom, compassion, and steadfast love and for the 10countless lives he shaped, supported, and uplifted; and 11    WHEREAS, Chalmus English was preceded in death by his 12parents and his siblings, Ernest English, Houston English, 13Odessa English, and Barbara Lewis;
