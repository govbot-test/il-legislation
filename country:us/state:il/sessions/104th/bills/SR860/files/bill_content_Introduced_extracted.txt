and 5    WHEREAS, Spencer Leak began working for the historic, 6family-owned business, Leak and Sons Funeral Homes, as a young 7boy, dedicating his life to the family trade; he earned a 8Bachelor of Science in Mortuary Science and Funeral Service 9from Southern Illinois University Carbondale, and he became a 10licensed funeral director in 1989; and 11    WHEREAS, Spencer Leak served as vice president of Leak and 12Sons Funeral Homes, which has served the Chicagoland community 13with dignity and compassion for more than 90 years; as a 14third-generation owner, he modernized the business's 15technology and operations while personally overseeing 16thousands of funerals each year; and 17    WHEREAS, As a generous and community-minded leader, 18Spencer Leak gave back in countless ways; he was an active 19member of 100 Black Men of America, where he mentored young 20African American males, and he served on the boards of 21Operation PUSH and the Better Boys Foundation, which provides 22tutoring and support for youth in need; and   SR0860- 2 -LRB104 22294 ECR 38713 r1    WHEREAS, Spencer Leak was a proud member of Kappa Alpha 2Psi Fraternity, Inc., Liberty Baptist Church, and the Illinois 3Funeral Directors Association; he was recognized in Who's Who 4of Black Chicago and received the Men of Excellence Award from 5the Chicago Defender; and 6    WHEREAS, Above all, Spencer Leak was a devoted family man 7who was the loving husband of Dr. Donna Leak and the proud 8father of Spencer Leak III and Emma Sophia Leak; and 9    WHEREAS, Spencer Leak's passing leaves a profound void in 10the Chicagoland community, the funeral service profession, and 11the many lives he touched through his work, leadership, and 12quiet generosity; therefore, be it 13    RESOLVED, BY THE SENATE OF THE ONE HUNDRED FOURTH GENERAL 14ASSEMBLY OF THE STATE OF ILLINOIS, that we mourn the passing of 15Spencer Leak Jr. and extend our sincere condolences to his 16family, friends, and all who knew and loved him; and be it 17further 18    RESOLVED, That a suitable copy of this resolution be 19presented to the family of Spencer Leak as an expression of our 20deepest sympathy.
